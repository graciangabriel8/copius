Basmati rice

Riz basmati

Latin nameOryza sativa
FamilyGrains & flours
OriginHimalayan foothills
SeasonAll year
FlavourFloral · Nutty · Delicate · Mild
Typical price3–7 €/kg

What it is

The name means fragrant in Hindi, and the aroma comes from a compound also found in pandan leaf and fresh bread. Unusually for rice, it is aged a year or more before sale — the drying intensifies the perfume and makes the grains elongate further.

In the kitchen

Rinse until the water runs clear, then soak thirty minutes. The grains double in length rather than swelling fat.
