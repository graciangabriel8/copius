Blonde morel

Morille blonde

FamilyMushrooms
Season (France)April – June
Typical price (France)150–300 €/kg

What it is

The name comes from Old High German morhila, a little carrot, for the pitted cone that pushes up through leaf litter in spring. Chinese growers now raise several black morel species under plastic; the blonde is still lifted from the ground, one at a time.

In the kitchen

Never eat them raw or barely cooked - a morel wants ten good minutes in the pan. Halve them lengthwise first: the blonde is hollow, and grit settles at the base of the stem.
