Toasted sesame oil

Huile de sésame grillé

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)18–35 €/L

What it is

Two entirely different products share the name: the pale oil from raw seed, used for frying, and the dark oil from toasted seed, which is a seasoning and not a cooking fat at all. Buying the wrong one ruins the dish either way.

In the kitchen

Add it at the very end, by the half-teaspoon. Heated, its whole aroma burns off within seconds.
