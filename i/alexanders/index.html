Alexanders ✦

Maceron

Latin nameSmyrnium olusatrum
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean coasts, naturalised in Britain
SeasonMarch – May
Flavourbitter · herbal · resinous
Typical price5–12 €/kg

What it is

Rome carried it north as a pot-herb and until the seventeenth century it was one of the commonest plants in a British kitchen garden, before self-blanching celery — sweeter, milder, less work — pushed it out entirely. Its genus name comes from smyrna, myrrh: the resin is in the smell, and celery has none of it.

In the kitchen

Peel the stems like rhubarb before cooking — most of the bitterness sits in the skin — then braise ten minutes in butter and stock. The unopened flower buds are the best of the plant: sixty seconds in boiling water, dressed warm.
