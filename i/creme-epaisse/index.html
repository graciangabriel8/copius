Thick double cream

Crème épaisse

Latin nameCrème épaisse, 30–40% MG
FamilyDairy
OriginNormandy, France
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · creamy · milky · sweet
Typical price5–9 €/L

What it is

French dairy counters split cream two ways and the difference matters more than shoppers think: liquide whips and pours, épaisse does neither well but enriches without thinning. Confusing the two is the most common reason a home sauce goes wrong.

In the kitchen

It will not whip — the fat is already set. For chantilly you need crème liquide entière at 30% or above, and very cold.
