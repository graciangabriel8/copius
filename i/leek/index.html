Leek

Poireau

Latin nameAllium ampeloprasum
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean basin
Season (France)October – March
FlavourMild · Sweet · Green
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

Emperor Nero ate leeks in oil for days each month, convinced they cleared his singing voice — Romans mocked him as the “leek-eater”. Wales later made it a national emblem, worn on helmets into battle.

In the kitchen

The white cooks to silk, the green perfumes stocks. Slit it lengthwise and rinse well — sand hides between its layers.
