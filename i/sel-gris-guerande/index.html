Grey sea salt (sel gris)

Sel gris de Guérande

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)June – September
Typical price (France)2–5 €/kg

What it is

The paludiers move seawater by gravity alone through clay basins laid out in the Middle Ages, and let sun and wind take it to saturation. Grey salt is what settles on the clay floor and is raked up damp; fleur de sel is only the thin crust that forms on the surface on a dry, windy afternoon.

In the kitchen

It stays damp and will not pass through a grinder — dissolve it for brines and pasta water, or crush it under a blade. Ten grams per litre of cooking water is the working dose and needs nothing added.
