Sarriette

Latin nameSatureja hortensis
FamilyHerbs
OriginMediterranean
SeasonJune – September
Flavourpeppery · herbal · warm · resinous
Typical price2–4 €/botte

What it is

Germany calls it Bohnenkraut, the bean herb, because it does two things at once: it tastes right with beans and it genuinely helps digest them. Provence puts it on goat's cheese, where it is called pèbre d'ail, garlic pepper.

In the kitchen

Put a sprig in the water with any dried bean or lentil. It is the oldest trick in European bean cookery and it works.
