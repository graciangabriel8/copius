Olympia oyster

Huître olympia

FamilyShellfish & molluscs
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)25–45 €/douzaine

What it is

It is the only oyster native to the Pacific coast of North America, and Gold Rush San Francisco ate it down to almost nothing inside thirty years. Unlike the Pacific oyster it broods its larvae inside the shell instead of casting them into the water, and it takes four or five years to reach the size of a coin.

In the kitchen

Serve them on rock salt rather than crushed ice: each holds barely a teaspoon of liquor, and meltwater dilutes it to nothing. Count ten to a person, eat them naked, and let the copper-and-celery finish arrive on its own.
