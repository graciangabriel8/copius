Boiling fowl

Poule (à bouillir)

Latin nameGallus gallus domesticus
FamilyMeat
OriginFarmyards, worldwide
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · umami · meaty · mild
Typical price6–10 €/kg

What it is

A boiling fowl is a hen retired from laying, eighteen months old or more, and the connective tissue that makes her too tough to roast is exactly the collagen that sets a broth to jelly. The line about a chicken in every peasant's pot on Sundays was put into Henri IV's mouth by a biographer fifty years after the king died; whatever he actually said, the bird meant was a spent layer like this one, never a young roaster.

In the kitchen

Three hours at a bare shiver, never a boil — a rolling pot emulsifies the fat into the liquid and clouds the stock past saving. The meat is spent by then and belongs in a salad or a hachis; the broth was the object.
