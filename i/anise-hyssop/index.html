Anise hyssop ✦

Agastache (hysope anisée)

Latin nameAgastache foeniculum
FamilyHerbs
OriginNorthern plains of North America
SeasonJuly – September
FlavourAnise-like · Floral · Sweet
Typical price3–6 €/botte

What it is

It is neither anise nor hyssop but a North American mint whose leaves happen to carry methyl chavicol, the same molecule that scents tarragon. Plains nations brewed it and used it to sweeten other infusions, so settlers first met the plant as a sugar rather than as a herb.

In the kitchen

Heat destroys the aroma — infuse the leaves off the boil, covered, twenty minutes in cream or syrup, then strain. Keep the flowers raw and scatter them at the end; they hold what the leaves lose in a pan.
