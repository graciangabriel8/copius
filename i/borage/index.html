Borage

Bourrache

Latin nameBorago officinalis
FamilyHerbs
OriginMediterranean
SeasonMay – August
Flavourfresh · marine · grassy · delicate
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

The leaf tastes startlingly of cucumber and oyster, which is why Ligurians stuff pansotti with it and the English drop the blue star flowers into summer drinks. Roman soldiers took it before battle, believing it gave courage.

In the kitchen

The leaves are covered in prickly hairs that soften only with cooking or fine chopping. Use the flowers raw and the leaves cooked.
