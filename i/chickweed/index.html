Chickweed ✦

Mouron des oiseaux

Latin nameStellaria media
FamilyVegetables
OriginTemperate worldwide
Season (France)October – April
FlavourFresh · Green · Mild
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Chickweed tastes startlingly of raw sweetcorn, and it can be told from everything it grows beside by a single line of fine hairs running along one side of the stem, switching sides at each pair of leaves. It is a cool-season plant: it germinates in autumn, mats through winter and is finished by the first real heat.

In the kitchen

Shear the mat with scissors rather than pulling it: pull and the roots come with the plant, and the grit comes with the roots. Serve it raw and dressed at the last second — heated, it collapses to almost nothing.
