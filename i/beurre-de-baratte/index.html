Churned butter

Beurre de baratte

Latin nameBeurre de baratte AOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginNormandy & Poitou-Charentes
Season (France)All year
FlavourCreamy · Rich · Nutty · Milky
Typical price (France)12–20 €/kg

What it is

Made in a slow rotating churn from cream that has been left to ripen with lactic cultures for up to twenty hours before churning. That maturation is where the hazelnut note comes from — industrial butter skips it entirely.

In the kitchen

Keep it for spreading and for finishing sauces, where you can actually taste it. Frying with it is a waste of money.
