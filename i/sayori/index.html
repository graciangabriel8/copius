Halfbeak (sayori) ✦

Demi-bec du Japon (sayori)

Latin nameHyporhamphus sajori
FamilyFish
OriginCoastal Japan, spring shoals
SeasonFebruary – May
Flavourdelicate · sweet · fresh · marine
Typical price60–120 €/kg

What it is

Slit the belly and the cavity is lined with a black membrane, sometimes given as the source of haraguro — Japanese for a scheming person, literally black-bellied. The etymology is argued over, but the fish really is two-faced: glass and silver outside, soot within.

In the kitchen

Scrape the black lining out with the back of a knife under running water until the cavity is white, or the whole fish tastes bitter. The fillet is too thin to slice across — cut it into long ribbons and tie each one in a loose knot.

Goes with

Wasabi Shiso Ponzu Sudachi Myoga Negi Koikuchi shoyu Gari (pickled ginger)
