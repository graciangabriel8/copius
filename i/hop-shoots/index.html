Hop shoots ◆

Jets de houblon

Latin nameHumulus lupulus
FamilyVegetables
OriginFlemish and Alsatian hop yards
SeasonMarch – April
FlavourNutty · Bitter · Earthy
Typical price200–800 €/kg

What it is

They are dug white from under the soil by hand, kneeling in cold ground, and a whole hop plant yields about twenty-five grams; at Belgian spring auctions the kilo has passed a thousand euros. The season runs three weeks and nothing about it mechanises, which is the entire explanation for the price.

In the kitchen

They are already tender: ninety seconds in salted water, no more, then straight into butter. The classic Belgian service of poached egg and cream is not decoration — the bitterness needs fat opposite it.
