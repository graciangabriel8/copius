Fermented shrimp paste (belacan)

Pâte de crevette fermentée (belacan)

Latin nameAcetes spp.
FamilyCondiments
OriginCoastal Southeast Asia
SeasonAll year
Flavourumami · salty · pungent · briny
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

Belacan is made from Acetes, a shrimp barely a centimetre long, salted, left to break down in the sun and pressed into blocks that ferment for weeks — the smell is proteins being taken apart. A good block cuts like a firm cheese; one that crumbles was dried too fast and will give only salt.

In the kitchen

Toast it before it goes anywhere near a dish: a thin slice, two minutes a side in a dry pan, until the kitchen is unbearable and it snaps rather than bends. A pea-sized piece seasons a curry paste for four; untoasted and undosed it flattens everything else.

Goes with

Chili pepper Garlic Shallot Lime Lemongrass Tamarind paste
