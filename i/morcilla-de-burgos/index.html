Morcilla de Burgos

morcilla de Burgos

Latin nameSus scrofa (blood sausage)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginBurgos, Castile
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Earthy · Sweet · Meaty
Typical price (France)10–18 €/kg

What it is

It became an IGP in 2018, after a twenty-five-year fight over who could use the name, and rests on two things nothing substitutes: horcal onion, a flat sweet Castilian variety, and rice — at least 30 % onion, between 10 and 40 % rice. The grain is why a slice collapses into savoury crumbs in the pan instead of holding together like a French boudin.

In the kitchen

Fry thick slices in a dry pan, two minutes a side without touching them, then break them up; moved too early it turns to paste. Or crumble it in raw with rice or beans and let it dissolve.
