Glass eel ◆

Civelle (pibale)

Latin nameAnguilla anguilla
FamilyFish
OriginGironde & Adour estuaries
SeasonDecember – March
Flavourdelicate · mild · marine
Typical price400–1000 €/kg

What it is

They reach the Gironde and the Adour after two or three years drifting from the Sargasso, transparent and about six centimetres long, and the fishery now runs on quota with every kilo traced because the species is critically endangered. Prices climbed high enough that Spain answered with gulas - surimi threads with two ink dots for eyes - now more common on Spanish tables than the fish.

In the kitchen

Seconds, not minutes: garlic and chilli warmed in olive oil, the civelles tipped in, a count of twenty, straight to the table in the earthenware they cooked in. They carry almost no flavour of their own, so the oil and the garlic are the dish.
