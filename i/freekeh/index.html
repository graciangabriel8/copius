Freekeh

Freekeh

FamilyGrains & flours
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)7–14 €/kg

What it is

Wheat harvested green and set on fire — the high moisture in the young grain stops it burning while the chaff and husk char away. A thirteenth-century Baghdad cookbook already records it, and the smoke is the entire flavour.

In the kitchen

Rinse it well and pick it over — charred fragments and small stones are common even in good packets.
