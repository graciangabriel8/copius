Sunflower oil

Huile de tournesol

Latin nameHelianthus annuus
FamilyFats & oils
OriginRussia & Ukraine
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Nutty · Delicate · Toasty
Typical price (France)2–5 €/L

What it is

The Russian Orthodox Church banned most fats during Lent but had never heard of the sunflower, so it escaped the list — and by the nineteenth century Russia was pressing it by the shipload. A loophole created an industry.

In the kitchen

Its neutrality and high smoke point make it the default frying oil. Do not use it where you want the fat to taste of something.
