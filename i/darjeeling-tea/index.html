Darjeeling tea

Thé de Darjeeling

Latin nameCamellia sinensis var. sinensis
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginDarjeeling hills, West Bengal
Season (France)March – June
FlavourFruity · Floral · Delicate
Typical price (France)80–200 €/kg

What it is

The gardens sit between roughly 600 and 2,000 m and are planted with Chinese bush rather than assamica, which is why even the black grades are only partly oxidised and stay green-tinged in the leaf. It was the first Indian product granted a geographical indication, in 2004 — the annual crop is a fraction of what is sold under the name.

In the kitchen

Second flush is the muscatel one and the only grade worth putting in a poaching syrup for apricots or white peaches: 10 g per litre at 90 °C for four minutes, then sugar. First flush is greener and dies under heat — drink it, do not cook with it.
