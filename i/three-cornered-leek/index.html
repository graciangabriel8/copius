Three-cornered leek ✦

Ail à trois angles

Latin nameAllium triquetrum
FamilyHerbs
OriginMediterranean basin, naturalised west
SeasonFebruary – May
Flavourpungent · fresh · mild
Typical price10–20 €/kg

What it is

Roll a stem between your fingers and you feel three flat faces and three sharp angles — the shape names the plant and tells it apart by touch from every other wild allium. It escaped Mediterranean gardens so thoroughly that Britain now lists it among the plants it is an offence to spread into the wild.

In the kitchen

Treat it as a vegetable, not a herb: griddle the whole stems like thin leeks, two minutes a side, and scatter the raw bells over at the end. The white flowers are as edible as the stem and considerably stronger.
