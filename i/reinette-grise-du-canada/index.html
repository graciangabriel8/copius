Reinette grise du Canada apple

Pomme reinette grise du Canada

Latin nameMalus domestica
FamilyFruits
OriginFrench orchards, in spite of the name
Season (France)October – February
FlavourSour · Nutty · Fruity
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

Described in France in 1771 and grown around Paris from the early nineteenth century, the variety has no documented tie to Canada whatever — the name appears to have been borrowed to make a plain russet sound like an import. Its dry, granular flesh is the whole point: where a juicier apple collapses into sauce, this one concentrates and keeps its walls.

In the kitchen

Raw it is dull and leathery; give it heat. For a tart, slice it thin and lay it straight on the pastry without pre-cooking — it holds too little water to need it, and a knob of butter with a spoonful of sugar per apple is the whole dressing.
