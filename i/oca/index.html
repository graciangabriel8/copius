Oca ✦

Oca du Pérou

Latin nameOxalis tuberosa
FamilyVegetables
OriginAndean highlands
Season (France)November – January
FlavourSour · Sweet · Earthy
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

Oca is the second most cultivated tuber of the Andes after the potato, and reached New Zealand around 1860, where it is still sold as yam although it is no relation. Its oxalic tartness sits in the skin, and a week on a sunny windowsill breaks that acid down until the tuber turns frankly sweet.

In the kitchen

Do not peel and do not boil: roast whole at 200 °C for 25 minutes so the skin blisters and the inside stays lemony. Water leaches out the acid and the flavour together.
