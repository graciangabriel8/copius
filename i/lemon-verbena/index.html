Lemon verbena

Verveine citronnelle

Latin nameAloysia citrodora
FamilyHerbs
OriginAndes of Chile & Peru
Season (France)June – September
FlavourCitrusy · Floral · Fresh · Delicate
Typical price (France)3–6 €/pot

What it is

Brought from the Andes in the 18th century and named for a Spanish queen, it settled into French life as the grandmother’s tisane of Provence. Pastry chefs rediscovered what the grandmothers knew: no other leaf smells this purely of lemon without a drop of acidity.

In the kitchen

Infuse it in warm cream or syrup off the heat, covered, ten minutes — boiling murders its perfume. Transcendent with peaches and apricots.
