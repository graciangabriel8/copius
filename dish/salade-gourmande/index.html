The dish that changed something · 1970s

Salade gourmande

Michel Guérard · 1933–2024 · Eugénie-les-Bains, Landes

Foie gras and green beans dressed together and plated as a salad. A first course that is neither cooked to order nor sauced, which until then was not what a luxury restaurant served.

Why it works

Foie gras is fat with no acid and no crunch. The beans bring both, and the vinaigrette does cold what a sauce would have done hot.

What is in it
