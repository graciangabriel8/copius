Dried duck breast

magret de canard séché

Latin nameAnas platyrhynchos × Cairina moschata (breast, cured)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginGascony, South-West France
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Salty · Meaty · Peppery
Typical price (France)30–45 €/kg

What it is

In the South-West magret means one thing only: the breast of a duck raised for foie gras, so its fat cap is thick and soft-melting rather than firm. Salting it for a day and hanging it two to three weeks is a farm-scale habit — the breast is the single piece of the bird that goes neither into confit nor into the terrine.

In the kitchen

Cut on a slant so every slice carries both fat and lean; straight down you get a band of pure fat. Keep it wrapped in a cloth and let slices sit five minutes — cold, the fat stays waxy and mute.
