Meigan cai

Meigan cai (moutarde fermentée séchée)

Latin nameBrassica juncea
FamilyVegetables
OriginZhejiang and Guangdong, China
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · smoky · earthy · umami
Typical price10–20 €/kg

What it is

Mustard greens are salted, left to ferment, then sun-dried, steamed and dried again until they are almost black and hard as straw. In mei cai kou rou the dried greens sit under the pork belly in the steamer for hours, taking the rendering fat and giving back a sweetness the meat cannot make on its own.

In the kitchen

Rinse it several times in a bowl of water — field grit settles at the bottom and never leaves if you rinse under a tap. Soak 20 minutes, squeeze dry, then fry it briefly in oil before it joins anything wet, or it stays papery.

Goes with

Pork belly Dark soy sauce Shaoxing wine Chinese rock sugar Star anise Ginger Garlic
