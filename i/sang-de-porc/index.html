Pig’s blood

Sang de porc

FamilyCuts of meat
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)3–7 €/L

What it is

Blood clots within minutes of being drawn, so it is whisked as it falls and the fibrin strands lifted out — what the charcutier buys is already defibrinated, held with a little salt or vinegar. Gascony sets it alone in a pan as sanquette, Burgundy stirs it into a civet at the last second, and in boudin it is simply the binder.

In the kitchen

Never let it boil. Slacken it first with a ladle of the hot sauce, stir it back off the heat, and keep the pan under 80 °C — above that the proteins seize and the sauce turns to grit.
