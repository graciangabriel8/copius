Fennel pollen ◆

Pollen de fenouil

Latin nameFoeniculum vulgare
FamilySpices
OriginTuscany and Calabria
SeasonAll year
Flavouranise · honeyed · floral
Typical price700–1500 €/kg

What it is

Pollen is taken by cutting the umbels as the flowers open, drying them head-down and shaking the dust loose — a day's work yields grams, which is why it is priced like saffron. Tuscan producers call it the spice of angels; it is the same plant as fennel seed, caught three weeks earlier in its cycle.

In the kitchen

It is a finishing spice — heat flattens it, so dust it on after the pan, over pork, roast potatoes or grilled fish. A pinch per portion: it is far more intense than ground fennel seed and carries none of the seed's bitterness.
