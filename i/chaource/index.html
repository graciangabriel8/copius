Chaource

Chaource

Latin nameChaource AOP
FamilyDairy
OriginChampagne & Burgundy border
SeasonAll year
Flavourcreamy · tangy · milky · mild
Typical price20–30 €/kg

What it is

A tall white cylinder that keeps a chalky, faintly sour core long after the outside has turned to cream — the two textures in one mouthful are the point. Its home sits on the Champagne border, and the local pairing has never needed defending.

In the kitchen

Buy it young if you want the chalk. Fully ripe it is uniformly soft, and the contrast that makes it interesting is gone.

Goes with

Strawberry Cream Walnut Apple Chives Black pepper Grape Butter
