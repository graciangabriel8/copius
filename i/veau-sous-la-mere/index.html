Milk-fed veal

Veau sous la mère

Latin nameBos taurus (veau de lait)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginLimousin and southwest France
Season (France)All year
FlavourMilky · Delicate · Sweet · Mild
Typical price (France)25–50 €/kg

What it is

A calf raised sous la mère suckles two or three times a day and is usually given a nurse cow as well as its dam, because one cow cannot carry it to weight on milk alone. Nothing else is fed — no grain, no forage — and that is what keeps the flesh nearly white: iron from grass would redden it within days.

In the kitchen

Cook it pale — a blanquette, a braise held below a simmer, or a chop taken to just-set and no browner. A hard sear greys and tightens these delicate proteins, and the sweetness is the first thing to go.
