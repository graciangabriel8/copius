# Who already does this

**15 September 2026.** Four scan lanes, two auditors re-opening company sites.
55 entries, 51 audited: 44 confirmed, 4 with the model mis-stated, 1 price wrong,
1 dead, 1 unverifiable. Anything whose site would not resolve was killed rather
than reported.

## The answer in one line

**The idea exists — twice in hospitality, and once at scale in German-speaking
Europe. All three of the curated ones are small, and that is the finding.**

## The three that are your idea

### HospoJobs (by Holybelly) — France

[https://hospo.jobs/](https://hospo.jobs/) · audit: **CONFIRMED**

- **Model.** Hand-picked listings; no paid tier visible
- **Who pays.** unclear
- **Price.** No price stated anywhere on the pages of its own site I could open. Écotable's restaurant blog describes it as 100% free and says Nicolas Alary himself filters which offers go live — that is a third-party account, not hospo.jobs's own words. Read 2026-09-15.
- **Size.** "164 jobs postés depuis le 1er avril 2026" — roughly 35 listings a month, Paris-weighted.
- **Weakness.** This is the plan, already built — but free, Paris-only, ten years old and still at ~35 listings a month, with the curation depending on one restaurateur's personal judgement and reputation. It proves the demand exists and that nobody has found the money in it.

### Countertalk — United Kingdom (London-centred)

[https://countertalk.co.uk/](https://countertalk.co.uk/) · audit: **CONFIRMED**

- **Model.** Curated hospitality job board plus editorial/community. Does not list every job — employers are approved and every ad is manually vetted before publication.
- **Who pays.** employer
- **Price.** Essential £95 + VAT (live 4 weeks); Featured £195 + VAT (4 weeks); Premium £295 + VAT (6 weeks). Read 15 Sep 2026 on their own pricing page, FAQ and Terms — all three agree. Payment is taken via Stripe only after the post is approved.
- **Size.** Founded 2018 by Rav Gill. ~100+ verified employers listed, 17 pages of employer listings as of 15 Sep 2026. Eight years in and still roughly a hundred houses — curation caps supply.
- **Weakness.** Small and geographically concentrated (London and the independent/artisanal scene). A curated board has few ads, and few ads means weak candidate pull — the structural trap of this model. The removal right for "poor or unsafe working culture" is a right, not a mechanism: it fires on review, not on churn data. Nothing measures whether a listed employer actually retains staff.

### Fair Job Hotels e.V. — Germany, Austria, Switzerland

[https://www.fair-job-hotels.de/](https://www.fair-job-hotels.de/) · audit: **CONFIRMED**

- **Model.** Paid employer certification with a job board attached. Hotels apply, are assessed on working conditions, and pay an annual membership. The job board itself is white-labelled from hotelcareer.de rather than built in-house.
- **Who pays.** employer
- **Price.** Annual membership by headcount, read on their own Partnerhotel-werden page 15 Sep 2026: up to 20 employees €1,250/year; 21–50 employees €2,500/year; 51+ employees €3,750/year. Recurring, and independent of how much the hotel hires.
- **Size.** 117 member hotels across DE/AT/CH, roughly 10,500 employees. Members average 4.18 on kununu against an industry average of 3.4. Admission rules were loosened in the last few years (two sponsor hotels no longer required) to admit unclassified houses.
- **Weakness.** It takes money from the establishments it certifies — the same conflict of interest the planned French site will carry. Its credibility rests entirely on being seen to reject applicants. It also has no independent board: the jobs live on hotelcareer.de, so it never owns the candidate relationship. Hotels only, no restaurants.
