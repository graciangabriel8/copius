Burgundy snail

Escargot de Bourgogne

Latin nameHelix pomatia
FamilyShellfish
OriginBurgundy and eastern France
SeasonSeptember – November
Flavourearthy · meaty · mild
Typical price40–70 €/kg

What it is

The arrêté of 24 April 1979 forbids gathering Helix pomatia at any time below a shell diameter of 3 cm, and from 1 April to 30 June at any size at all — the wild French population had been collected almost to nothing. Nearly everything sold now comes from central and eastern Europe, and none of it is farmed: the species has never been bred at scale.

In the kitchen

Simmer them in court-bouillon for two to three hours before they ever meet butter — an hour leaves them rubbery and no quantity of garlic hides it. Fill the shell with butter first, then the snail, then more butter, so it never sits in a pocket of air.
