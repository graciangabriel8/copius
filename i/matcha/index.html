Matcha

Matcha

Latin nameCamellia sinensis
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginUji, Japan
Season (France)All year
FlavourBitter · Green · Umami · Earthy
Typical price (France)80–250 €/kg

What it is

The bushes are shaded for three weeks before harvest, which forces them to make more chlorophyll and more theanine — that is where the colour and the savoury depth come from. The leaves are then stone-ground, a kilo taking about an hour.

In the kitchen

Never use boiling water — 70 to 80°C at most. Boiling water makes it aggressively bitter within seconds.
