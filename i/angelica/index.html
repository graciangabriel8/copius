Angelica

Angélique

Latin nameAngelica archangelica
FamilyHerbs
OriginNorthern Europe
Season (France)May – July
FlavourSweet · Herbal · Anise-like · Musky
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Niort in the Deux-Sèvres has candied its hollow green stems since the eighteenth century, and the town still runs on it. The name comes from a legend that an archangel revealed the plant as a cure during a plague year.

In the kitchen

The candied stem is the usable form for most cooks — dice it into a cake or a fruit compote where you would use citrus peel.
