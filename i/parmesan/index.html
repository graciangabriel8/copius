Parmesan

Latin nameParmigiano Reggiano

OriginEmilia-Romagna, Italy
Season (France)All year
FlavourUmami · Salty · Nutty · Rich
Typical price (France)25–40 €/kg

What it is

Boccaccio, in 1350, dreamed up a paradise with a mountain of grated parmesan where cooks rolled pasta down the slopes. The dream still has a bank branch: Italian banks accept wheels of aging Parmigiano as loan collateral, guarded in climate-controlled vaults.

In the kitchen

Never discard the rind: dropped into a soup or a pot of beans, it melts its umami into the broth like a bouillon cube from heaven.

Its 86 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
