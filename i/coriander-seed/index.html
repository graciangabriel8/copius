Coriander seed

Graine de coriandre

Latin nameCoriandrum sativum
FamilySpices
OriginSouthern Europe & Near East
Season (France)All year
FlavourCitrusy · Warm · Floral · Nutty
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

The seed and the leaf come from one plant and taste nothing alike — the leaf is soapy and green, the seed warm and orange-peel sweet. Seeds were found in Tutankhamun's tomb, and the plant does not grow wild in Egypt, so someone imported them for him.

In the kitchen

Toast and crush it coarsely rather than grinding to powder. The husk is papery and the fragments give little bursts of citrus.
