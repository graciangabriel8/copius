Plantain

Banane plantain

Latin nameMusa × paradisiaca
FamilyFruit
OriginSoutheast Asia & West Africa
SeasonAll year
Flavourmild · sweet · earthy
Typical price2–4 €/kg

What it is

It is the banana that never became a dessert: too starchy and too astringent to eat raw at any stage, it is cooked green as a starch, half-ripe as a vegetable and black-skinned as something close to a sweet. Across the Congo basin it is counted with the grains rather than the fruits — a staple in all but name.

In the kitchen

Score the skin lengthways along the ridges and lever it off with your thumb; a plantain does not peel like a banana, and a green one will not peel at all under running water. Green plantain fries twice: once at 150 °C to cook it through, then flatten, then again at 190 °C to crisp.
