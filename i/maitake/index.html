Maitake

Maïtaké

Latin nameGrifola frondosa
FamilyMushrooms
OriginJapan & North America
Season (France)September – November
FlavourEarthy · Umami · Woody · Meaty
Typical price (France)25–50 €/kg

What it is

Its Japanese name means dancing mushroom, said to describe people finding one — a single cluster at the base of an oak can weigh twenty kilos and reappear on the same tree for decades. English calls it hen of the woods.

In the kitchen

Roast it in large pieces at high heat until the frilled edges crisp. Sliced small it loses everything that makes it distinctive.
