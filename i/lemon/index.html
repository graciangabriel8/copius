What it is

Roman aristocrats displayed lemons as proof of wealth long before anyone squeezed one over fish. Centuries later, the British Navy loaded ships with citrus against scurvy — a fruit that won battles without firing a shot.

In the kitchen

The zest holds the perfume, the juice the acidity. A squeeze at the very end of cooking sharpens almost any dish that tastes flat.
