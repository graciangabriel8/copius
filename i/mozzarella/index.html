Mozzarella

Mozzarella

Latin nameMozzarella di bufala
FamilyDairy
OriginCampania, Italy
SeasonAll year
Flavourmilky · delicate · fresh · creamy
Typical price8–14 €/kg

What it is

Named for the gesture — mozzare, to lop off — as cheesemakers tear each ball from the stretched curd by hand. The true one comes from water buffalo, brought to Italian marshlands centuries ago, and connoisseurs eat it the day it was pulled, never chilled.

In the kitchen

Take it out of the fridge an hour ahead — cold numbs its milky perfume. Tear it, never slice it, and salt it just before serving.
