Zucchini

Courgette

Latin nameCucurbita pepo
FamilyVegetables
OriginAmericas, refined in Italy
Season (France)May – September
FlavourMild · Fresh · Green
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

Its ancestors crossed the Atlantic with the conquistadors, but the slim green zucchini we know was shaped in 19th-century Italian market gardens near Milan. Italians still prize its golden blossom as much as the fruit.

In the kitchen

It is nine-tenths water: cook it fast and hot, or salt and drain it, so it browns instead of steaming.
