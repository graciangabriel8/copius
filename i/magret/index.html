Duck breast

Magret de canard

Latin nameAnas (pectoralis, canard gras)
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginThe breast of a fattened duck
SeasonAll year
FlavourRich · Meaty · Sweet
Typical price18–28 €/kg

What it is

A cut with a birth certificate: in 1959, chef André Daguin at the Hôtel de France in Auch first cooked a fattened duck’s breast rare, like a steak — scandal, then triumph. So particular is the magret that French law reserves the name for breasts of ducks raised for foie gras.

In the kitchen

Score the skin in a diamond pattern, start in a cold pan skin-down, and render patiently — most of the cooking happens on that side. Rose inside, mahogany outside.
