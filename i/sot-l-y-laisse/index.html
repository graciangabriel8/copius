Poultry oyster

Sot-l’y-laisse

Latin nameGallus gallus domesticus
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginFrance, taken off the whole bird
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Rich · Delicate
Typical price (France)16–26 €/kg

What it is

The name is an eighteenth-century joke that explains itself: only a fool would leave it there. It is the pair of muscles sitting in the hollow of the pelvic bone above the parson's nose, which English calls oysters for the shape — two to a bird, perhaps fifteen grams each.

In the kitchen

Sold by the kilo off turkey and chicken carcasses, they want ninety seconds in foaming butter and a minute's rest — they are already the tenderest meat on the bird, and longer cooking only tightens them. Dry them well first or they will not colour.
