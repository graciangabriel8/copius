Signature dish · 2009

Edible stones

Andoni Luis Aduriz · 1971 · Mugaritz, Errenteria, Basque Country

Potatoes coated in kaolin clay and served as a bowl of pebbles. You are told they are food and you still hesitate, which is the dish.

Why it works

The clay is inert and sets to a dull mineral crust, so the eye reads stone while the tooth goes through into steamed potato — the gap between the two is the whole effect.

What is in it

Potato Calcium chloride
