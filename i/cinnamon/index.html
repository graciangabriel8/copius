Cannelle

FamilySpices
OriginSri Lanka
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · warm · woody
Typical price25–50 €/kg

What it is

Herodotus reported that giant birds built nests of cinnamon sticks on cliffs, and harvesters tricked them into dropping it — a tall tale spice merchants encouraged to guard their sources. The truth was quieter: the rolled inner bark of a Sri Lankan tree.

In the kitchen

True Ceylon cinnamon is soft and layered like a cigar; cassia is harder and louder. Bloom either in warm butter or oil to open the perfume.
