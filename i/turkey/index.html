Turkey

Dinde

Latin nameMeleagris gallopavo
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginMexico
SeasonNovember – December
FlavourMild · Meaty · Delicate · Umami
Typical price7–14 €/kg

What it is

A Mexican bird that English speakers named after Turkey and the French after India — dinde, from poule d'Inde — because both assumed anything exotic came from somewhere east. Neither guess was even close.

In the kitchen

Brine it or cook the legs and breast separately. They want temperatures forty minutes apart and no roasting method reconciles them.

Goes with

Chestnut Sage Cranberry Butter Thyme Onion Bay leaf Lard
