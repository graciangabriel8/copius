Stone pine kernel

Pignon de pin parasol

Latin namePinus pinea
FamilyNuts
OriginMediterranean
SeasonAll year
Flavournutty · resinous · sweet · buttery
Typical price100–180 €/kg

What it is

It takes three years for a stone pine cone to ripen, and the kernels are extracted by hand — which is why Mediterranean pine nuts cost several times what Chinese ones do. Some Chinese species cause a metallic taste distortion lasting days.

In the kitchen

Check the origin on the packet. Pinus pinea is the Mediterranean one and the only one worth paying for.
