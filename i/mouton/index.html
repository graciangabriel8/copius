Mutton

Mouton

Latin nameOvis aries
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginBritain, North Africa, the Middle East
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Rich · Musky · Earthy
Typical price (France)12–22 €/kg

What it is

English keeps the Norman word for the meat and the Saxon one for the animal — mutton on the table, sheep in the field — a split left over from the century when the people who ate it and the people who herded it spoke different languages. Mutton means a carcass over two years old, and its flavour comes from branched-chain fatty acids in the tallow that a lamb simply has not built yet.

In the kitchen

Trim the hard fat hard before cooking — that is where the strongest flavour sits — and give it three hours of moist heat, not one. It is the right animal for a navarin or a curry, where lamb dissolves into blandness.
