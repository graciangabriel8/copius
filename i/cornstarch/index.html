What it is

Invented in 1842 by Thomas Kingsford, who was trying to make starch for laundry rather than food — the culinary use came later and by accident. Its French brand name, Maïzena, has entirely replaced the generic word.

In the kitchen

Slake it in cold liquid first, always. Tipped into anything hot it seizes into lumps that will never dissolve.
