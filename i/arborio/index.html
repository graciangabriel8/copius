Arborio rice

Riz arborio

Latin nameOryza sativa
FamilyGrains & flours
OriginPo valley, Italy
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Creamy · Sweet · Delicate
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

The creaminess of a risotto is not cream but amylopectin, a starch that leaches out of these short fat grains as they are stirred. Arborio holds a firm core longest, which is why it forgives an inattentive cook.

In the kitchen

Never rinse it. Washing removes exactly the surface starch that makes the dish work.
