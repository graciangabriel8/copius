Chinese truffle

Truffe de Chine

FamilyMushrooms
Season (France)November – February
Typical price (France)60–150 €/kg

What it is

Tuber indicum is a genuine Asian species, harvested in Yunnan and Sichuan, that happens to look almost exactly like the Périgord truffle in cross-section while carrying a fraction of its aroma. DNA testing of European truffle products has turned it up repeatedly in jars and oils labelled melanosporum, and it has since been found growing in a few European orchards, its spores having travelled with the trade.

In the kitchen

Buy it knowingly and use it for what it does: its firm flesh holds through cooking, slices cleanly for garnish and fills out a stuffing where melanosporum would be wasted. Expect nothing from infusion — a night with eggs or in butter gives almost no aroma, so the perfume has to come from elsewhere.
