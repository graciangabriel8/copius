What it is

The name is a plural — broccoli, the little arms or shoots of the cabbage. Italian immigrants carried it to America in the 1920s, where it was so unfamiliar that shipments were labelled in Italian only; within twenty years it was on every plate.

In the kitchen

Do not throw away the stalk. Peeled of its tough skin it is sweeter and crunchier than the florets.
