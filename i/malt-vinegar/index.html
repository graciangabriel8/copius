Malt vinegar

Vinaigre de malt

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)4–8 €/L

What it is

Made from soured ale rather than wine, in a country that never had vineyards to spare. It is the one vinegar built for fried food — the malt sweetness stands up to hot fat where a wine vinegar just tastes thin.

In the kitchen

Shake it on hot chips the moment they leave the fryer, before they cool. Steam carries it into the potato.
