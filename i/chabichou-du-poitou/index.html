What it is

Its truncated-cone mould is called a bonde, after the wooden bung that stops a wine cask. The name of the cheese is said to come from chebli, Arabic for goat — the story goes that goats were left behind near Poitiers after 732 — which is a good deal more romantic than any document supports.

In the kitchen

It holds its shape in the oven where a lactic log slumps: split it across, sit it on toast under a hot grill for four or five minutes, and the paste warms without running.
