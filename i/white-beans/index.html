White beans

Haricots blancs

Latin namePhaseolus vulgaris
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginAmericas
SeasonAll year
FlavourMild · Buttery · Earthy
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

Beans crossed from the Americas in the 1530s; Catherine de’ Medici reportedly carried a pouch of them to France in her wedding luggage. Tuscans became such devotees that the rest of Italy nicknamed them mangiafagioli — the bean-eaters.

In the kitchen

Cook them gently — a hard boil bursts their jackets — and always with a bay leaf and a whole garlic clove. Their broth is liquid silver: keep it.
