Umezu

Vinaigre d’umeboshi (umezu)

Latin namePrunus mume
FamilyCondiments
OriginJapan, the umeboshi barrels
SeasonAll year
Flavoursalty · sour · fresh · herbal
Typical price12–22 €/L

What it is

Despite the name, umezu is not a vinegar: it is the brine the ume plums give up under salt, drawn off before the fruit goes out to dry in the July sun. The pink arrives only afterwards, when red shiso leaves are packed in and their pigment turns scarlet against the acid.

In the kitchen

Count it as salt and acid at once — a teaspoon replaces both the vinegar and the salt in a vinaigrette, so season only after tasting. Never reduce it: heat carries off the shiso perfume and leaves flat brine behind.
