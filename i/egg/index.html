What it is

Kitchen lore says the hundred folds of a chef’s toque stand for the hundred ways to cook an egg. It binds, it lifts, it enriches, it clarifies — no single ingredient does more jobs in a kitchen, and none does them more quietly.

In the kitchen

Old eggs peel easily but poach raggedly; fresh ones hold together in water. Know your egg’s age and give it the job it deserves.
