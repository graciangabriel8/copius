Mustard

Moutarde

Latin nameBrassica juncea / Sinapis alba
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginMediterranean & Asia; Dijon, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourHot · Pungent · Tangy
Typical price (France)3–8 €/kg

What it is

Romans ground the seeds into grape must — mustum ardens, “burning must”, gave mustard its name. The popes at Avignon kept an official Grand Moutardier du Pape, and Dijon built a gastronomic empire on the little seed’s fire.

In the kitchen

Heat kills its bite: whisk it in off the flame to finish a sauce. It is also nature’s emulsifier — the secret spine of every vinaigrette.
