Chenpi ✦

Chenpi (écorce de mandarine séchée)

Latin nameCitrus reticulata
FamilySpices
OriginXinhui, Guangdong
SeasonAll year
Flavourbitter · citrus · resinous · woody
Typical price25–60 €/kg

What it is

Under China's geographical indication the peel may not be called chenpi until it has aged three years, and the Xinhui district of Guangdong holds the name for the finest lots. Old vintages are dated, stored and traded much as old wine is, the raw bitterness of fresh peel having turned camphorous and medicinal.

In the kitchen

Soak briefly and scrape the white pith off the inside before use - that is where the raw bitterness sits. A piece the size of a thumbnail will perfume a whole duck or a pot of red bean.
