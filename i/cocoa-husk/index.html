Cocoa bean husk

Coques de cacao

FamilySweet pantry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

The husk is about a tenth of the bean's weight, winnowed off after roasting, and in nineteenth-century Britain and France it was sold on its own as cocoa shell tea to households that could not afford the nib. It carries the smoke of the roast with almost none of the fat and almost none of the bitterness.

In the kitchen

Infuse it like tea - about 30 g per litre of cream or milk at 80C for twenty minutes, then strain and press the husks. Boiling drags out a papery bitterness that will not leave again.
