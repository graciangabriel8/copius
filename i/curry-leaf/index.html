Curry leaf

Feuille de curry

Latin nameMurraya koenigii
FamilyHerbs
OriginIndia & Sri Lanka
SeasonAll year
Flavourcitrus · herbal · warm · resinous
Typical price8–15 €/100 g

What it is

Nothing to do with curry powder, which contains none of it. The leaf is thrown into hot oil at the very start of a South Indian dish, where it crackles and perfumes the fat — a step called tempering, and the dish is unrecognisable without it.

In the kitchen

Dried curry leaves are close to worthless. Buy them fresh and freeze what you do not use — they freeze perfectly.

Goes with

Mustard seed Coconut milk Turmeric Ghee Chili pepper Red lentil Ginger Lime
