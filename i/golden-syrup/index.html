Golden syrup

Golden syrup

Latin nameSaccharum officinarum
FamilySweet
OriginPlaistow, London
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · buttery · tangy
Typical price8–14 €/kg

What it is

Abram Lyle began selling it from his refinery on the Thames in 1881, and the tin has carried a dead lion, a swarm of bees and Samson's riddle since the 1880s - Guinness records it as the world's oldest unchanged brand packaging. The picture is apt: the syrup is a by-product of refining, sweetness left over from the strong.

In the kitchen

It is partly inverted, so it will not crystallise and it holds on to water - a spoonful in a biscuit dough keeps it chewy for days. Weigh it rather than spooning it, and oil the spoon or half of it stays behind.
