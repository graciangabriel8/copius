Japanese amberjack (buri)

Sériole du Japon (buri)

Latin nameSeriola quinqueradiata
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginSea of Japan, winter
Season (France)November – February
FlavourRich · Umami · Meaty
Typical price (France)35–60 €/kg

What it is

Buri is a shusse-uo, a fish renamed as it grows — wakashi, inada, warasa, then buri — so a New Year gift of one carries a wish for promotion. The winter fish taken off Himi and Toyama carries so much fat that the market quotes it separately, as kan-buri.

In the kitchen

For buri-daikon, simmer the daikon alone until translucent first, then add the fish for the last ten minutes. Pour boiling water over the collar and bones before they go in — that step is what removes the blood smell that ruins the broth.
