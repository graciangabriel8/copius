Rice vinegar

Vinaigre de riz

Latin nameAcetum oryzae
FamilyCondiments
OriginChina & Japan
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · sweet · mild · delicate
Typical price4–9 €/L

What it is

Far gentler than any European vinegar — around half the acidity — which is why sushi rice can be seasoned with it heavily without becoming sharp. Japan has made it since rice wine has existed, which is a very long time.

In the kitchen

Check whether it is seasoned. Sushi vinegar already contains sugar and salt, and using it as plain vinegar wrecks a dressing.

Goes with

Soy sauce Ginger Toasted sesame oil Rice Cucumber Spring onion Chili pepper Sesame
