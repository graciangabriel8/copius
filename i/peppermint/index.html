Peppermint

Menthe poivrée

Latin nameMentha × piperita
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginSterile hybrid, cultivated
Season (France)May – September
FlavourFresh · Pungent · Herbal
Typical price (France)25–50 €/kg

What it is

It is a sterile cross of watermint and spearmint, so every plant is a cutting of a cutting; the Mitcham clone grown in Surrey from the 1750s is still the reference for oil quality. Its dominant compound, menthol, acts on the cold receptor — the chill is a nerve signal, not a temperature.

In the kitchen

Never boil it: menthol is volatile and above 85 °C you drive off the cold note and keep the bitter herb. It does not stand in for garden mint, which is carvone-led and sweet — swap one for the other in a chocolate and you have made a different confection.
