Kukicha

Kukicha

Latin nameCamellia sinensis var. sinensis
FamilyCondiments
OriginJapan, from the tea sorting table
SeasonAll year
Flavournutty · creamy · mild
Typical price40–80 €/kg

What it is

Kukicha is what the sorting machines pull aside: stems and leaf ribs separated from the blade when sencha or gyokuro is graded. Caffeine concentrates in the young buds and barely reaches the stalks, so a tea made from the offcuts brews mild enough for a child and round enough to take milk.

In the kitchen

Stems need heat the blade cannot take: 80 °C and two full minutes, otherwise the cup comes out thin and tastes of nothing. Karigane — the same cut taken off gyokuro bushes — is the version worth the extra money.

Goes with

Rice Chestnut Honey White chocolate Hazelnut Pear
