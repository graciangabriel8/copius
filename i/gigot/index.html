Leg of lamb

Gigot d’agneau

Latin nameOvis aries (leg)
FamilyCuts
OriginThe hind leg, whole
SeasonMarch – June
Flavourmeaty · rich · grassy
Typical price18–30 €/kg

What it is

France’s Easter centrepiece, studded with garlic slivers and carved at the table by whoever holds rank. The seven-hour version — gigot de sept heures — cooks until it is served with a spoon, a dish old cookbooks sealed shut with a flour-and-water lute so no perfume could escape.

In the kitchen

Two schools, both right: hot and pink with the bone in, rested under foil — or seven hours at a whisper with garlic and white beans until the knife is unemployed.
