Pecan

Noix de pécan

Latin nameCarya illinoinensis
FamilyNuts
OriginMississippi valley
SeasonOctober – December
Flavoursweet · buttery · toasty · rich
Typical price20–35 €/kg

What it is

A native North American nut that indigenous peoples gathered long before any orchard existed — the word is Algonquian, for a nut requiring a stone to crack. Jefferson planted them at Monticello and sent trees to Washington.

In the kitchen

Sweeter and softer than a walnut, with none of its bitterness. They are not interchangeable in baking despite the resemblance.

Goes with

Maple syrup Butter Vanilla Dark chocolate Honey Cream Cinnamon Coffee
