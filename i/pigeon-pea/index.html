Pigeon pea (toor dal)

Pois d’Angole (toor dal)

Latin nameCajanus cajan
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginIndian subcontinent
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Sweet · Nutty · Mild
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

Cajanus cajan is a woody shrub that crops for several years running, which is why it stands at the edge of compounds and field boundaries from Gujarat to Jamaica rather than in tidy rows. The English name is traced to Barbados, where the seed was grown as feed for pigeons long before European kitchens took it seriously.

In the kitchen

Unsoaked toor dal takes about 40 minutes at a bare simmer and a spoon of oil to hold the foam down. It thickens hard as it cools, so stop the pot looser than you mean to serve it.
