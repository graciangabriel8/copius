Wedge clam

Telline

Latin nameDonax trunculus
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginCamargue & Landes beaches
Season (France)May – September
FlavourBriny · Marine · Sweet · Delicate
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

It lives in the swash zone and reburies itself with every wave, so it is taken by walking backwards through knee-deep surf dragging a wide-mouthed rake called a tellinier. Two centimetres is a normal one and a portion is counted in hundreds, which is why the Camargue limits a hand-gatherer to a kilo a day.

In the kitchen

Purge them a full day in cold sea water changed twice, then cook the whole kilo in one wide pan over the fiercest heat: thirty seconds after the last shell opens, they are done. Pour the juice off through muslin and give it back to the dish — there is always sand in it, and it is also the best thing on the plate.
