Shaoxing wine

Vin de Shaoxing

Latin nameOryza sativa var. glutinosa (brewed)
FamilyCellar
OriginShaoxing, Zhejiang
SeasonAll year
Flavourwarm · rich · umami · fruity
Typical price5–12 €/L

What it is

Shaoxing has brewed rice wine in clay jars sealed with lotus leaf and mud for centuries, and the custom of nü'er hong is still told there — a jar buried when a daughter is born, opened at her wedding. What matters in a kitchen is the ageing: oxidised and amber, the aroma sits closer to a dry sherry than to sake.

In the kitchen

Buy the drinkable bottle, not the one labelled cooking wine — that one is salted so it can be sold as a foodstuff, and it takes the seasoning of the dish out of your hands. Splash it down the side of a hot wok so it flashes off.
