Lamb tongue

Langue d’agneau

FamilyCuts of meat
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)9–16 €/kg

What it is

The rough skin over a tongue is keratin, the same material as a hoof, which is why every tongue is peeled and none is eaten with its surface intact. A lamb's is small enough that the skin slips off whole like a glove, and the tongue can then be served whole, one per plate — which neither ox nor veal allows.

In the kitchen

Ninety minutes in aromatic water is enough; beyond that they shrink and turn woolly. Peel them warm, split them lengthwise and dress them while still tepid — cold lamb tongue takes up almost nothing.
