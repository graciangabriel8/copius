Lavender honey

Miel de lavande

Latin nameLavandula angustifolia
FamilySweet
OriginHaute-Provence
SeasonJuly – August
Flavourfloral · sweet · delicate · herbal
Typical price18–30 €/kg

What it is

Hives are trucked up to the Provençal plateaux in July when the lavender opens, a migration beekeepers have made for generations. It crystallises into a fine white cream rather than gritty crystals, which is the mark of the real thing.

In the kitchen

Use it where its perfume is the point — over goat's cheese, in an ice cream, with apricots. Cooked hard it is wasted.

Goes with

Goat cheese Apricot Peach Almond Cream Fig Lemon Yogurt
