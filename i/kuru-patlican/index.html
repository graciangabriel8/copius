Dried aubergine ✦

Aubergine séchée

Latin nameSolanum melongena
FamilyVegetables
OriginSouth-eastern Anatolia
SeasonAll year
FlavourSmoky · Earthy · Umami · Mild
Typical price25–45 €/kg

What it is

In Gaziantep and Urfa small aubergines are hollowed out in late summer and strung up in the sun until the shells go papery, a winter store from long before refrigeration. Drying concentrates them into something smoky and dense that a fresh aubergine never becomes, whatever you do to it.

In the kitchen

Boil the shells three or four minutes only, just until pliable — any longer and they tear as you fill them. Stuff them two-thirds full: the rice swells and splits a tight shell open in the pot.
