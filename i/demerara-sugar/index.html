Demerara sugar

Sucre demerara

FamilySweet pantry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

Demerara is a river in what is now Guyana, and almost no demerara sugar has come from there for a long time - most of it is milled in Mauritius. The word survives as a crystal specification rather than an origin.

In the kitchen

Buy it for what the crystal does, not for molasses it barely has: scattered on a shortbread lid or a crumble it stays crunchy right through the oven. Creamed into a cake batter it simply dissolves, and you have paid extra for white sugar.
