Dried shiitake (donko)

Shiitaké séché (donko)

Latin nameLentinula edodes
FamilyMushrooms
OriginJapan and China
Season (France)All year
FlavourUmami · Earthy · Woody
Typical price (France)50–120 €/kg

What it is

Donko means winter mushroom: the cap is picked young and still curled under, in cold dry weather that cracks the surface into white fissures. The drying does the real work — enzymes build the guanylate that the fresh mushroom barely carries, and sunlight turns the ergosterol in the flesh into vitamin D.

In the kitchen

Soak in cold water in the fridge, six hours or overnight; hot water is faster but drags out bitterness. Bring the soaking liquid slowly up through 60–70 °C rather than straight to the boil — that is the band where the guanylate is released — and leave the gritty last centimetre in the bowl.
