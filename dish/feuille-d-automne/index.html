The dish that changed something · 1960s

Feuille d’automne

Gaston Lenôtre · 1920–2009 · Paris, from Normandy

Chocolate mousse and meringue under a canopy of chocolate shavings. Lenôtre worked out how to make pastry of this standard in quantity and send it out, which is why the rest of France could suddenly buy it.

Why it works

Meringue gives the crack, mousse gives the melt, and the shavings are the first resistance the spoon meets — the order of textures is the recipe.

What is in it

Dark chocolate Cream White sugar
