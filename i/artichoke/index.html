Artichaut

Latin nameCynara cardunculus var. scolymus
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean
SeasonMay – September
FlavourBitter · Earthy · Nutty · Delicate
Typical price1,50–3 €/pièce

What it is

It is an unopened thistle flower, eaten before it can bloom. It also contains cynarin, a compound that makes whatever you drink next taste sweet — which is why wine is famously difficult alongside it, and why sommeliers dread the course.

In the kitchen

Rub every cut surface with lemon immediately. It browns faster than almost any other vegetable.
