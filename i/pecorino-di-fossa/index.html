Pecorino di Fossa

Latin nameFormaggio di Fossa di Sogliano DOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginSogliano al Rubicone, Romagna
Season (France)November – February
FlavourPungent · Umami · Earthy · Salty
Typical price (France)40–60 €/kg

What it is

The wheels go down into sandstone pits in August, wrapped in cloth and sealed under a lid of clay and sand, and the pits stay shut until the feast of Santa Caterina on 25 November. Three months without oxygen do what no cellar does: the paste crumbles, loses its shape against the pit wall and turns sulphurous, almost truffled.

In the kitchen

It arrives already at full pitch, so use it as a seasoning: shave it over hot tagliatelle at the table rather than cooking with it, and salt the dish only after tasting.
