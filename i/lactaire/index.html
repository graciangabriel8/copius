Saffron milk cap

Lactaire délicieux

Latin nameLactarius deliciosus
FamilyMushrooms
OriginEuropean pine forests
Season (France)September – November
FlavourEarthy · Bitter · Nutty · Umami
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

Cut it and it bleeds orange latex that slowly turns green — alarming, and entirely normal. It appears in Roman frescoes at Herculaneum, making it one of the few mushrooms with a two-thousand-year-old portrait.

In the kitchen

Catalonia grills them whole with garlic and parsley and nothing else. They are too firm to benefit from a sauce.
