Signature dish · v. 2015

Beetroot in salt crust

Mauro Colagreco · 1976 · Mirazur, Menton — born in La Plata, Argentina

One beetroot from the garden above Menton, baked in salt for hours and served with a caviar cream.

Why it works

The crust seals the root so it cooks in its own water and loses nothing; the salt seasons through the skin at the rate the flesh can take it, which no seasoning afterwards can imitate.

What is in it

Beetroot Caviar Cream Fleur de sel de Guérande
