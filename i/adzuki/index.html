Adzuki bean

Haricot azuki

Latin nameVigna angularis
FamilyPulses
OriginEast Asia
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · earthy · nutty · mild
Typical price6–10 €/kg

What it is

In Japan and China it is treated as a dessert ingredient rather than a savoury one — boiled with sugar into anko, the sweet red paste inside almost every traditional confection. Red beans and rice are eaten at celebrations for the auspicious colour.

In the kitchen

Change the water once during cooking if you want a clean sweet paste. The first water carries the bitterness.

Goes with

White sugar Rice Sesame Vanilla Coconut milk Ginger Dark chocolate Honey
