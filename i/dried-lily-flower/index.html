Dried lily flower (golden needles)

Fleurs de lys séchées (aiguilles d’or)

Latin nameHemerocallis citrina
FamilyVegetables
OriginHunan and Gansu, China
SeasonAll year
Flavourmusky · earthy · mild
Typical price30–50 €/kg

What it is

Hemerocallis means beauty for a day, and the flower is exactly that — which is why the crop is the unopened bud, taken before it can spend itself. Fresh buds can make you ill; the drying and the long soak are not a preservation habit but the step that makes them edible.

In the kitchen

Soak in warm water for 30 minutes, then pinch off the hard woody nub at the stem end — skip it and every mouthful hits a splinter. Tie each one in a loose knot before it goes into hot-and-sour soup so it holds its shape.

Goes with

Wood ear Dried shiitake (donko) Pork Egg Chinkiang vinegar Silken tofu (kinugoshi) White pepper
