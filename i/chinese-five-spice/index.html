Chinese five-spice

Cinq-épices chinois

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–35 €/kg

What it is

Star anise, cassia, clove, fennel and Sichuan pepper is the classic set, but the five is doctrine rather than inventory — it names the five flavours of Chinese medicine, and plenty of commercial blends carry six or seven spices. Read the composition on a Cantonese jar and you will rarely land on five.

In the kitchen

It is anise-led and takes over fast: a level teaspoon per 500 g of meat. Add it with the braising liquid rather than the first sizzle of oil, since clove and cassia scorch and turn acrid.
