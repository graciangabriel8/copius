Black bean

Haricot noir

Latin namePhaseolus vulgaris
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginMexico & Central America
SeasonAll year
FlavourEarthy · Sweet · Creamy · Umami
Typical price4–7 €/kg

What it is

The backbone of Brazilian feijoada and Cuban moros y cristianos, and it colours its cooking liquid deep purple-black — that liquid is not waste but the sauce, and throwing it away is the commonest mistake made with this bean.

In the kitchen

Keep the cooking liquid and reduce it. It is where the flavour and the body of the dish live.

Goes with

Cumin Lime Cilantro / Coriander Chili pepper Garlic Onion Avocado Orange
