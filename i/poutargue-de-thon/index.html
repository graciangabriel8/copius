Tuna bottarga

Poutargue de thon

Latin nameThunnus thynnus (roe)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginSicily & Sardinia
SeasonAll year
Flavourumami · salty · meaty · rich
Typical price120–250 €/kg

What it is

The trap fisheries of western Sicily took bluefin on their spawning run, and the roe of the trapped females was salted and pressed into slabs; the great tonnara of Favignana ran from 1859 until it closed in 1977. What comes out is darker, meatier and far louder than mullet bottarga — the same technique on a fish twenty times the size.

In the kitchen

Grate it, do not shave it — the block is coarser and drier than mullet, and a slice sits on the tongue like leather. It will carry a dish that already has garlic, chilli and breadcrumbs in it, where mullet bottarga would vanish.

Goes with

Olive oil Garlic Lemon Chili pepper Parsley Tomato Taggiasca olive Celery
