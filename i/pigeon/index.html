Pigeon

Pigeon

Latin nameColumba livia domestica
FamilyMeat
OriginMediterranean & Near East
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · meaty · earthy · umami
Typical price15–25 €/pièce

What it is

Every French château had a colombier, and the right to keep one was a feudal privilege abolished in 1789 — the birds ate the peasants' grain and only the lord could own them. Its breast is dark because pigeons actually fly.

In the kitchen

Serve the breast pink, closer to duck than chicken. Cooked through it turns liverish and grey.

Goes with

Foie gras Cherry Beetroot Thyme Red wine vinegar Juniper berry Pea Hazelnut
