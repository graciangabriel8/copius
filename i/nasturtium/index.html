Nasturtium

Capucine

Latin nameTropaeolum majus
FamilyFlowers
OriginAndean South America
SeasonJune – October
FlavourPeppery · Hot · Floral
Typical price3–6 €/barquette (20 g)

What it is

Linnaeus named the genus Tropaeolum after the Roman trophy pole hung with a captured shield and helmet: the round leaf is the shield, the spurred flower the helmet. Its green seed pods, pickled, were Europe's garden substitute for capers long before anyone thought to put the flowers on a plate.

In the kitchen

Pick the pods green and firm and brine them for a week for something very close to a caper. Leaf heat tracks the plant's thirst — a nasturtium in dry soil is markedly hotter than the same plant watered.
