Maple syrup

Sirop d’érable

Latin nameAcer saccharum
FamilySweet pantry
OriginNortheastern North America
Season (France)March – April
FlavourSweet · Woody · Toasty · Warm
Typical price (France)20–35 €/L

What it is

Indigenous peoples of the northeastern forests taught settlers to read the “sugar moon” — the late-winter thaw when maples let their sap rise. It takes forty litres of sap, boiled down over days, to make a single litre of syrup: patience you can pour.

In the kitchen

Darker grades carry more maple character — save “golden” for pancakes and cook with “dark”. It loves pork fat and coffee equally.
