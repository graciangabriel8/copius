Lily bulb ✦

Bulbe de lys

Latin nameLilium brownii
FamilyVegetables
OriginLanzhou, Gansu, China
SeasonSeptember – November
Flavoursweet · mild · delicate
Typical price30–60 €/kg

What it is

Chinese cooking keeps two lilies apart: the bitter medicinal bulb of the pharmacopoeia, and the sweet Lanzhou bulb from Gansu, which never entered it and is grown as a vegetable. Only the second is worth cooking — it comes apart into separate white scales, each one crisp as a water chestnut.

In the kitchen

Break the bulb into scales and trim the brown edges, then cook them for barely a minute — stir-fried past that they go from crisp to chalky and never come back. Ten minutes in cold water takes off any lingering bitterness.
