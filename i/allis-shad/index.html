Alose

Latin nameAlosa alosa
FamilyFish
OriginLoire, Gironde & Atlantic rivers
SeasonApril – June
Flavourrich · meaty · marine · mild
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

It lives at sea and climbs the rivers once, in spring, to spawn - the Loire and the Gironde carried the great runs until the dams, and the counts now come in hundreds where they once came in tens of thousands. The classic alose à l'oseille braises it for hours on a bed of sorrel, whose acid was long held to dissolve the fine bones; cooks still disagree about whether it does.

In the kitchen

Give it two to three hours in a low oven on a thick bed of sorrel - a short cooking leaves you picking bones all through the meal. Have the fillets scored deeply every few millimetres: the cuts sever the fine bones, which no filleting removes.

Goes with

Sorrel Butter Cream Shallot White wine vinegar Lemon Parsley Bay leaf
