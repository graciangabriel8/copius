Pollock

Lieu noir

Latin namePollachius virens
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginNorth Atlantic
Season (France)October – March
FlavourMild · Marine · Delicate · Sweet
Typical price (France)8–14 €/kg

What it is

For decades it was the cheap alternative nobody wanted, sold as a filler and processed into surimi. Overfishing of cod turned it into the sustainable choice, and the same fish that was once a compromise is now the recommendation.

In the kitchen

It is drier than cod, so brine it briefly — twenty minutes in salted water before cooking makes a visible difference.
