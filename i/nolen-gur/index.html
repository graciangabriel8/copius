Date palm jaggery ✦

Jaggery de palmier dattier

Latin namePhoenix sylvestris
FamilySweet
OriginBengal, date palm groves
SeasonNovember – February
Flavoursmoky · sweet · rich
Typical price12–25 €/kg

What it is

The shiuli climb the date palms at dusk, cut a strip of bark and hang an earthen pot; the sap runs overnight and has to be boiled at dawn, because by midday it has begun to ferment. It is made only while Bengal's nights stay below about fourteen degrees - November to February, and not a week longer.

In the kitchen

It is semi-liquid and burns quickly - stir it into warm milk or rice pudding off the heat, never into a boiling pot. Cane jaggery is not a substitute; the smoke is the whole flavour.
