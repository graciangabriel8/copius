Saba-bushi

Saba-bushi (maquereau séché)

FamilyFish & seafood
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)60–100 €/kg

What it is

Tokyo soba shops rarely build a dipping broth on bonito alone — the kaeshi, dark with soy and sugar, swallows it. Mackerel dried into a bushi gives an oily, faintly bitter stock with enough weight to answer back, usually blended with round-herring and frigate-tuna shavings.

In the kitchen

Use thick shavings and a long, gentle steep — ten minutes around 85°C. Boiled hard the oil emulsifies: the broth clouds and turns frankly fishy, which is why it stays out of clear soups.
