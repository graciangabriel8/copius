Tomatillo

Tomatille

Latin namePhysalis philadelphica
FamilyVegetables
OriginMexico
SeasonJuly – October
Flavoursour · fresh · tangy · grassy
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

Domesticated in Mexico before the tomato and still the backbone of salsa verde. It grows inside a papery husk that splits when the fruit is ready — the plant's own ripeness indicator, and the reason it keeps for weeks.

In the kitchen

Char them under a grill before blending. Raw they are aggressively acidic; blistered they turn sweet and smoky.

Goes with

Cilantro / Coriander Chili pepper Lime Onion Garlic Avocado Cumin Olive oil
