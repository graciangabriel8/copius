Epazote

Épazote

Latin nameDysphania ambrosioides
FamilyHerbs
OriginMexico & Central America
SeasonJune – September
Flavourpungent · bitter · herbal · resinous
Typical price10–20 €/100 g

What it is

Mexican cooks put it in black beans for the same reason Germans put savory in theirs — it is held to reduce the gas. Its taste is genuinely divisive: descriptions run from petrol to turpentine to oregano, and all three are fair.

In the kitchen

One sprig for a whole pot, added in the last ten minutes. It is not an herb you scatter generously.

Goes with

Black bean Chili pepper Corn Onion Garlic Tomatillo Cilantro / Coriander Lime
