Burrata

Burrata

Latin nameBurrata di Andria IGP
FamilyDairy
OriginPuglia, Italy
SeasonAll year
Flavourcreamy · milky · delicate · sweet
Typical price4–8 €/pièce

What it is

Invented on a Puglian farm around 1900 to use up mozzarella offcuts: the scraps were shredded, drowned in cream, and wrapped in a pouch of fresh curd. A cheese designed so that nothing would be thrown away is now the most expensive thing on the antipasti list.

In the kitchen

Eat it the day you buy it, at room temperature, and open it at the table. Its whole appeal is the moment the cream runs out.

Goes with
