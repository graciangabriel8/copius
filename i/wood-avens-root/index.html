Wood avens root ✦

Racine de benoîte

Latin nameGeum urbanum
FamilySpices
OriginEuropean woodland edges
Season (France)March – April, September – November
FlavourWarm · Woody · Earthy
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

Augsburg brewers hung a linen bag of the dried root inside each cask, and the beer was said to keep from souring as well as taste of clove — the root carries eugenol, the compound cloves are named for. Its French name is a worn-down herba benedicta, blessed herb, the heading monastic gardeners filed it under.

In the kitchen

Dig it in autumn or very early spring and use it fresh; dried and kept a year it holds almost nothing. One root the length of a finger will clove a litre of milk or stock, and it comes out at twenty minutes or turns astringent.
