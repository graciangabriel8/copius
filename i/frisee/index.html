Frisée

Frisée

Latin nameCichorium endivia var. crispum
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean
Season (France)September – December
FlavourBitter · Fresh · Green · Mild
Typical price (France)1,50–3 €/pièce

What it is

Growers tie the outer leaves over the heart or cap it with a plate for a fortnight before harvest, so the centre blanches to pale yellow and loses its bitterness. The dark green edge and the blond heart are the same leaf, treated differently by light.

In the kitchen

It is built for a hot dressing — bacon fat, a poached egg, a splash of vinegar. Its ribs stand up to heat where a soft lettuce collapses.
