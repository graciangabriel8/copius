What it is

Persia grew it, Arab agronomists praised it as the “prince of vegetables”, and it reached French court cooking with Catherine de’ Medici — dishes served “à la florentine” still bow to her Florence. Popeye’s iron myth came later, from a misplaced decimal point.

In the kitchen

A mountain of leaves melts to a handful: buy more than seems reasonable, and finish with butter and a whisper of nutmeg.
