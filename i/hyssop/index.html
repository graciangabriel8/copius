Hyssop

Hysope

Latin nameHyssopus officinalis
FamilyHerbs
OriginSouthern Europe
Season (France)June – September
FlavourBitter · Herbal · Floral · Resinous
Typical price (France)4–8 €/100 g

What it is

Named in the Bible and grown in monastery gardens for a thousand years, it fell almost entirely out of cooking — too bitter, too medicinal for modern palates. It survives mainly in Chartreuse and a handful of other monastic liqueurs.

In the kitchen

Treat it like a bitter rather than an herb. A few leaves in a fruit compote or a game sauce, and no more.
