Hydrolysed soy protein (Versawhip) ✦

Protéine de soja hydrolysée

Latin nameGlycine max (hydrolysate)
FamilyTexture agents
OriginEnzyme-treated soy protein, United States
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Nutty
Typical price (France)150–300 €/kg

What it is

Enzymes chop soy protein into fragments short enough to unfold and race to the air-water interface, which is what whipping actually requires; the result foams at a fraction of the dose of egg white. It also ignores everything that ruins a meringue — heat, acid, alcohol — so a hot foam or a whipped spirit becomes possible where albumin would simply curdle.

In the kitchen

Disperse 0.5 to 1 % in cold liquid, add a scrape of xanthan for stability, and whip it in a stand mixer for five to eight minutes: it starts more slowly than egg white, then climbs all at once. It sets no structure on standing, so pipe it while it is up.
