Cavolo nero

Chou noir de Toscane

Latin nameBrassica oleracea var. palmifolia
FamilyVegetables
OriginTuscany, Italy
Season (France)October – February
FlavourBitter · Earthy · Mild · Umami
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

Tuscans will tell you ribollita cannot be made before the first frost, and they are not being romantic — the cold converts starch to sugar and the bitterness drops. The soup is named for being reboiled: it is meant to be a day old.

In the kitchen

Strip the leaf from the stem by pulling it backwards down the rib. The stem is too fibrous to soften in any reasonable time.
