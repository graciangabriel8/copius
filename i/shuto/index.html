Shutō (fermented bonito viscera) ✦

Shutō

FlavourSalty · Umami · Briny · Rich
Typical price (France)100–200 €/kg

What it is

The name means sake thief: the salt is so insistent that a cup vanishes beside it, and Kochi has sold it under that name for a couple of centuries. It is bonito stomach and gut, heavily salted and left to break down for months until it is dark, sticky and closer to a seasoning than a dish.

In the kitchen

Treat it as salt with an opinion: half a teaspoon melted into butter will season a whole plate of pasta or a pan of clams. If a piece is too fierce to eat straight, chop it with spring onion and a drop of sesame oil.
