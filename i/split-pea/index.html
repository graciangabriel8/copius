Split pea

Pois cassé

Latin namePisum sativum
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginNear East
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Earthy · Mild · Creamy
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

A field pea dried, skinned and split along its natural seam, which is where it wants to divide anyway. Splitting doubles the surface and halves the cooking time — a piece of food engineering several thousand years old.

In the kitchen

It foams enormously at first boil. Skim it once and the soup stays clear instead of turning grey.
