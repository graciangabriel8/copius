Chinese rose wine ✦

Alcool de rose chinois (mei kuei lu)

Latin nameRosa rugosa
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginNorthern China, Cantonese kitchens
Season (France)All year
FlavourFloral · Sweet · Warm
Typical price (France)8–16 €/L

What it is

Sorghum spirit is steeped with rose petals and rock sugar and bottled at around fifty-four percent, strong enough that a spoonful is the whole dose. It is what makes Cantonese lap cheong smell of roses rather than of pork, and no rice or grain wine stands in for it.

In the kitchen

A teaspoon per 500 g of pork is the working dose in char siu; beyond that the rose turns soapy. Put it into the marinade cold, since heat drives the aroma off, and brush on another half-teaspoon at the end of the roasting so something reaches the table.
