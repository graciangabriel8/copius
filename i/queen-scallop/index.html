Queen scallop

Pétoncle

Latin nameAequipecten opercularis
FamilyShellfish
OriginIrish Sea, Isle of Man
SeasonJune – December
Flavoursweet · marine · delicate
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

The Manx queenie fishery works by provoking the animal: a light chain towed ahead of the trawl makes the scallop clap its valves and swim straight up into the net. That escape response is sluggish in cold water, so the fishery opens only in June, once the sea has warmed enough for the queenies to jump.

In the kitchen

It weighs a third of a king scallop and cooks in a third of the time: thirty seconds a side in a dry, very hot pan, no more. Sold shucked, it sits in its own water, so drain it on paper first or the pan loses heat and poaches it grey.
