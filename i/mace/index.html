Mace

Macis

Latin nameMyristica fragrans
FamilySpices
OriginBanda Islands, Indonesia
SeasonAll year
Flavourwarm · floral · sweet · musky
Typical price150–300 €/kg

What it is

The scarlet lace that wraps a nutmeg seed inside the fruit — one tree yields both spices, and mace is far scarcer because there is so little of it per nut. The Dutch fought a war for the Banda Islands over exactly this.

In the kitchen

It is nutmeg's more delicate sibling — use it where nutmeg would be too heavy, in a béchamel or a pale potted meat.
