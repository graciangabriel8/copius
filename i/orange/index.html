Orange

Orange

Latin nameCitrus sinensis
FamilyFruit
OriginSouthern China
SeasonNovember – March
Flavoursweet · citrus · floral
Typical price2–4 €/kg

What it is

The word travelled with the fruit: Sanskrit naranga became Persian narang, Arabic naranj, then orange. Kings built glass palaces — orangeries — just to keep these trees alive through northern winters.

In the kitchen

Its zest loves dark chocolate and duck; its segments lift a fennel or beet salad out of winter gloom.

Goes with
