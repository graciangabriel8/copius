Edamame

Édamame

Latin nameGlycine max
FamilyVegetables
OriginJapan
Season (France)July – September
FlavourFresh · Green · Buttery
Typical price (France)4–8 €/kg

What it is

The name means stem bean: in Edo they were sold boiled and still attached to the cut stalk, a summer street food eaten standing up. They are ordinary soybeans picked at about four-fifths of maturity, and their sugar starts converting to starch within hours of harvest, which is why frozen ones are often better than fresh ones that have travelled.

In the kitchen

Rub the pods with coarse salt before boiling — it scours the fuzz and drives salt through the shell — then five minutes at a hard boil and straight onto a tray to cool in the air, never in water. Salt them again while they are steaming hot; a pod salted cold tastes of nothing.
