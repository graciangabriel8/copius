Arracacha

Arracacha

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

It belongs to the carrot and celery family and was domesticated in the northern Andes; Brazil, where it is called mandioquinha-salsa, now grows more of it than anywhere else. Its roots begin to break down within days of lifting, which is why a vegetable that feeds millions of Colombians has almost no export trade.

In the kitchen

It cooks in half the time of potato and passes the point of no return quickly: twelve to fifteen minutes at a simmer and it is already purée. Mash it with butter and nothing else — it thickens a soup on its own and needs neither cream nor flour.
