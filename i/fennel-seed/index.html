Fennel seed

Graine de fenouil

Latin nameFoeniculum vulgare
FamilySpices
OriginMediterranean
Season (France)All year
FlavourAnise-like · Sweet · Warm · Herbal
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

The Greeks called the plant marathon and named a battlefield after the fennel growing on it — the run that gave us the marathon began in a fennel field. Indian restaurants still set out sugared seeds by the door as a digestive.

In the kitchen

Crack the seeds lightly before they go into a sausage or a pork rub. Whole they stay inert; powdered they turn medicinal.
