Fuki (butterbur stalk) ✦

Pétiole de pétasite du Japon (fuki)

Latin namePetasites japonicus
FamilyVegetables
OriginJapanese mountain villages
Season (France)March – May
FlavourBitter · Green · Fresh
Typical price (France)18–35 €/kg

What it is

Fuki is one of the few vegetables domesticated in Japan itself rather than brought in from the continent, and the Akita variety throws leaves wide enough to shelter a child under. The part eaten is the leaf stalk, cut from March to May, and it is inedible until it has been de-bittered: rolled in salt on the board, blanched, then peeled while still hot.

In the kitchen

Do the salt rolling, itazuri, before blanching, not after — it bruises the skin so the strings lift away in one pull. Peel the stalks while they are still hot; cold, the skin welds back on and takes the flesh with it.
