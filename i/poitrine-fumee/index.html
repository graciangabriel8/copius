Smoked cured pork belly

poitrine fumée

Latin nameSus scrofa (belly, brined and smoked)
FamilyMeat
OriginNorthern Europe
SeasonAll year
Flavoursmoky · salty · meaty · woody
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

Brine first, smoke second: the belly takes salt in a cure, then hangs over beech, oak or fruitwood — cold-smoked below 30 °C for aroma alone, hot-smoked above 60 °C when the smoke is meant to cook it too. The smoke once did the work of a dry cellar in a wet climate; now it is only flavour, and the loudest one in the pot.

In the kitchen

Blanch the lardons two minutes in boiling water before frying if the dish is delicate — it takes the harshest edge off the smoke and the salt and leaves the rest. Never do this to pancetta.
