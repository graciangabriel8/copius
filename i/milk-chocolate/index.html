Milk chocolate

Chocolat au lait

Latin nameTheobroma cacao
FamilySweet pantry
OriginVevey, Switzerland, 1875
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Creamy · Milky · Rich
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

Daniel Peter spent eight years failing to combine milk and chocolate — the water in milk seizes cocoa butter. His neighbour Henri Nestlé had just invented condensed milk, and the two of them solved it in 1875 by removing the water first.

In the kitchen

It burns and seizes far more easily than dark. Melt it over water that is barely steaming, never simmering.
