Bayonne ham

Jambon de Bayonne

Latin nameJambon de Bayonne IGP
FamilyMeat
OriginAdour basin, southwest France
SeasonAll year
Flavoursalty · umami · nutty · rich
Typical price25–40 €/kg

What it is

Salted with salt from the Adour basin — the designation requires it — and dried through a winter in air off the Pyrenees. The producers still rub the exposed face with a paste of pork fat and flour to slow the drying, a step called the pannage.

In the kitchen

Slice it thicker than Spanish ham and let it come to room temperature. Cold and paper-thin, it tastes only of salt.

Goes with

Cantaloupe melon Fig Butter Piquillo pepper Olive oil Espelette pepper Comté T65 bread flour
