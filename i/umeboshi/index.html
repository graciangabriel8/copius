Umeboshi

Umeboshi

Latin namePrunus mume (salted)
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginJapan
SeasonAll year
FlavourSour · Salty · Fruity · Pungent
Typical price30–60 €/kg

What it is

Ume — an apricot cousin, not a plum — salted for months and blushed crimson with red shiso. Samurai carried them as field rations against fatigue, and a single one centred on white rice makes the hinomaru bentō: lunch as the Japanese flag.

In the kitchen

Use the flesh as a paste: a fingertip’s worth wakes a rice bowl, a dressing or steamed greens with salt and lightning at once.
