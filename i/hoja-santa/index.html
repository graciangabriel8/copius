Hoja santa ✦

Hoja santa (feuille sainte)

Latin namePiper auritum
FamilyHerbs
OriginSouthern Mexico
SeasonJune – September
FlavourAnise-like · Herbal · Peppery
Typical price25–50 €/100 g

What it is

The aroma is safrole, the compound that once flavoured sassafras root beer and that American regulators pulled from the market in 1960 — the leaf itself has stayed a daily herb across southern Mexico throughout. Piper auritum is a true pepper relative, and grows leaves the size of a dinner plate.

In the kitchen

Use the leaf as the vessel: wrap fish or fresh cheese in it and the aroma transfers through the steam without the leaf being eaten. Blended into a green mole, strip out the thick central vein first or the sauce turns stringy and bitter.
