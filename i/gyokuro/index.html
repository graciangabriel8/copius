Gyokuro

Gyokuro

Latin nameCamellia sinensis var. sinensis
FamilyCondiments
OriginUji, Kyoto prefecture
SeasonApril – May
Flavourumami · sweet · marine
Typical price200–600 €/kg

What it is

The bushes are covered for roughly three weeks before picking. Starved of light the leaf stops building the bitter catechins and stockpiles theanine instead, which is why a cup of gyokuro reads closer to a shellfish stock than to tea.

In the kitchen

Brew at 50–60 °C and never hotter — above that the catechins come out and bury the umami. Two minutes, a heavy dose of leaf, very little water; keep the spent leaves, they are edible dressed with soy and vinegar.

Goes with

Rice Scallop Gilt-head bream Nori Egg Kombu
