Languedoc olive oil

Huile d’olive du Languedoc

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)28–36 €/L

What it is

The appellation rules out a single-variety oil: every lot blends at least two varieties, led by the Lucques or the Olivière, and neither may exceed 70% of the oil. The olives are picked when 40 to 70% have turned from green to yellow, fruit the specification calls moderately ripe, and milled within eight days, which is where its dominant tomato and almond come from; bitterness stays at 3 out of 10 or below. It became a European AOP in 2023.

In the kitchen

A medium-intensity oil for everyday raw use: dress tomatoes, grilled courgettes or sardines with it, and reach for it wherever a more bitter oil would take over.
