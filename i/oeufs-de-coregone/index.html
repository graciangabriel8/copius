Vendace roe ✦

Œufs de corégone

Latin nameCoregonus albula (roe)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginBothnian Bay, Sweden
SeasonSeptember – October
Flavoursweet · delicate · briny · mild
Typical price200–400 €/kg

What it is

Kalix löjrom took a European protected designation in 2010, one of a handful Sweden holds, and the fishery runs for a regulated five weeks from around the twentieth of September. The brackish water of the Bothnian Bay is what makes it orange — vendace caught in fresh water give roe that is plainly yellow.

In the kitchen

Serve it cold on a warm blini with sour cream and finely diced red onion, and cut the onion at the last second so its juice never sits on the grain.

Goes with

Crème fraîche Red onion Dill Potato Butter Rye berries Lemon
