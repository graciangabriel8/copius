Ghee

Ghee

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–25 €/kg

What it is

Butter simmered until the water boils off and the milk solids brown at the bottom, then strained — which removes everything that would spoil. In a climate without refrigeration it keeps for a year, and Vedic texts treat it as sacred.

In the kitchen

Let the solids brown, do not just melt the butter. That browning is the difference between ghee and clarified butter.
