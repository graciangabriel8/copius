Shrimp

Crevette

Latin namePenaeus spp.
FamilyShellfish
OriginAll the world’s coasts
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · marine · briny · delicate
Typical price15–25 €/kg

What it is

Greeks grilled them over coals twenty-five centuries ago and argued about it in comedies. The cook’s secret has not changed since: the sweetness is in the shell — every discarded head is a spoonful of bisque thrown away.

In the kitchen

They cook in the time it takes to regret walking away — two minutes, until just pink and curled into a loose C. A tight O means overdone.

Goes with

Garlic Chili pepper Lemon Coconut milk Cilantro / Coriander Saffron
