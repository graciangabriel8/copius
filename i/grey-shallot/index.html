Grey shallot

Échalote grise

Latin nameAllium oschaninii
FamilyVegetables
OriginCentral Asian foothills
SeasonAugust – January
FlavourPungent · Sweet · Warm
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

It is not a variety of the common shallot but a separate species, and it sets no usable seed: every grey shallot in France descends by division from the one before it. Cooks call it the true shallot, and the seed-grown shallots of most supermarkets are, botanically, onions.

In the kitchen

It is a beast to peel — a minute in hot water and the grey skin lifts off in one piece. For a beurre rouge or a bercy, sweat it in butter without colour; browning it costs exactly the aromatics you paid extra for.
