Smoked sea salt

Sel fumé

Latin nameSodium chloride (cold-smoked)
FamilyCondiments
OriginNorthern Europe
SeasonAll year
Flavoursmoky · salty · woody
Typical price12–25 €/kg

What it is

It is cold-smoked, never heated: the crystals sit over smouldering wood for a day or more at a temperature that will neither melt nor dry them, taking the smoke on as an aroma rather than a coating. Colour tells you the wood - alder gives a pale gold, oak and beech a deeper brown.

In the kitchen

The aroma is volatile, so it goes on at the table or off the heat; cooked into a braise it disappears and you have paid for plain salt. A pinch on eggs, butter or a boiled potato is where it reads loudest.
