What it is

The Italian double zero describes only how far the grain has been refined, so a 00 sold for shortcrust and a 00 sold for Neapolitan pizza carry the same label and behave nothing alike. What separates them is the W value printed alongside: around 180 for fresh pasta, 300 and above for a dough meant to proof twenty-four hours.

In the kitchen

Read the W, not the zeros. For fresh pasta, 100 g of a soft 00 takes one egg and no water at all, and the sheet should leave the rollers silky rather than floury.
