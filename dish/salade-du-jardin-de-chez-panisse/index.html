Signature dish · 1971

Chez Panisse garden salad

Alice Waters · 1944 · Chez Panisse, Berkeley, California

Mixed young leaves, picked that day, dressed simply. It sounds like nothing now because this dish is the reason it sounds like nothing.

Why it works

Young leaves carry water and bitterness in a narrow window; picked that morning and dressed at the last second, the oil coats before the salt can draw anything out.
