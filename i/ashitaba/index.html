Ashitaba

Ashitaba (angélique d’Hachijō)

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)March – May
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

The name means tomorrow’s leaf: pick one today and another is said to be up by morning, a claim the plant nearly earns, since it regrows in a few days. Cut the stem and it bleeds a sulphur-yellow sap loaded with chalcones — that sap is the bitterness you taste, and the reason the islanders drank the plant long before they cooked it.

In the kitchen

For tempura, batter only the underside of the leaf and fry at 170 °C for twenty seconds, green side up — coated on both faces it steams and the bitterness turns metallic. As a green, blanch thirty seconds in salted water and shock it: the bitterness drops back to something like celery.
