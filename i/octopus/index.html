Octopus

Poulpe

Latin nameOctopus vulgaris
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginMediterranean & eastern Atlantic
Season (France)May – October
FlavourSweet · Marine · Meaty · Umami
Typical price (France)12–22 €/kg

What it is

Greek fishermen beat it against rocks forty times to break down the collagen, and freezing does exactly the same job — ice crystals rupture the muscle fibres. A frozen octopus is genuinely more tender than a fresh one.

In the kitchen

Simmer it with a wine cork in the water if you like — the tradition is harmless. Freezing it first is what actually works.
