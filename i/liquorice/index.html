Liquorice

Réglisse

Latin nameGlycyrrhiza glabra
FamilySpices
OriginMiddle East & southern Europe
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · anise · earthy · bitter
Typical price20–40 €/kg

What it is

Its sweetness comes from glycyrrhizin, which is around fifty times sweeter than sugar and works on a completely different receptor — which is why the sweetness arrives late and lingers. Roots were found in Tutankhamun's tomb alongside the coriander.

In the kitchen

A small piece of root in a braise with lamb or beef adds depth nobody can identify. Powdered it takes over instantly.

Goes with

Lamb Dark chocolate Fennel Beef Orange Star anise Cream Raspberry
