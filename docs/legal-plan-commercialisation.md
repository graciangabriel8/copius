# The legal plan for the first euro

**15 September 2026.** Six research lanes, three adversarial auditors. 129 claims
were re-opened against their sources: 105 confirmed, 16 overstated, 5
unverifiable, 2 stale, 1 wrong. The corrections are folded in below and marked
where they changed the answer.

Nothing here is due before June 2027. It exists so the decisions that *are*
irreversible get made deliberately rather than by default.

## The one thing that is irreversible

**Choose the business address before opening the INPI formality, not after.**

Everything else on this page can be changed later. That cannot, cheaply.

There is **no lawful way to sell online in France without a postal address being
publicly readable.** Both the e-commerce identification duty and the consumer
pre-contractual duty demand the address where the trader is established, and
there is no exemption for micro-sellers, students or digital goods. The
achievable outcome is not "no address" — it is "my family's home is not the
address."

Three corrections the auditors made here:

- **Home domiciliation is not blocked.** The researcher said he cannot domicile
  at home because he is neither owner nor tenant. Wrong: it works on an
  *attestation d'hébergement* signed by the lease-holder. So the route is open —
  it simply publishes the family home, which is the thing to avoid.
- **LCEN article 19 is not expiring.** An earlier review flagged the cited
  version as ending 1 January 2027. That date is a VAT cross-reference being
  recodified; the substance is untouched.
- **The August 2025 decree letting company directors hide their personal address
  at the RCS does not cover entrepreneurs individuels.** It does not help him.

**The instrument** is a domiciliation contract, roughly **11–25 € HT/month**
(2026 figures; 2027 not published), minimum three months, signed *before* the
INPI filing so the domiciliation address is what enters the register.

The free **INSEE non-diffusion** request is real and worth filing immediately
after immatriculation — but know its limit: it cleans the Sirene-fed directories,
not the RNE, and it never removes the address he must publish himself.

**One open question worth a lawyer's written line before the first sale:**
article 1-1 LCEN says *domicile* where the selling-specific texts say *établi*.
Whether publishing a domiciliation address discharges the article 1-1 wording for
a personne physique is not settled by anything official either lane could open.

## The numbers

**Cotisations URSSAF, 2026.** 25,6 % of revenue for BNC (activité libérale non
réglementée), 21,2 % for BIC prestations de services. Flat on revenue, not
profit. The 4,4-point gap is decided by how the activity is classified *on the
creation form* — settle it before filing.

*Corrected:* 25,6 % is the **end** of a completed trajectory, not a rising path.
It was scheduled to reach 26,1 % on 1 January 2026 and a décret of 8 September
2025 cut it to 25,6 %. Plan on 25,6 % and treat any further move as a new
political decision.

**CFP** adds 0,2 % for a liberal activity, so the all-in is **25,8 %** — and
*corrected:* a micro-entrepreneur pays it inside every URSSAF échéance, not as a
single November payment. That cash-flow shape belongs to the classic EI.

**ACRE** still exists and he qualifies on age alone (18–25). But the exoneration
was cut from 50 % to 25 % for micro-entreprises created on or after 1 July 2026
(décret n° 2026-69). First-year BNC is therefore **19,2 %**, not 12,8 %.

*The trap the audit caught:* the research told him both to create early and
declare zero while building, *and* to protect ACRE. Those contradict. The ACRE
window runs from the declared start date, so every empty quarter burns a
reduction he cannot recover. **Create on the first day of the quarter in which he
actually starts selling.**

**Micro ceilings, 2026:** 83 600 € for services and BNC. A non-constraint.

## TVA — a near-non-event, with three tripwires

Under the franchise en base he charges nothing and writes one sentence on every
invoice: *« TVA non applicable, article 293 B du code général des impôts »*, with
no VAT amount, rate or line anywhere on the document.

**The contested 25 000 € single threshold is abandoned, not suspended** —
deleted by article 1 of loi n° 2025-1044 du 3 novembre 2025, and the
administration's doctrine of 1 July 2026 reflects the deletion. Thresholds for
services are **37 500 €** (year N-1) and **41 250 €** (in-year).

The tripwires:

1. **41 250 € in-year.** VAT starts the day he crosses it, mid-invoice. Digital
   sales and restaurant consulting count together — both are services.
2. **10 000 € of B2C sales to consumers in other EU countries**, cumulated
   EU-wide. Below it, French rules absorb them. Above, VAT is due in the buyer's
   country through OSS — *and the French franchise does not cover this*, whatever
   his French turnover. At 19 € a sale that is roughly 525 sales.
3. **First-year proration.** BOFiP prorates the *ceilings*, not the turnover, so
   the in-year hard stop in the launch year sits below 41 250 € in proportion to
   the days remaining. Confirm with his SIE before pricing a launch.

**The channel is a VAT decision, not a fee decision.** A genuine merchant of
record (Paddle states it is the reseller) takes the VAT problem entirely. Stripe
direct does not — he is the seller and owns EU VAT himself.

## Consumer law — cheap, and two things load-bearing

The whole layer costs about **30 €** (a mediator subscription) plus one careful
checkout page.

**The digital-content waiver is all-or-nothing.** C. consom. art. L221-28 13°:
three cumulative conditions plus a durable-medium confirmation kept as proof. If
any one is missing, the buyer withdraws within 14 days, keeps the file and owes
nothing.

**The checkout order is prescribed:** order recap with total and an edit control,
the L221-14 information, the waiver acknowledgement, then a button that says the
order carries an obligation to pay. Not a template's order — this one.

**Delete the ODR-platform link** that every CGV template still carries. The
platform closed on 20 July 2025.

**Selling to a lycée removes this layer entirely** — no rétractation, no
mediator. It needs a different document set, not a trimmed consumer one.

## The drawings sentence — already closed, keep it closed

Checked today: **the live site makes no hand-drawn claim.** Zero matches across
the HTML, JS and manifest, with the grep proven to fire on a planted string. The
unsent AURA email no longer carries *"dessin original"* either.

The rule to keep: *"original hand-drawn illustration"* beside a price would be a
false statement about the *mode de fabrication* of the thing being paid for, and
L121-2 2° b) names mode de fabrication explicitly. Today the site is free and
non-professional so the article does not bite; the day a price appears next to
that sentence, it does.

*The auditors declined to certify the 750 000 € figure as his exposure* — the
tier exists, but quoting a maximum as a personal forecast is not sound. The
remedy is free either way: say *illustrations vectorielles originales* or
*pictogrammes dessinés pour Copius*, and claim hand-made only where a source file
proves it.

## Taking money

**Immatriculation comes first, whatever the form asks.** Stripe's onboarding
schema for a French individual contains no SIRET field — the endpoint was pulled
and the 17 returned fields carry none. But Stripe's Services Agreement forbids
using the Services *"for personal, family, or household purposes"*, and its
"individual" entity type means sole trader, not private person. French law points
the same way: selling repeatedly without immatriculation is **travail dissimulé
par dissimulation d'activité** — 3 years and 45 000 € for a personne physique.

**The registre des traitements becomes mandatory the day he has customers.**
CNIL names *gestion des clients/prospects* as the example of a non-occasional
processing that cancels the under-250-employees derogation. Start from CNIL's
free .ods model, the week the first customer pays.

**Record the payment provider as a dual role** — sous-traitant for operating the
platform, and controller in its own right for its fraud and compliance duties.

**Retention is sequential, not contradictory:** base active for the life of the
commercial relationship, then archivage intermédiaire on a different basis
holding invoices. *Corrected:* the 10-year figure comes from the Code de commerce
and binds *commerçants*. If the activity is BNC he is not one, and the binding
floor is LPF L102 B at **6 years**. Keeping 10 is safe either way.

**E-invoicing, corrected.** The 1 September 2027 emission deadline covers
invoices between French *assujettis*. Selling a learning path to individuals is
B2C — that is **e-reporting**, a different obligation. A lycée is a non-assujetti
public buyer and is invoiced through **Chorus Pro**, which has bound suppliers of
every size since 2020. And a plateforme agréée is generally a paid service; the
reform has already been postponed once.

## The lycée route

**Legally easy, structurally hard.**

Easy: since 1 April 2026 a public buyer can order anything under **60 000 € HT**
with no advert, no tender and no competitor. A 300 € or 800 € licence is a devis,
a bon de commande and the proviseur's signature.

Hard: three gates. He must exist as an opérateur économique — the invoice legally
requires a SIREN. Invoices go through Chorus Pro. And if pupils are to reach
Copius through the ENT médiacentre, the supplier must adhere to the **GAR**: a
ministry contract plus three built interfaces. That is a rewrite of a static PWA,
not a setting.

*Corrected:* the research said favoritisme is the buyer's risk "not his". A
private contractor who participates is routinely prosecuted as **complice** under
art. 121-7 C. pén. Keep a devis in the file and do not arrange the comparison.

**LDE/PopLab referencing is not a legal requirement** — that is specific to
manuels scolaires.

**The Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes appel à projets is not a door for him.** Only lycées
apply, naming a resource by identifier inside their own dossier, and eligible
resources are GAR-compatible. No 2026-2027 edition was found published as of
today; last year's opened on 22 September.

**Time any approach to the budget cycle, not the school year:** the Région
notifies the dotation before 1 November and the CA votes the budget before 30
November.

## Three student traps

1. While rattaché to his parents' foyer fiscal, **eligibility for the versement
   libératoire is tested on his parents' revenu fiscal de référence**, not his.
2. His declared income feeds the revenu brut global the CROUS uses to set a
   bourse échelon two years later — and even under the versement libératoire it
   raises his parents' tax through the taux effectif.
3. Under 20, a child stops counting as *à charge* for prestations familiales
   above 55 % of the SMIC.

None of these is a reason not to proceed. All three are reasons to tell his
parents before filing, not after.

## Re-check before acting

Every figure here is a 2026 figure. The ACRE rate alone moved twice inside 2026.
Nothing is published for 2027. **Re-run this the month before he files**, and
treat the cotisation rate, the ACRE rate, the TVA thresholds and the e-invoicing
calendar as the four most likely to have moved.
