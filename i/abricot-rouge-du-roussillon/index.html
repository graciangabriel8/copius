Roussillon red apricot

Abricots rouges du Roussillon

Latin namePrunus armeniaca 'Rouge du Roussillon'
FamilyFruits
OriginRoussillon, Pyrénées-Orientales
Season (France)June – August
FlavourSweet · Fruity · Mild
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

The appellation admits four varieties sharing one look, an orange ground speckled bright red: the old Rouge du Roussillon population and its clone A157, Aviéra, Avikandi and Royal Roussillon. Fruit is picked by hand at a set colour stage, must reach the packing station within 12 hours and be shipped within 6 days of picking. Small (35–55 mm), above 12° Brix and low in acid, they are supple and juicy rather than firm, which is why they bruise easily.

In the kitchen

Keep them out of the fridge and eat them within a day or two. For jam or a tart, add lemon juice: the low acidity that makes them taste sweet also leaves a jam flat and slow to set.
