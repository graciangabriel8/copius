Goose foie gras ◆

foie gras d’oie

Latin nameAnser anser (fattened liver)
FamilyMeat
OriginSouth-West France and Alsace
SeasonNovember – January
Flavourrich · buttery · delicate · mild
Typical price80–140 €/kg

What it is

Goose liver is paler, larger and finer-grained than duck: its fat cells are smaller, so it renders almost nothing in the pan or the terrine where a duck liver loses a fifth of itself. That structure is why it was the Alsatian and Périgord terrine liver long before duck took the market on price.

In the kitchen

Bring it to 18 °C before deveining — cold it shatters, warm it smears. For a terrine cook to a core of 52–55 °C and no further; past 58 °C even a goose liver breaks and floods the mould with fat.

Goes with

Fig Quince Black pepper Fleur de sel de Guérande Pear Vin de paille
