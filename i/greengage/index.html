Greengage

Reine-claude

FamilyFruits
Season (France)August – September
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

Named in French for Claude of France, queen to François I, and in English after Sir William Gage, who received the trees with the labels lost. Two countries named the same fruit after two different people, and both names stuck.

In the kitchen

Ignore the colour. A ripe greengage stays green — judge it by give under the thumb and by scent alone.
