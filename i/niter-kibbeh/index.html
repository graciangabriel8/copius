Niter kibbeh

Niter kibbeh (beurre clarifié épicé éthiopien)

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)25–45 €/kg

What it is

The butter is simmered rather than merely melted, with korarima, koseret and besobela — three Ethiopian aromatics with no real substitute — until the water has gone and the spices have given their oil up to the fat. What is strained off keeps for months at room temperature, which is the whole point in a kitchen without refrigeration.

In the kitchen

Start the wot with it rather than finishing with it — the flavour is fat-soluble and needs those first minutes with the onions. One tablespoon per two onions is the working ratio.
