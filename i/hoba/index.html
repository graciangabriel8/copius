Hōba (magnolia leaf) ✦

Feuille de magnolia (hōba)

Latin nameMagnolia obovata
FamilyHerbs
OriginHida, Gifu prefecture
SeasonAll year
Flavourwoody · smoky · resinous
Typical price1–3 €/feuille

What it is

The leaves reach forty centimetres, the largest of any Japanese tree, and Hida farmhouses used them as plate, wrapper and cooking vessel long before hōba-miso appeared on a restaurant menu. They are gathered in autumn and used dried the following year: a fresh leaf holds too much water to scent anything and merely steams.

In the kitchen

Soak the dried leaf ten minutes and pat it dry before it goes over the coals, or it chars through and drops the miso into the fire. Sweeten the miso with sake and mirin and pull it the moment it bubbles at the rim — the leaf gives its camphor note in the last minute, not the first.

Goes with

Miso Negi Shiitake Beef Junmai sake Hon-mirin
