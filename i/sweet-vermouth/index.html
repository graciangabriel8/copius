Sweet red vermouth

Vermouth rouge

Latin nameArtemisia absinthium
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginTurin, Piedmont
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Bitter · Warm · Herbal
Typical price (France)10–20 €/L

What it is

Antonio Benedetto Carpano is credited with the first sweet vermouth, made in Turin in 1786 by sweetening and spicing a white wine for a court that found wormwood tonics too austere. The German root of the name — Wermut, wormwood — survives even though the modern bottle leans on cinchona bark, vanilla and burnt-sugar caramel for its bitterness and its colour.

In the kitchen

Add it at the start of a braise and let it reduce with the stock; poured over a finished sauce it tastes raw and medicinal. Once open it oxidises like any wine — refrigerate and finish it within a month.
