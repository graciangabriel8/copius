Radicchio

Trévise

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)October – February
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

The long Treviso variety is forced in spring water drawn from underground, which keeps it at a constant temperature while the outer leaves are stripped away — a process closer to a spa treatment than farming, and it takes weeks.

In the kitchen

Grill or roast it. Heat and a little sugar or balsamic turn the bitterness into something closer to caramel.
