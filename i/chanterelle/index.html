Chanterelle

Girolle

Latin nameCantharellus cibarius
FamilyMushrooms
OriginOld-growth woodlands
Season (France)June – October
FlavourFruity · Peppery · Delicate
Typical price (France)20–40 €/kg

What it is

Golden trumpets that smell faintly of apricot, chanterelles have never been farmed — they live in quiet partnership with tree roots. Foragers speak of their spots in whispers, if at all.

In the kitchen

Never soak them — brush them clean. Butter, a little cream, and they need nothing else.
