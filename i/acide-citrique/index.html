Citric acid

Acide citrique

Latin nameCitric acid (E330)
FamilyTexture agents
OriginAspergillus niger fermentation
Season (France)All year
FlavourSour · Tangy · Citrusy
Typical price (France)8–15 €/kg

What it is

Carl Wilhelm Scheele crystallised it out of lemon juice in 1784, and for more than a century the world's supply came from Italian lemons held by a growers' cartel. Then a Pfizer chemist, James Currie, found that the mould Aspergillus niger fed on sugar would excrete it by the vat, and the lemon monopoly was finished within a few years.

In the kitchen

One gram in a litre gives roughly the acidity of 20 ml of lemon juice, without the water or the peel oil — you weigh it in for precision, not economy. Dissolve it in a spoon of water and add it off the heat: sprinkled dry into a hot purée it makes local hot spots of acid that curdle dairy and split a sauce.
