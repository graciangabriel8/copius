Fromage blanc

Fromage blanc

Latin nameCaillé frais
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginFrance
Season (France)All year
FlavourTangy · Milky · Fresh · Delicate
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

Fresh curd drained but never aged, never salted, never ripened — a cheese caught at the first hour of its life. In its loosest form, faisselle, it is sold still dripping in the perforated pot it drained in, and eaten with nothing but sugar or a little cream.

In the kitchen

Whip it with a fork before serving and it lightens completely. Sweet with honey, savoury with shallot and herbs — it goes either way.

Its 18 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
