Dry sausage

Saucisson sec

Latin nameSalsicium siccum
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginFrance
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Rich · Meaty
Typical price (France)18–32 €/kg

What it is

The white bloom on the casing is a deliberately introduced mould, Penicillium, not a fault — it keeps unwanted moulds off and regulates drying. Lyon and the Ardèche each claim to make the best, and neither will concede.

In the kitchen

Peel the casing only on the piece you are cutting. Stripped entirely, the whole sausage dries out within a day.

Goes with
