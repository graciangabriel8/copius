Red onion

Oignon rouge

Latin nameAllium cepa var. cepa
FamilyVegetables
OriginCentral Asia
SeasonJuly – October
Flavoursweet · pungent · fresh · mild
Typical price2–4 €/kg

What it is

Milder and sweeter than a yellow onion because it carries less of the sulphur compound that makes you cry, which is why it is the one eaten raw. Its purple comes from the same anthocyanins as red cabbage, and it bleeds them into vinegar within minutes.

In the kitchen

Soak raw slices in cold water for ten minutes to pull the harshness out, or pickle them briefly in vinegar — they turn bright pink.

Goes with

Tomato Feta Olive oil Cucumber Balsamic vinegar Olive Lime Cilantro / Coriander
