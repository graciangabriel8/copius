Earth chestnut ✦

Châtaigne de terre

Latin nameBunium bulbocastanum
FamilyVegetables
OriginEuropean hedgerows and field margins
Season (France)April – June, September – October
FlavourNutty · Sweet · Earthy
Typical price (France)30–60 €/kg

What it is

The tuber forms a hand's depth down, on a stem no thicker than a thread, so it cannot be pulled — it has to be traced from the flowering stalk and dug out whole. That single fact is why a root tasting of raw hazelnut and chestnut has been foraged across Europe for centuries and never became a field crop.

In the kitchen

Scrub rather than peel: the skin is thin and most of the nut flavour sits directly under it. Eat them raw and sliced into a salad, or give them five minutes in butter at the most — boiled, they go floury and the hazelnut disappears.
