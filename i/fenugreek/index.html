Fenugreek

Fenugrec

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

It is the smell of commercial curry powder and of maple syrup imitation both — the compound responsible, sotolon, is the same. People who eat a lot of it genuinely smell of maple, which has caused more than one unnecessary medical consultation.

In the kitchen

Toast it barely, and stop before it darkens. A second too long and the bitterness becomes the only thing you taste.
