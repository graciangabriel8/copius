Bottarga

Poutargue

Latin nameMugil cephalus (roe)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginSardinia & Provence
SeasonAll year
Flavourbriny · umami · salty · rich
Typical price200–350 €/kg

What it is

Mullet roe salted, pressed and air-dried into an amber ingot — a technique the Phoenicians carried across the Mediterranean three thousand years ago. Sardinia grates its “Mediterranean caviar” over pasta; Martigues, near Marseille, guards its own as le caviar provençal.

In the kitchen

Shave it paper-thin or grate it at the very end, never cooked. With lemon, olive oil and raw celery, it needs exactly nothing else.

Goes with

Lemon Olive oil Garlic Celery Egg
