Iberian pork (bellota) ◆

Porc ibérique de bellota

Latin nameSus scrofa domesticus
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginDehesa, Extremadura and Andalusia
SeasonDecember – March
FlavourRich · Nutty · Meaty · Sweet
Typical price30–55 €/kg

What it is

Between October and March the pigs are turned loose on the dehesa for the montanera and eat several kilos of acorns a day; the oleic acid from the acorns goes straight into their fat, which is why it glosses and softens at room temperature. Almost every animal is destined to become ham — the fresh cuts sold as presa, secreto and pluma are simply what is left of the carcass once the four legs have gone to the drying loft.

In the kitchen

Cook it like beef, not like pork: sear hard and serve it pink at around 58 °C, because the fat is intramuscular and rendering it out leaves the muscle dry. Salt only after searing — salt applied early draws water to the surface and kills the crust.
