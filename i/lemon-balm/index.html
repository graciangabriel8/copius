Lemon balm

Mélisse

Latin nameMelissa officinalis
FamilyHerbs
OriginSouthern Europe
SeasonMay – September
FlavourCitrusy · Fresh · Mild
Typical price2–4 €/pot

What it is

Melissa is Greek for honey bee: beekeepers rubbed the crushed leaf inside a new hive to persuade a swarm to settle in it. Carmelite friars in Paris were distilling it as eau de mélisse des Carmes from 1611, and something close to the same formula is still sold in French pharmacies.

In the kitchen

Its lemon note collapses on heat, so infuse it cold — a handful in a jug of water or cream, a few hours in the fridge, beats any hot infusion. Bruise it, do not chop: cut edges blacken and go grassy within minutes.
