Summer truffle

Truffe d’été

FamilyMushrooms
Season (France)May – August
Typical price (France)120–300 €/kg

What it is

A fraction of the price of the winter black truffle and a fraction of the perfume — the flesh is pale hazelnut rather than dark, and the aroma is faint. It is honest, but it is not a substitute, and menus that blur the two are being careless.

In the kitchen

Do not cook it and do not pay winter prices. Shave it raw over something warm and fatty at the last moment.
