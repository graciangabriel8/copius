Mammola artichoke

Artichaut mammola

Latin nameCynara cardunculus var. scolymus
FamilyVegetables
OriginRoman countryside, Lazio
SeasonFebruary – May
FlavourEarthy · Bitter · Nutty
Typical price2–4 €/pièce

What it is

In Rome the crown head of the plant is the cimarolo, cut first and sold as the mammola: round, spineless, and with a choke so young it barely exists. The side buds that follow are the same plant and a different vegetable — they go to the pan, never whole into the fryer.

In the kitchen

For alla giudia, fry twice: once at 130 °C until a knife slides through the base, then hot at 180 °C, pressing the head down so the leaves open into a flower and crisp. One frying gives you either a raw heart or burnt leaves.
