Crosnes ✦

Crosne

Latin nameStachys affinis
FamilyVegetables
OriginChina & Japan
SeasonNovember – February
Flavournutty · delicate · sweet · fresh
Typical price14–25 €/kg

What it is

Little pearly tubers shaped like Michelin men, named after the village of Crosne where a French agronomist first planted them in the 1880s after bringing them back from Asia. Restaurant kitchens adore and curse them equally: exquisite crunch, maddening to clean.

In the kitchen

Don’t peel — shake them in a tea towel with coarse salt to strip the skins, then glaze them in butter barely two minutes. They should still snap.

Goes with

Butter Parsley Cream Chicken
