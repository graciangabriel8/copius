Bamboo shoot

Pousse de bambou

Latin nameBambusoideae spp.
FamilyVegetables
OriginEast & Southeast Asia
SeasonMarch – May
Flavourmild · earthy · fresh · umami
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

Dug in spring before the shoot breaks ground, because it grows fast enough to be measured hourly and turns woody within days. Fresh shoots contain cyanogenic compounds and must be boiled — one of the few vegetables that is genuinely toxic raw.

In the kitchen

Tinned shoots keep a metallic edge. Boil them for five minutes in fresh water before use and it goes.

Goes with

Soy sauce Ginger Sesame Shiitake Chili pepper Spring onion Garlic Fish sauce
