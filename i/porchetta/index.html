Porchetta

porchetta

Latin nameSus scrofa (boned middle, roasted)
FamilyMeat
OriginAriccia, Lazio
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · herbal · meaty · toasty
Typical price18–30 €/kg

What it is

Ariccia, in the hills south of Rome, held its first porchetta festival in 1950 and took an IGP in 2011 for a whole boned pig roasted with rosemary, garlic and pepper. It is charcuterie rather than a roast dinner: sold by weight from a counter or a van, carved cold, eaten in a roll.

In the kitchen

Dry the skin uncovered in the fridge overnight and salt it only at the last minute — salt draws moisture, and wet skin goes leathery instead of blistering. Finish at 240 °C for the last twenty minutes.

Goes with

Fennel Rosemary Garlic Black pepper Lemon Wild fennel fronds
