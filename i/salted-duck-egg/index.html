Salted duck egg

Œuf de cane salé

Latin nameAnas platyrhynchos domesticus
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginGaoyou, Jiangsu, China
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Rich · Umami · Buttery
Typical price (France)1–2 €/pièce

What it is

Twenty to forty days in strong brine does something to the yolk alone: salt breaks the low-density lipoproteins that held its fat emulsified, so the fat pools and the yolk sets firm and orange while the white stays raw and fiercely salty. A bead of oil in the cut yolk is the mark of one properly cured.

In the kitchen

Kitchens buy them for the yolk and throw most of the white away. Steam the raw yolks ten minutes with a splash of rice wine to drive off any eggy smell, then mash them into butter or crumble them over fried food — they are a fat and a seasoning, not an egg.
