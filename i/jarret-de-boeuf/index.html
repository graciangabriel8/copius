Beef shin

Jarret de bœuf

Latin nameBœuf — jarret
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginFrance
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Meaty · Umami · Earthy
Typical price (France)9–14 €/kg

What it is

The hardest-working muscle on the animal, and therefore the most collagen-rich — which is exactly why it makes the best braise and the best stock. Cut across the bone it becomes osso buco, though Milan uses veal.

In the kitchen

Three hours minimum, and it is better the next day. Rushing it produces the toughest meat in the butcher's shop.

Its 8 pairings are in the full version (€4.90 a month or €39 a year).
