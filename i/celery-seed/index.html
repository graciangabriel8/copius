Celery seed

Graine de céleri

Latin nameApium graveolens
FamilySpices
OriginMediterranean
SeasonAll year
Flavourbitter · earthy · herbal · warm
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

It comes from smallage, the wild ancestor of celery, and it is startlingly concentrated — a quarter-teaspoon carries more celery flavour than a whole stick. Ground with salt it becomes celery salt, which is the entire argument of a Bloody Mary.

In the kitchen

It turns bitter fast if you overdo it. Start with half what you think, especially in a coleslaw or a court-bouillon.

Goes with
