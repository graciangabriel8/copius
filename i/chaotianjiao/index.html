Facing-heaven chilli

Piment chaotianjiao

Latin nameCapsicum annuum
FamilySpices
OriginSichuan and Hunan
SeasonAll year
Flavourhot · citrus · pungent
Typical price25–50 €/kg

What it is

Facing-heaven chillies grow pointing upward instead of hanging, which is exactly what the name records; the pods are short, thick-skinned and land somewhere around 50,000 to 75,000 Scoville. In a Sichuan kitchen they mostly go whole into hot oil rather than into a grinder, because the aroma comes off the skin and the seeds stay shut inside.

In the kitchen

Drop them whole into oil at about 150°C for a few seconds, until they darken one shade, then add the meat; they are there to perfume the oil and are not meant to be eaten. Snip them open beforehand only if you actually want the heat released.
