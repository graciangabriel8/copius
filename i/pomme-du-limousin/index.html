Limousin apple

Pomme du Limousin

Latin nameMalus domestica 'Golden Delicious'
FamilyFruits
OriginLimousin plateaux: Corrèze, Creuse, Dordogne, Haute-Vienne
Season (France)September – July
FlavourSweet · Tangy · Fruity
Typical price (France)4–5 €/kg

What it is

Golden Delicious, grown in the Limousin since the 1950s on crystalline plateaux, in orchards generally 350 to 450 m up. Cold nights and warm, sunny days there build both sugar and acid, so the appellation demands at least 12.5 °Brix, 3.7 g/L of malic acid and a firmness of 5 kg/cm², and the fruit takes a pink blush. Picked by hand, never treated after harvest and held in controlled atmosphere, it keeps its crunch until the appellation lapses, between 1 June and 1 August depending on colour.

In the kitchen

Its firm, acid-backed flesh holds its shape in a tart or sautéed in butter; cook it longer for a compote. Keep it in the fridge crisper: it has come out of cold store and softens fast in a warm fruit bowl.
