Lobster ◆

Homard

Latin nameHomarus gammarus
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginNorth Atlantic; Brittany prizes the blue
SeasonMay – September
FlavourSweet · Marine · Rich · Delicate
Typical price45–80 €/kg

What it is

Colonial New England fed lobster to prisoners and servants — the story goes that some contracts capped it at three meals a week. The rails and the rich reversed its fortune entirely; Brittany’s cobalt-shelled homard bleu now outranks nearly everything else in the tank.

In the kitchen

Undercook rather than over — the flesh turns to eraser past its point. Roast the shells for bisque, and dare the old Senderens pairing: a whisper of vanilla in the butter.
