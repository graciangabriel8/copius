Shad roe ✦

Œufs d’alose

Latin nameAlosa spp. (roe)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginAtlantic rivers
Season (France)March – May
FlavourRich · Meaty · Mild · Earthy
Typical price (France)20–40 €/kg

What it is

Shad run up rivers to spawn each spring, and the roe comes out as a pair of lobes joined by a membrane — sold, priced and cooked as one piece. John McPhee called it America’s founding fish for how much of it the colonies ate; in France the same run once fed every town along the Loire and the Gironde.

In the kitchen

Prick the membrane in a few places or the lobes burst in the pan. Six to eight minutes over gentle heat in butter or bacon fat, turned once — pushed further it goes chalky and there is no way back.
