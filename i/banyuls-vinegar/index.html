Banyuls vinegar

Vinaigre de Banyuls

Latin nameVinaigre de Banyuls
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginRoussillon, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourSour · Sweet · Nutty · Rich
Typical price (France)14–26 €/L

What it is

Made from Banyuls, a fortified sweet wine from the terraced vineyards above the Mediterranean, and aged in oak barrels left outdoors through the seasons. The result is closer to sherry vinegar than to any French one.

In the kitchen

Its sweetness lets it stand alone — a spoonful on a warm salad of duck or foie gras needs no oil at all.

Its 9 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
