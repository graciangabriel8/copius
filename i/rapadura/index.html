Rapadura

Rapadura

Latin nameSaccharum officinarum
FamilySweet pantry
OriginBrazil & Latin America
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Rich · Sweet · Smoky
Typical price (France)8–15 €/kg

What it is

Whole cane juice boiled down and left to set in a block, then scraped — rapar means to scrape. It is called panela in Colombia, piloncillo in Mexico, jaggery in India: the same product with five names and no refining at any point.

In the kitchen

It does not dissolve like white sugar. Melt it in a little hot liquid first, or you get gritty pockets.
