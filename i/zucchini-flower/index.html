Zucchini flower

Fleur de courgette

Latin nameCucurbita pepo
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean kitchen gardens
SeasonJune – September
Flavourdelicate · mild · grassy
Typical price0,50–1 €/pièce

What it is

The flower opens at dawn and has closed for good by noon, which is why it reaches the market picked wet at first light and never survives a second day. Male flowers arrive on a bare stem — they were never going to become anything — while the female comes attached to a finger of fruit, and the two are sold at different prices for exactly that reason.

In the kitchen

Pull out the stamen or pistil from inside: it is bitter and it holds water that will blow a batter open. Never wash them, brush them; and fry at 180 °C so the petal sets before the filling has time to warm and run.

Goes with

Ricotta Mozzarella di bufala campana Anchovy Basil Olive oil Lemon Parmesan Mint
