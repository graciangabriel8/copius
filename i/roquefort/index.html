Roquefort

Latin namePenicillium roqueforti
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginRoquefort-sur-Soulzon, Aveyron
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Rich · Pungent
Typical price (France)28–45 €/kg

What it is

It ripens only in the Combalou caves, where a collapsed mountain left natural chimneys called fleurines that breathe damp air through the rock. In 1925 it became the first French cheese granted an appellation — the law arrived to protect a draught.

In the kitchen

Never cook it hard. Melt it off the heat into warm cream, or it turns bitter and grainy.
