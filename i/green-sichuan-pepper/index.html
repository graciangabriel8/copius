Green Sichuan pepper

Poivre de Sichuan vert

Latin nameZanthoxylum schinifolium
FamilySpices
OriginSichuan & Chongqing
Season (France)All year
FlavourTingling · Citrusy · Fresh · Herbal
Typical price (France)150–300 €/kg

What it is

A different species from the red huajiao, picked while the husks are still green. The tingle comes from hydroxy-alpha-sanshool, which does not act on taste at all: it fires the mouth’s touch receptors at around fifty vibrations a second, so what you register is a frequency rather than a flavour.

In the kitchen

Heat kills it. Warm the husks in oil at about 100 °C for a minute, strain, and use the green oil cold over noodles or poached chicken — scorched, it stops tingling and turns bitter.
