Époisses

Latin nameÉpoisses AOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginCôte-d’Or, Burgundy
Season (France)All year
FlavourPungent · Creamy · Salty · Rich
Typical price (France)7–11 €/pièce

What it is

Washed with Marc de Bourgogne three times a week for its whole ripening, which is why it smells the way it does and why its rind glows brick orange. Cistercian monks devised it in the sixteenth century; by the 1950s only two producers were left, and it nearly vanished.

In the kitchen

Serve it in its wooden box with a spoon. At full ripeness it has no structure to cut, and a knife just pushes it around.

Its 9 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
