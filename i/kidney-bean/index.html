Kidney bean

Haricot rouge

Latin namePhaseolus vulgaris
FamilyPulses
OriginPeru & Central America
SeasonAll year
Flavourearthy · sweet · creamy · mild
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

Raw or undercooked, it is genuinely poisonous — it carries phytohaemagglutinin, and as few as four raw beans can make you seriously ill. Ten minutes at a hard boil destroys it, but a slow cooker never gets hot enough on its own.

In the kitchen

Boil hard for ten full minutes before any slow cooking. This is the one bean where the instruction is a safety rule, not a preference.

Goes with

Chili pepper Cumin Onion Tomato Garlic Paprika Rice Cilantro / Coriander
