Crème fraîche

Latin nameCrème fraîche d’Isigny AOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginNormandy, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourCreamy · Tangy · Rich · Milky
Typical price (France)4–8 €/kg

What it is

Cream left to ripen with its own lactic bacteria until it thickens and turns faintly sour. That acidity is the whole trick: it holds the fat together, so crème fraîche can boil in a sauce without splitting where ordinary cream curdles.

In the kitchen

It is the safe cream for anything acidic — lemon, wine, tomato. Add ordinary cream to those and it will split.
