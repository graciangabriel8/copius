Guinea fowl

Pintade

Latin nameNumida meleagris
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginWest Africa
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Earthy · Delicate · Umami
Typical price (France)10–16 €/kg

What it is

Domesticated in West Africa and kept by the Greeks and Romans, who called it the Numidian hen. It sits between chicken and pheasant — slightly gamey, considerably leaner, and it has a reputation for drying out that is entirely deserved.

In the kitchen

Bard it with fat or cook it under a lid with liquid. Roasted naked like a chicken it will disappoint every time.
