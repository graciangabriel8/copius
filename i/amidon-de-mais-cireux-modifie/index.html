Instant modified waxy maize starch

Amidon de maïs cireux modifié

FamilyTexture agents
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

A waxy maize mutant collected in China in 1908 gave a starch that is almost pure amylopectin — no amylose, so nothing that can recrystallise and weep. American mills scaled it when the Pacific war cut off tapioca, and the pre-gelatinised grade, cooked and drum-dried before it is ever sold, is what thickens a cold fruit coulis in thirty seconds.

In the kitchen

Whisk it into the sugar or the dry ingredients before it meets liquid; dropped straight into water it seizes into lumps no blender will open. One to two percent of the liquid weight gives a nappé, three a paste.
