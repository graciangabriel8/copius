Signature dish

Aubergine with buttermilk sauce

Yotam Ottolenghi · 1968 · London — born in Jerusalem

Roast aubergine halves spooned with a buttermilk and yoghurt sauce and scattered with za'atar and pomegranate seeds, from Ottolenghi's 2010 vegetable book Plenty.

Why it works

Roasting collapses the aubergine's air-filled flesh so it drinks up the olive oil, and the lactic acid in the buttermilk and yoghurt cuts that oil on the palate.

What is in it

Eggplant Buttermilk Pomegranate Za’atar Lemon thyme
