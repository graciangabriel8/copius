Thyme

Thym

Latin nameThymus vulgaris
FamilyHerbs
OriginMediterranean hills
Season (France)All year
FlavourHerbal · Woody · Warm
Typical price (France)1,50–3 €/botte

What it is

Medieval ladies embroidered a bee hovering over thyme on the scarves of their knights — the herb meant courage. Egyptians used it for embalming; cooks use it for nearly everything that simmers.

In the kitchen

Strip the leaves by sliding your fingers down the stem against the grain. Whole sprigs can simmer and be fished out like bay leaves.
