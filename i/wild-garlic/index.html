Wild garlic

Ail des ours

Latin nameAllium ursinum
FamilyHerbs
OriginEuropean woodlands
Season (France)March – May
FlavourPungent · Green · Fresh
Typical price (France)10–25 €/kg

What it is

“Bear’s garlic” in half of Europe’s languages: legend says bears eat it first thing after hibernation to wake their blood. For a few weeks each spring, whole forests smell of garlic, and foragers fill baskets with the leaves — the kitchen’s brief, green thunderclap.

In the kitchen

Beware its deadly lookalike, lily of the valley: crush a leaf — no garlic smell, no picking. Blitzed with pine nuts and parmesan it makes spring’s definitive pesto.
