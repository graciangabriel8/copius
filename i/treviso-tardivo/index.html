Treviso tardivo

Trévise tardive

Latin nameCichorium intybus
FamilyVegetables
OriginVeneto, Italy
Season (France)November – February
FlavourBitter · Fresh · Earthy
Typical price (France)12–20 €/kg

What it is

Tardivo is less harvested than re-grown: after the first frosts the plants are lifted and stood root-deep in running spring water at around 13 °C in the dark, and three weeks later pale new leaves have pushed out and curled into claws. Everything green is then stripped away, so what leaves the field is several times what reaches the stall.

In the kitchen

Split the heads lengthwise through the root so the claws stay attached, then grill or braise them cut-side down until the white rib chars — raw, that rib gives crunch and nothing else. Finish with a spoonful of vinegar or orange juice, which pulls the bitterness back into range.
