Dextrose

Dextrose

Latin nameZea mays
FamilySweet
OriginMaize starch hydrolysis
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · mild
Typical price5–10 €/kg

What it is

Dextrose is glucose in crystal form, and its molecule weighs 180 against sucrose's 342 - a gram of it puts nearly twice as many particles into the water. Freezing point depression counts particles rather than sweetness, which is why a sorbet maker reaches for it.

In the kitchen

Replace fifteen to twenty percent of a sorbet's sucrose with dextrose and the scoop softens without getting sweeter - it sweetens at about 70 against sugar's 100, with roughly twice the effect on the freezing point. Past a quarter of the sugars it stops setting at all.
