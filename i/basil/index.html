Basil

Basilic

Latin nameOcimum basilicum
FamilyHerbs
OriginIndia
SeasonJune – September
FlavourHerbal · Anise-like · Peppery · Fresh
Typical price2–4 €/pot

What it is

Its name comes from basileus — Greek for king — and in India its cousin tulsi is holy enough to be planted in temple courtyards. A royal and sacred plant that ended up, gloriously, on tomatoes.

In the kitchen

Heat murders it: tear the leaves by hand and add them off the flame, at the very last moment.
