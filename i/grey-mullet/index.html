What it is

The word poutargue travels back through Arabic butarkha to a Greek compound meaning salted fish eggs, and the roe it names is this fish's — Martigues has pressed and waxed grey mullet roe since at least the Middle Ages. The animal that carries it sells for a fraction of the price of what it is transporting.

In the kitchen

Skin it and leave the fillets uncovered on ice overnight — the muddy note lives in the skin and the fat directly beneath it, and a night of air changes the fish completely. Failing that, salt the flesh for 20 minutes and rinse.
