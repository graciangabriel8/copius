Hon-mirin

Mirin (hon-mirin)

Latin nameOryza sativa var. glutinosa
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginJapan
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Mild · Rich
Typical price (France)16–30 €/L

What it is

Real mirin is a liquor: glutinous rice and koji left to sweeten in shochu until the whole thing sits at about 14 % alcohol, and Japan taxes it as one. That tax is why supermarket shelves carry mirin-fu chomiryo, a sweetened imitation held under one per cent, and why the genuine bottle stands with the sake.

In the kitchen

Boil it thirty seconds to burn the alcohol off before it goes into a dressing that will not be cooked further. In a glaze it goes in last: the glucose colours fast and will be bitter before the fish is done if the pan is above medium.
