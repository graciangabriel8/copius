Glutinous rice

Riz gluant

Latin nameOryza sativa var. glutinosa
FamilyGrains & flours
OriginLaos, Thailand, Japan
SeasonAll year
FlavourSweet · Milky · Mild
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

Glutinous rice has nothing to do with gluten - the name records a waxy starch with essentially no amylose, which is what makes the cooked grains cling into a single mass. It is also why the grain is steamed above water rather than boiled in it.

In the kitchen

Soak four hours at the least, overnight for preference, then steam twenty-five to thirty minutes in a cloth-lined basket. Boil it and you get glue, not rice.
