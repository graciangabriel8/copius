Red grouse ◆

Grouse d’Écosse

Latin nameLagopus lagopus scotica
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginHeather moorland, Scotland and northern England
SeasonAugust – December
FlavourBitter · Earthy · Smoky · Meaty
Typical price30–60 €/pièce

What it is

Red grouse has never been reared in captivity, so every bird on a menu was shot wild on managed heather moor. The season opens on 12 August by statute — the earliest game of the British year — and it is the bird's year-round diet of heather shoots that gives the flesh its almost bitter, peat-edged flavour.

In the kitchen

Roast the crown at 230 °C for twelve minutes and no longer; the breast wants to come out at 52 °C, and the legs are sinewy enough to be worth taking off for a sauce or a toast. Serve it on its own fried crumbs and bread sauce — a strong garnish fights it and loses.
