# Signature dishes — proposal for the remaining chefs

**15 September 2026.** Stage 3 of the dishes plan. Gabriel reviews the writing;
nothing here is in `js/data-chefs.js` yet.

## What ran

68 chefs. 29 already carry a dish. Eight are excluded because they are in the
atlas for a book or a system rather than a plate — Taillevent, La Varenne,
Scappi, Gouffé, Montagné, and Vatel, Menon and Dubois on the same reasoning.
That left **31 to plan**.

Eleven agents researched roughly three chefs each, then four auditors re-opened
every cited page and tried to refute the claim rather than confirm it. The brief
made `found: false` an explicitly correct answer.

No proposal was found to name the wrong chef. The failures are all about
evidence quality, which is the cheaper kind to fix.

## Ready — the source carries the claim (15)

Each was checked twice: the researcher opened a page and quoted a sentence, and

### aizpitarte — Tocino del cielo (candied egg yolk on a caramelised macaron)

*Tocino del cielo (jaune d'œuf confit sur macaron caramélisé)* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** An Andalusian dessert of egg yolk set in sugar syrup, served at Le Chateaubriand as a single caramelised bite sitting on a macaron base.

**Why it works.** The concentrated sugar syrup binds the free water and raises the temperature at which the yolk's proteins coagulate, so the yolk sets into a dense, smooth custard rather than curdling into grains.

Ingredients: `egg`, `sugar`, `egg`  · unmapped: water

Source: [https://www.konbini.com/lifestyle/comment-ce-restaurant-parisien-a-cha](https://www.konbini.com/lifestyle/comment-ce-restaurant-parisien-a-change-pour-toujours-le-cours-de-la-gastronomie/)

### alleno — Crisp langoustine tart under oscietra caviar

*Tarte friande de langoustine recouverte de grains de caviar Osciètre* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** A thin crisp pastry tart of langoustine finished with a layer of oscietra caviar spread over it with a spatula.

**Why it works.** The salt of the dish comes from the caviar's own brine, released as the grains burst on the palate and seasoning the langoustine from above, while the baked pastry underneath stays dry and supplies the only crisp texture against two soft layers.

Ingredients: `langoustine`, `caviar`, `butter`  · unmapped: puff pastry

Source: [https://www.finedininglovers.fr/experts/yannick-alleno](https://www.finedininglovers.fr/experts/yannick-alleno)

### bailey — Foie gras and grits

*Foie gras et gruau de maïs* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Seared foie gras over slow-cooked corn grits with a red-wine gravy, built from the Southern lunch-counter dish of beef liver and grits.

**Why it works.** Fat rendered from the searing foie gras emulsifies into the starch the slow-cooked corn has released, and the acid in the red-wine gravy cuts through that fat.

Ingredients: `foie-gras`, `butter`  · unmapped: grits, red wine

Source: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grey_(restaurant)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grey_(restaurant))

### bise — Gratin of crayfish tails

*Gratin de queues d'écrevisses* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Shelled crayfish tails bound in a cream sauce built on crayfish butter and browned in the dish, kept on the Auberge du Père Bise menu by later chefs.

**Why it works.** The pigments and aroma compounds of the crayfish shells are fat-soluble, so pounding them into butter and reducing them with cream extracts what water alone leaves in the shell, and the resulting fat-rich sauce browns under the grill instead of splitting.

Ingredients: `crayfish`, `butter`, `cream`, `shallot`  · unmapped: white wine

Source: [https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/jean-sulpice-en-5-plats](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/jean-sulpice-en-5-plats)

### crenn — Kir Breton

*Kir breton* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** An edible version of the Breton aperitif served as the first bite at Atelier Crenn: a thin white chocolate and cocoa butter sphere filled with apple cider and topped with crème de cassis.

**Why it works.** Cocoa butter melts just below body temperature, so the shell gives way on the tongue instead of being chewed and releases the cider it held as liquid in a single burst.

Ingredients: `apple`, `white-chocolate`, `cocoa-butter`, `blackcurrant`

Source: [https://www.theworlds50best.com/northamerica/en/the-list/atelier-crenn](https://www.theworlds50best.com/northamerica/en/the-list/atelier-crenn.html)

### darroze — Lobster with tandoori spices, carrot, citrus and coriander

*Homard aux épices tandoori, mousseline de carottes aux agrumes et coriandre fraîche* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Lobster poached and glazed in a butter sauce spiked with tandoori spices, set against a carrot and candied-citrus purée with fresh coriander; it began as a scallop dish Darroze built after a trip to India and has stayed on the Connaught menu in some form for years.

**Why it works.** The aroma compounds of the tandoori blend are fat-soluble, so infusing them into the butter sauce fixes them onto the lean lobster meat, while the acid of the citrus stops the carrot purée's sugar from flattening the shellfish's own sweetness.

Ingredients: `lobster`, `carrot`  · unmapped: tandoori spices, citrus, coriander

Source: [https://eatweekguide.com/helene-darroze-at-the-connaught/](https://eatweekguide.com/helene-darroze-at-the-connaught/)

### gagnaire — Chocolate soufflé biscuit

*Biscuit soufflé au chocolat* · 1981 · `signature`

**What it is.** A chocolate soufflé biscuit Gagnaire adapted from the soufflé omelette in the Escoffier guide, and the only dish that has stayed on his menu since 1981.

**Why it works.** The beaten whites hold air bubbles whose water turns to steam in the oven and inflates the batter, while the egg proteins coagulate and the cocoa butter re-sets on cooling, fixing the foam in place.

Ingredients: `dark-chocolate`, `egg`, `egg`, `sugar`

Source: [https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/pierre-gagnaire-en-5-plats](https://fr.gaultmillau.com/fr/news/pierre-gagnaire-en-5-plats)

### hasegawa — Dentucky Fried Chicken

*Dentucky Fried Chicken* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** A deboned chicken wing stuffed with glutinous rice and a filling that changes with the season, fried and handed over in a red-and-white striped takeaway box printed with Hasegawa's face.

**Why it works.** Deboning presses the collagen-rich skin directly against the rice, so in the fryer the skin renders and dries into a crust while the enclosed rice steams in the wing's own moisture and stays soft.

Ingredients: `chicken`, `glutinous-rice`, `plum`, `black-sesame`, `daikon`

Source: [https://www.newsweek.com/den-restaurant-chef-zaiyu-hasegawa-tokyo-5306](https://www.newsweek.com/den-restaurant-chef-zaiyu-hasegawa-tokyo-530607)

### kofoed — Razor clams, minerals and sour cream

*Couteaux, minéraux et crème aigre* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** A tartare of diced razor clam bound with crème fraîche and lemon, served inside an edible baked dough 'shell' painted with charcoal and algae powder to look like the real one.

**Why it works.** The lemon juice and zest denature the proteins of the raw diced clam so the flesh firms without any heat, while the fat of the 38% crème fraîche coats the dice and slows the acid's action on them.

Ingredients: `razor-clam`, `creme-fraiche`, `lemon`, `parsley`  · unmapped: charcoal powder, algae powder

Source: [https://www.the50.com/stories/News/make-rasmus-kofoeds-razor-clam-from](https://www.the50.com/stories/News/make-rasmus-kofoeds-razor-clam-from-geranium-at-home.html)

### munk — Lamb heart tartare with blood sauce and an organ donor card

*Tartare de cœur d'agneau, sauce sang en poche de transfusion, carte de donneur d'organes* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Raw lamb heart cut as tartare and served in the opened heart, with a cherry-juice and chicken-stock reduction poured over it from a blood transfusion bag and an organ donor registration card set beside the plate; about 1,500 diners went on to register as donors.

**Why it works.** Heart is a lean, hard-working muscle whose high myoglobin content reads as metallic iron on the palate, so the acid and sugar of the cherry reduction mask those iron notes while the gelatine in the chicken stock gives the raw dice the lubrication its low fat content cannot.

Ingredients: `lamb`, `cherry`, `chicken`

Source: [https://robbreport.com/food-drink/dining/alchemist-restaurant-copenhag](https://robbreport.com/food-drink/dining/alchemist-restaurant-copenhagen-denmark-rasmus-munk-2856450/)

### ottolenghi — Aubergine with buttermilk sauce

*Aubergine rôtie, sauce au babeurre* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Roast aubergine halves spooned with a buttermilk and yoghurt sauce and scattered with za'atar and pomegranate seeds, from Ottolenghi's 2010 vegetable book Plenty.

**Why it works.** Roasting collapses the aubergine's air-filled flesh so it drinks up the olive oil, and the lactic acid in the buttermilk and yoghurt cuts that oil on the palate.

Ingredients: `eggplant`, `buttermilk`, `pomegranate`, `zaatar`, `lemon-thyme`  · unmapped: Greek yoghurt

Source: [https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/aubergine-with-buttermilk-sauce/](https://thehappyfoodie.co.uk/recipes/aubergine-with-buttermilk-sauce/)

### pic-andre — Pike boudin Richelieu with Nantua sauce

*Boudin de brochet Richelieu, sauce Nantua* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** André Pic's own version of the Lyon pike quenelle, rolled into a sausage instead of being shaped with two spoons and served under Nantua sauce.

**Why it works.** The lean pike proteins bind with egg and cream and set firm as they poach, while the crayfish butter in the Nantua sauce carries the shells' fat-soluble pigments and aromas into the béchamel, which water-based cooking would leave behind.

Ingredients: `pike`, `crayfish`, `butter`, `cream`, `egg`

Source: [https://www.produits-laitiers.com/les-plats-emblematiques-des-chefs-1-](https://www.produits-laitiers.com/les-plats-emblematiques-des-chefs-1-du-debut-du-xxe-siecle-aux-annees-1960/)

### roca — Apple and foie gras timbale with vanilla oil

*Timbale de pomme et foie gras à l'huile de vanille* · 1996 · `signature`

**What it is.** Alternating layers of caramelised apple and duck foie gras pressed into a timbale and dressed with vanilla-infused oil, on El Celler de Can Roca's menu since 1996 and formally given to the city of Girona in 2009.

**Why it works.** The malic acid of the caramelised apple cuts the fat film the foie gras leaves on the palate, while vanillin, being fat-soluble, dissolves into that same fat and carries its aroma through every layer.

Ingredients: `foie-gras`, `apple`, `vanilla`, `sugar`  · unmapped: oil

Source: [https://cellercanroca.com/los-hermanos-roca/roca-timeline/](https://cellercanroca.com/los-hermanos-roca/roca-timeline/)

### verge — Zucchini flower stuffed with black truffle (le poupeton)

*Poupeton de fleur de courgette aux truffes noires* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** A male zucchini flower stuffed with black truffle and mushrooms, gently cooked and served with a butter sauce built on mushroom fumet; the name comes from the bundled flower's resemblance to a doll.

**Why it works.** Truffle aroma compounds are fat-soluble, so the mushroom-fumet butter dissolves them and carries them through the flower's thin, almost tasteless petal wall, which survives the cooking because the flower steams in its own moisture rather than meeting direct heat.

Ingredients: `zucchini`, `truffle`, `mushroom`, `butter`

Source: [https://www.180c.fr/la-gazette/christophe-bacquie-la-fleur-de-courgett](https://www.180c.fr/la-gazette/christophe-bacquie-la-fleur-de-courgette/)

### veyrat — Disappearing pasta

*Pâtes disparaissantes* · — · `signature`

**What it is.** Noodles made with no flour and no egg — strips of melted Beaufort set firm — that dissolve into the broth poured over them at the table.

**Why it works.** The noodle is pure cheese and holds its shape only while cold: the hot broth takes it past the melting point of the milk fat, the casein network slackens, and the strip collapses into the liquid, which it salts and enriches as it goes.

Ingredients: `beaufort`, `saffron`, `mushroom`  · unmapped: vegetable broth

Source: [https://www.gillespudlowski.com/390122/restaurants/megeve-la-nouvelle-](https://www.gillespudlowski.com/390122/restaurants/megeve-la-nouvelle-jeunesse-de-marc-veyrat)

## Correctly found nothing (3)

These are not gaps to fill. Forcing a plate onto them would be the invention the
brief was written to prevent.

**ono** — No single dish is documented as Jiro Ono's signature. Sukiyabashi Jiro serves only a daily omakase of roughly twenty pieces of nigiri, all of them classic edomae items (akami, kohada, anago, tamagoyaki) that every edomae sushiya serves; the pages opened attribute to him a method and a standard of craft, not a named plate. The Tokyo Metropolitan Government's own article on the restaurant names dishes served there but identifies none as his. The tamagoyaki is widely discussed because of the ten-year apprenticeship shown in 'Jiro Dreams of Sushi', but it is a traditional preparation he did not create, so promoting it to a signature would be an invention. Sources opened: en.wikipedia.org/w/api.php extracts for 'Jiro Ono' and 'Sukiyabashi Jiro'; https://www.tokyoupdates.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/en/post-1581/ ; https://reportergourmet.com/en/chef/839-jiro-ono

**ros** — No opened source names a single dish as Ana Roš's signature, and the one source that addresses the question directly records her rejecting the concept: in a Stone Journal interview she explains why she does not believe in signature dishes and calls them irrelevant, since the menu at Hiša Franko is rebuilt around whatever the Soča valley yields that season. Her English Wikipedia entry names no dish at all. What is documented is signature INGREDIENTS — marble trout above all, which has never been off the menu — not a named plate. Candidates circulating in search-engine summaries (a corn beignet, a raw marble trout salad) did not survive opening the pages: neither the Food Perestroika account of the beignet nor the Accounting For Taste review contains the word 'signature' anywhere, so that framing came from the summariser, not from any source. Promoting either would be attributing a menu item, not a documented signature.

**atadika** — No single dish is documented as Selassie Atadika's signature. Every source I opened describes a philosophy and a format rather than a named plate: her own Midunu chef page states only "At Midunu, Selassie cooks with curiosity and restraint, allowing ingredients, place, and conversation to lead", and the TIME Earth Awards profile names no dish at all, describing her work through ingredients and sustainability principles. The two plausible candidates both fail. Garifoto is documented by Al Jazeera as her mother's Sunday recipe and her own quarantine comfort food, not a Midunu creation — the piece quotes her saying "In these times of uncertainty and in hopes of finding comfort, I find myself turning to mum's go-to garifoto." The Midunu chocolate truffles are a confectionery product line that grew out of the tasting menu's final course, not a dish. Attributing a signature dish here would mean stretching the New African Cuisine style into a plate that no source names.
