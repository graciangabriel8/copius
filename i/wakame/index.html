Wakame

Wakamé

Latin nameUndaria pinnatifida
FamilySeaweed
OriginCold coastal waters of Japan and Korea
SeasonFebruary – May
Flavourmarine · briny · delicate
Typical price45–90 €/kg

What it is

Wakame travels the world in ballast water and on hulls; it sits on the register of the hundred worst invasive species and has taken hold from the Venice lagoon to Tasmania. In Brittany, where it arrived uninvited, it is now grown deliberately on ropes.

In the kitchen

Dried, it swells six to ten times, so a tablespoon feeds two bowls of soup, and two minutes in cold water is enough — longer leaves it slack. Add it to miso soup off the heat: boiling pulls out the slime and turns it grey.

Goes with

Cucumber Miso Rice vinegar Sesame Silken tofu (kinugoshi) Ginger
