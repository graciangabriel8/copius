Usukuchi (light soy sauce)

Sauce soja claire (usukuchi)

Latin name薄口醤油
FamilyCondiments
OriginTatsuno, Hyogo
SeasonAll year
Flavoursalty · umami · delicate · mild
Typical price8–15 €/L

What it is

Brewed at Tatsuno in Hyogo from the seventeenth century for the clear dashi cooking of the Kansai, it ferments for less time, at a higher salt content, with a little amazake added - all of which hold the colour back. It is roughly two percentage points saltier than dark shoyu, so the pale bottle is the strong one.

In the kitchen

Swapping it for dark shoyu, use about a fifth less and correct at the end. Keep it for dishes where the colour of the broth is the dish - a clear suimono, simmered white turnip, chawanmushi.

Goes with

Kombu Katsuobushi Hon-mirin Daikon Junmai sake Rice Scallop Hakurei turnip
