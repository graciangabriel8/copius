Red wine vinegar

Vinaigre de vin rouge

Latin nameAcetum vini rubri
FamilyCondiments
OriginFrance & Italy
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · tangy · fruity · earthy
Typical price2–6 €/L

What it is

The word is a plain description — vin aigre, sour wine — and for most of history it was an accident rather than a product. Roman soldiers drank it diluted as posca, which is what was offered to Christ on the cross.

In the kitchen

Deglaze a pan with it rather than wine when you want the acidity to survive reduction. Wine loses its edge, vinegar keeps it.
