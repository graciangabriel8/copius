Pink peppercorn

Baies roses

Latin nameSchinus terebinthifolia
FamilySpices
OriginBrazil, grown on Réunion
Season (France)All year
FlavourFruity · Resinous · Floral · Sweet
Typical price (France)60–120 €/kg

What it is

Not a pepper but a cashew relative, which is why the FDA stopped imports in 1982, saying the berries could cause a severe toxic reaction. France disputed it on behalf of its Réunion growers and the FDA answered that the ban would lift on proof of safety; imports resumed, though no document records when. Almost all the aroma sits in the brittle pink shell, and the seed inside is a small hard pip that gives nothing.

In the kitchen

Crush them between your fingers over the finished plate — a mill shatters the shell to dust and a hot pan kills them outright, the aroma being a volatile resin that goes at the first real heat. Anyone allergic to cashew or pistachio should leave them alone.
