Blue cheese

Fromage bleu

Latin namePenicillium roqueforti
FamilyDairy
OriginRoquefort caves, France
SeasonAll year
Flavourpungent · salty · creamy · rich
Typical price10–20 €/kg

What it is

A shepherd boy, the story goes, abandoned his bread-and-cheese lunch in a Roquefort cave to chase a shepherdess; returning weeks later, he found the cheese veined with blue — and delicious. The caves’ natural draughts still raise every true Roquefort today.

In the kitchen

Its salt and power beg for sweetness: pears, figs, honey, sweet wines. Crumbled over a hot steak, it becomes an instant sauce.
