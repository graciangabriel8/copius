Guindilla

Guindilla (piparra)

Latin nameCapsicum annuum
FamilyVegetables
OriginBasque Country
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · fresh · mild · tangy
Typical price30–50 €/kg

What it is

Guindillas are picked pale green and unripe and go straight into white wine vinegar, which is why they carry almost no heat — around 500 Scoville, less than a poblano. Their moment came in 1940s San Sebastián: the story goes that a regular at Casa Vallés speared one with an anchovy and an olive, and the bar named the skewer a gilda after the Rita Hayworth film.

In the kitchen

Eat them whole, holding the stem, stem and all — at this heat level there is nothing to gain by seeding them. Never cook them: the vinegar cure has already softened the wall, and heat leaves you nothing but skin.
