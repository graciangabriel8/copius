Pedro Ximénez

Pedro Ximénez (PX)

FamilyWines & spirits
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)16–35 €/L

What it is

The grapes are laid out on esparto mats in the September sun for a week or more — the soleo — until they are raisins, and the press yields a must so sugary that yeast can barely work on it. What is bottled carries around 400 grams of sugar a litre, black as treacle, tasting of raisin, fig and liquorice.

In the kitchen

Use it as a sauce, not a wine: a spoonful over vanilla ice cream, or over blue cheese. In a jus a tablespoon is the ceiling — more and it sets to toffee on the plate as it cools.
