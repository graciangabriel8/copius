Chinese rock sugar

Sucre candi chinois

Latin nameSaccharum officinarum
FamilySweet
OriginSichuan and Guangdong
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · mild
Typical price5–10 €/kg

What it is

Bing tang means ice sugar: crystals grown over days on threads or bamboo splints hung in a supersaturated syrup, rather than milled out of it. Size is the whole point - one lump dissolves across a long braise instead of all at once.

In the kitchen

For a red-braise, melt a two-centimetre lump in the oil to a pale caramel before the soy sauce goes in - that is where the lacquer comes from. One shade past pale and the whole pot turns bitter.
