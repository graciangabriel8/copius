Borlotti bean

Haricot borlotti

FamilyLegumes & pulses
Season (France)August – October
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

The cranberry-splashed pod and bean are spectacular raw and lose every trace of the pattern in the pot — they cook to a uniform brown. Nothing is wrong; the pigment is simply not heat stable, and Italian cooks have never minded.

In the kitchen

Cook them with a whole peeled onion, a carrot and a stick of celery, then discard all three. It is the Italian way and it works.
