Ammonium bicarbonate

Bicarbonate d’ammonium

FamilyTexture agents
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–40 €/kg

What it is

Bakers called it hartshorn because it was once distilled from shavings of deer antler, and German recipes still say Hirschhornsalz. It breaks down completely into ammonia, carbon dioxide and water, so alone among chemical leaveners it leaves no salt behind to taste.

In the kitchen

Thin dry biscuits only, under about 7 mm: in anything moist or tall the ammonia is trapped and the crumb keeps its smell. Use 5 g per kilo of flour, dissolved first in a spoonful of the recipe's liquid, and expect the oven to smell alarming for a minute.
