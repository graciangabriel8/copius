Sansho pepper ✦

Poivre sansho

Latin nameZanthoxylum piperitum
FamilySpices
OriginJapan
SeasonAll year
Flavourcitrus · numbing · peppery · fresh
Typical price300–700 €/kg

What it is

Sanshō belongs to the citrus family, not the pepper family, and its tingle comes from hydroxy-alpha-sanshool rather than from capsaicin or piperine. The same shrub gives kinome, the young leaf clapped between the palms to wake it before it goes on a spring dish.

In the kitchen

Buy it in small tins and finish it within a few months: ground sansho loses its lift faster than almost any spice, and a jar over a year old is just mild numbness. Dust it on after cooking, never into the sauce.

Goes with

Eel Chicken Miso Soy sauce Silken tofu (kinugoshi) Button mushroom
