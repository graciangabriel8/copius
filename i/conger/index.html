European conger

Congre

Latin nameConger conger
FamilyFish
OriginNortheast Atlantic & Brittany
SeasonSeptember – February
Flavourmeaty · marine · mild · rich
Typical price7–13 €/kg

What it is

It has no scales and no pelvic fins, never leaves salt water, and the females reach two metres; once they mature for the spawning migration they stop feeding altogether and their teeth and bones begin to break down. Brittany buys it by the section, because the two ends of the fish are two different ingredients.

In the kitchen

Buy the head end and only that: the tail half is a thicket of hair-fine bones that no filleting solves. Keep every trimming - conger gelatine sets a fish stock more firmly than any other bone in the box.
