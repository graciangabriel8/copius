Signature dish · 1995

Smoked eel, foie gras, spring onion and green apple

Martín Berasategui · 1960 · Lasarte-Oria, Basque Country

Four layers, no sauce, eaten in one. It has stayed on the menu unchanged for thirty years, which is its own kind of argument.

Why it works

Two fat, smoky layers against two that are sharp and watery: the apple and the onion are the acid and the crunch that a sauce would otherwise have to supply.

What is in it
