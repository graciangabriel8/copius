White chocolate

Chocolat blanc

Latin nameTheobroma cacao — beurre de cacao
FamilySweet pantry
OriginSwitzerland, 1930s
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Creamy · Milky · Buttery
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

Cocoa butter, sugar and milk with no cocoa solids at all — which is why purists argue it is not chocolate. It was invented to use up surplus cocoa butter left over from making cocoa powder, and it is a by-product that became a product.

In the kitchen

Check the label says cocoa butter and not vegetable fat. That single line separates the real thing from confectionery.
