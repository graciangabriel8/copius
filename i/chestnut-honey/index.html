Chestnut honey

Miel de châtaignier

Latin nameCastanea sativa
FamilySweet
OriginCorsica, Cévennes, Italy
SeasonJune – July
Flavourbitter · earthy · woody · rich
Typical price14–24 €/kg

What it is

Dark, resinous and genuinely bitter — the honey people either love or send back. Its pollen count is so distinctive that laboratories use it as a reference sample, and Corsica protects its version with an appellation.

In the kitchen

It is a savoury honey. Put it with blue cheese, game or a duck glaze rather than on toast.

Goes with

Blue cheese Pecorino Romano Venison Walnut Duck Fig Ricotta Chestnut
