Pomegranate

Grenade

Latin namePunica granatum
FamilyFruit
OriginPersia & Northern India
SeasonSeptember – December
Flavourtangy · sweet · fruity
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Persephone ate six pomegranate seeds in the underworld, and the Greeks used that to explain winter itself. The Spanish city of Granada carries its name, and its jewelled seeds have decorated feasts from Persia to Andalusia.

In the kitchen

Halve it and slap the back with a wooden spoon over a bowl — the seeds rain out. They wake up anything rich or creamy.

Goes with

Walnut Yogurt Mint Orange Cinnamon Duck Quinoa
