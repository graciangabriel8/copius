Filé powder ✦

Poudre de filé

Latin nameSassafras albidum
FamilySpices
OriginLouisiana
Season (France)All year
FlavourWoody · Herbal · Earthy · Mild
Typical price (France)150–300 €/kg

What it is

The Choctaw ground dried sassafras leaf and called it kombo, a word that may well be the root of gumbo itself. Filé is simply the French for threaded, which is a warning as much as a name: let the pot boil and it goes stringy.

In the kitchen

Take the gumbo off the heat before it goes anywhere near the powder, then stir in a scant teaspoon per bowl. Better still, set the jar on the table and let each person thicken their own.
