Shiratamako

Shiratamako

Latin nameOryza sativa glutinosa
FamilyGrains
OriginJapan
SeasonAll year
Flavourmild · milky · delicate
Typical price20–35 €/kg

What it is

Shiratamako is made wet: the rice is soaked, ground with water into a slurry, pressed, dried into a cake and broken into pebbles rather than milled to dust. That detour costs more, and it is the reason its dough stays soft and stretchy hours after dry-milled mochiko has gone stiff.

In the kitchen

Do not add the water all at once. Work it in a spoonful at a time, crushing the granules between your fingers until the dough gives like an earlobe — the standard Japanese test — then boil the balls until they float and give them thirty seconds more.
