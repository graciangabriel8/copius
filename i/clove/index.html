Clove

Clou de girofle

Latin nameSyzygium aromaticum
FamilySpices
OriginMaluku Islands, Indonesia
SeasonAll year
Flavourwarm · pungent · sweet · woody
Typical price25–50 €/kg

What it is

Chinese courtiers of the Han dynasty held cloves in their mouths before addressing the emperor, so their breath would be worthy. On Ternate, islanders planted a clove tree for each newborn child — the tree and the child grew together.

In the kitchen

An onion studded with two or three cloves, dropped into a broth or béchamel, is the old French trick for depth without visible spice.
