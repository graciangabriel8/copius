Vergeoise

Vergeoise

Latin nameSaccharum — sucre de betterave
FamilySweet
OriginNorthern France & Belgium
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · toasty · rich · warm
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

A beet sugar, not a cane one — which is what separates it from cassonade and why the north of France insists on it. Speculoos, tarte au sucre and gaufres de Lille are all built on vergeoise brune and taste wrong without it.

In the kitchen

Blonde and brune are not interchangeable. Brune is caramelised twice and carries most of the flavour.

Goes with

Butter Cinnamon T55 flour Cream Egg Apple Clove Dark chocolate
