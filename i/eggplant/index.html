Eggplant

Aubergine

Latin nameSolanum melongena
FamilyVegetables
OriginIndia & Southeast Asia
Season (France)June – September
FlavourMild · Earthy · Rich
Typical price (France)2,50–5 €/kg

What it is

Medieval Europe called it the “mad apple” and blamed it for insanity; Italian still says melanzana. The Ottomans knew better — a dish of it was so good, they say, that an imam fainted at the taste.

In the kitchen

It drinks oil like a sponge when raw; salt it first or roast it dry, then dress it generously.
