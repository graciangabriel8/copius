Abalone ✦ ◆

Ormeau

Latin nameHaliotis tuberculata
FamilyShellfish
OriginBrittany, Channel Islands & Japan
SeasonOctober – February
Flavourmarine · sweet · meaty · briny
Typical price120–250 €/kg

What it is

A sea snail in a mother-of-pearl ear, prised off rocks at the lowest tides. Japan’s ama — women free-divers whose craft is two thousand years old — have gathered them for emperors; in Brittany the ormeau is so coveted that its harvest is rationed to a handful of big-tide days a year.

In the kitchen

Tenderise or braise — there is no middle path: pound the escalopes and flash them in butter, or steam it hours with kombu until it slices like a wild mushroom.

Goes with

Butter Kombu Soy sauce Shiitake
