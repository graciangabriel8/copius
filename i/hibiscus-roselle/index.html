Hibiscus / Roselle

Hibiscus (bissap)

Latin nameHibiscus sabdariffa
FamilyHerbs
OriginSahel and West Africa
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · tangy · fruity
Typical price12–25 €/kg

What it is

What is sold as hibiscus is not the petal but the calyx — the fleshy cup left behind after the flower drops, harvested a few weeks later and dried. It travels under a different name in every country that drinks it: bissap in Senegal, karkadé in Egypt, agua de Jamaica in Mexico, sorrel in the Caribbean at Christmas.

In the kitchen

Brew below boiling and no longer than ten minutes: hot and long pulls tannin out along with the acid and the drink turns drying. Never use aluminium or bare cast iron — the pigment reacts with the metal and the red goes grey.
