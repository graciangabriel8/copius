Soy sauce

Sauce soja

Latin nameGlycine max (fermented)
FamilyCondiments
OriginChina
SeasonAll year
Flavoursalty · umami · rich · toasty
Typical price4–9 €/L

What it is

Born in China over two thousand years ago as a way to stretch precious salt, brewed from soybeans, wheat and patient moulds. Some Japanese breweries have kept their mash tubs alive for centuries — the microbes are the family heirloom.

In the kitchen

Think of it as liquid seasoning, not just an Asian marker: a teaspoon deepens a beef stew, a vinaigrette or even a caramel.
