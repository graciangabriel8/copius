Signature dish · 1961

Bœuf bourguignon

Julia Child · 1912–2004 · Paris, then Cambridge, Massachusetts

Not her invention, but the version that made French cooking reproducible in a home kitchen abroad — written out step by step with nothing assumed.

Why it works

The meat is dried and browned in batches, because a crowded pan steams instead of colouring, and that colour is most of the finished flavour.
