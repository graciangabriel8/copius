Signature dish · v. 2012

Berlingots

Anne-Sophie Pic · 1969 · Maison Pic, Valence

Pasta folded into sweet-shop pyramids around a running cheese, opened at the table by a broth poured over them.

Why it works

The filling is held just below setting, so the heat of the poured broth is what finishes it; the shape exists to give the broth somewhere to pool.

What is in it

Parmesan Cream Black trumpet Butter
