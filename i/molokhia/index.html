Molokhia

Corète potagère

Latin nameCorchorus olitorius
FamilyVegetables
OriginNile Delta and the Levant
Season (France)June – September
FlavourEarthy · Green · Mild
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

The Fatimid caliph al-Hakim banned it around 1005, for reasons the chroniclers cannot agree on: an aphrodisiac, or simply a dish loved by a rival dynasty. His successor lifted the ban, but the Druze, who revere al-Hakim, have kept it for a thousand years and eat no molokhia at all.

In the kitchen

Chop it to a near-paste with a two-bladed makhrata and add it to the broth off the boil: a rolling boil breaks the mucilage and the soup separates into water and green flecks. Once it has separated it will not come back.
