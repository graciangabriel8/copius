Jasmine rice

Riz jasmin

Latin nameOryza sativa
FamilyGrains
OriginThailand
SeasonAll year
Flavourfloral · sweet · delicate · mild
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

Named for the colour of the milled grain rather than any scent of jasmine — Thai calls it hom mali, fragrant jasmine-white. Unlike basmati it is best fresh from the harvest, and Thai cooks pay attention to the crop year.

In the kitchen

Use less water than the packet says, roughly one and a quarter to one. It is stickier than basmati and floods easily.

Goes with

Coconut milk Lemongrass Chili pepper Fish sauce Cilantro / Coriander Lime Ginger Garlic
