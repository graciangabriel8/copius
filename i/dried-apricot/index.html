Dried apricot

Abricot sec

Latin namePrunus armeniaca
FamilyFruits
OriginTurkey & Central Asia
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Sour · Fruity · Honeyed
Typical price (France)8–15 €/kg

What it is

The bright orange ones are treated with sulphur dioxide to keep the colour; the brown ones are not. The brown are more honeyed and figgy, the orange fresher and more acidic — they are effectively two different ingredients.

In the kitchen

Soak them in warm tea rather than water before they go into a tagine. It plumps them without washing out the flavour.
