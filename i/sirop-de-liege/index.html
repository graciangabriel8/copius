Liège syrup

Sirop de Liège

Latin nameSirop de Liège
FamilySweet pantry
OriginWallonia, Belgium
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Sour · Fruity · Rich
Typical price (France)8–15 €/kg

What it is

Pears and apples boiled for hours until they reduce to a near-black paste, with no added sugar at all — the sweetness is entirely the fruit's. It has been made in the Pays de Herve since the seventeenth century and is eaten with cheese.

In the kitchen

Belgians put it with strong cheese and with rabbit. Treating it as a jam misses most of what it can do.
