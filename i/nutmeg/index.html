Nutmeg

Noix de muscade

Latin nameMyristica fragrans
FamilySpices
OriginBanda Islands, Indonesia
Season (France)All year
FlavourWarm · Sweet · Woody · Pungent
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

Once it grew on ten tiny islands and nowhere else on Earth. In 1667 the Dutch traded Manhattan to the English to keep Run, a nutmeg island two miles long — history’s verdict on that swap is still funny.

In the kitchen

Buy it whole, grate it directly over the dish, and stop earlier than you think: three passes of the grater season a whole béchamel.
