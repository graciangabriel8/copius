Sudachi

Latin nameCitrus sudachi
FamilyFruit
OriginTokushima, Japan
SeasonAugust – October
Flavoursour · citrus · resinous
Typical price3–6 €/pièce

What it is

Tokushima has grown sudachi since the Edo period and now supplies around 98 percent of Japan’s crop, under a protected geographical indication that requires the fruit to be picked dark green. Left on the tree it yellows and loses the pine edge that is the entire point, so a ripe sudachi is a failure rather than a stage.

In the kitchen

Cut it across the equator rather than through the poles — the halves press cleanly and hold the seeds back. Take the zest first if you want any: once the fruit is cut and squeezed, the peel is finished.
