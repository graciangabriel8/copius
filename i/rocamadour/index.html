Rocamadour

Rocamadour

Latin nameRocamadour AOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginCausses du Quercy, Lot
Season (France)March – October
FlavourCreamy · Tangy · Nutty · Milky
Typical price (France)1,50–2,50 €/pièce

What it is

A lease of 1451 between the Bishop of Évreux and his tenants in Quercy fixed the tithe in these cheeses, which means it served as currency before it was ever a delicacy. It weighs about 35 grams — the whole cheese is a single mouthful.

In the kitchen

Warm it, do not cook it: two or three minutes at 180 °C on toast, until it slumps but takes no colour. Past that the fat separates and the paste goes grainy.
