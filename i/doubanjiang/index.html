Doubanjiang

Doubanjiang

Latin name豆瓣醬
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginPixian, Sichuan
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Hot · Earthy
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

Sichuan calls it the soul of its cooking. The best comes from Pixian and is fermented in open jars for three years or more, stirred by hand and covered against rain — the paste darkens from red to near black as it ages.

In the kitchen

Fry it in oil until the fat turns red before anything else goes in. That step is not optional — it is where the flavour is released.
