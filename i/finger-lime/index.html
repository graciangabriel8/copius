Citron caviar

Latin nameCitrus australasica
FamilyFruit
OriginAustralian rainforest
SeasonSeptember – December
Flavourcitrus · tangy · fresh · delicate
Typical price80–180 €/kg

What it is

Australia’s rainforest kept this secret for millennia: a finger-shaped citrus whose flesh falls apart into hundreds of pearls that pop on the tongue like sour caviar. Aboriginal peoples ate it long before bush-food chefs made it the most photographed garnish in fine dining.

In the kitchen

Halve it and squeeze gently — the pearls flow out intact. Spoon them over oysters or scallops at the very last second; heat collapses the caviar.
