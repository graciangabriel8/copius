Koji rice

Riz koji

Latin nameAspergillus oryzae
FamilyCondiments
OriginJapanese koji rooms
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · umami · floral
Typical price20–35 €/kg

What it is

The Brewing Society of Japan named Aspergillus oryzae the national fungus in 2006, after a thousand years of domestication that has left it unable to make the toxins its wild relatives still produce. It is bought alive: the mould’s amylase cuts starch into sugar and its protease cuts protein into glutamate, which is the entire reason miso, sake and amazake exist.

In the kitchen

Keep it refrigerated and use it within a few weeks: the enzymes fade, and a koji that smells of anything but chestnut and mushroom is finished. They work best around 55-60 °C and are dead by 70, so an amazake or a shio-koji marinade held too hot converts nothing at all.
