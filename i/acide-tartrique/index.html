Tartaric acid

Acide tartrique

FamilyTexture agents
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)20–45 €/kg

What it is

Scheele isolated it in 1769 from the crust that settles inside a wine cask, and in 1848 Louis Pasteur sorted its two mirror-image crystals apart by hand under a lens. No other kitchen acid started a whole branch of chemistry.

In the kitchen

A pinch - 0.5 g per kilo of sugar - added at 114 C inverts just enough of a syrup to stop it graining, and no more. Heavier and it will never set at all; it is the harshest of the fruit acids on the palate, so keep it where grape or wine already belongs.
