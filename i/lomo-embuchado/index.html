Lomo embuchado

lomo

Latin nameSus scrofa (loin, cured)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginSpain
SeasonAll year
FlavourMeaty · Salty · Peppery · Mild
Typical price40–70 €/kg

What it is

The whole loin is rubbed with pimentón, garlic and oregano, then pushed into a wide casing — embuchado means stuffed into the gut — and dried three to four months. Because the loin carries almost no internal fat, it is the piece a Spanish curing house loses most weight on, and the first to case-harden if the drying room runs too dry.

In the kitchen

Cut it as thin as the knife allows and let the slices stand ten minutes at room temperature; straight from the fridge a lean cured loin gives you salt and nothing else.
