Star anise

Badiane

Latin nameIllicium verum
FamilySpices
OriginSouthern China & Vietnam
Season (France)All year
FlavourAnise-like · Sweet · Woody · Warm
Typical price (France)25–60 €/kg

What it is

Until recently it was the industrial source of shikimic acid, the starting material for the antiviral oseltamivir — a global flu scare in 2005 caused a genuine shortage of a cooking spice. It is one of the five in Chinese five-spice.

In the kitchen

One whole star is enough for a large pot. Two is too many, and there is no way to take it back out once it has infused.

Its 31 pairings are in the full version, coming soon (€3 a month or €29 a year).
