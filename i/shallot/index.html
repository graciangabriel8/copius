Shallot

Échalote

Latin nameAllium ascalonicum
FamilyVegetables
OriginCentral Asia, via Ascalon
Season (France)All year
FlavourPungent · Sweet · Delicate
Typical price (France)4–7 €/kg

What it is

Its name remembers Ascalon, the ancient port from which crusaders reportedly carried it home. The onion’s finishing-school graduate, it underwrites half of French sauce-making — béarnaise, beurre blanc, red-wine reductions, the mignonette on your oysters — while almost never being named on the menu.

In the kitchen

Mince it fine and sweat it without colour: browned shallot turns bitter where onion turns sweet. Raw in a vinaigrette, ten minutes in the vinegar first tames it.
