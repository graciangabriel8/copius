Prosciutto di San Daniele

jambon de San Daniele

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–70 €/kg

What it is

Three ingredients are permitted: Italian pork, sea salt, and the air of San Daniele, where alpine cold meets Adriatic damp. The trotter stays on and the leg is pressed flat for a day or two, which gives it the guitar silhouette and lets the salt reach the centre without the ham being salted harder.

In the kitchen

Cut it thicker than a Parma — the flesh is moister and a veil-thin slice tears on the board. Take it off the fridge shelf thirty minutes ahead, and never dress it with anything acidic.
