Genmai miso

Miso de riz complet (genmai miso)

Latin nameOryza sativa, Glycine max
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginJapan
Season (France)All year
FlavourUmami · Nutty · Salty · Earthy
Typical price (France)12–22 €/kg

What it is

Koji cannot get through the bran: Aspergillus oryzae grows readily into polished rice and stalls on the intact husk layer, which is why brown-rice miso stayed rare until millers learned to score the bran just enough for the mould to enter without stripping it. What survives that scoring — the oils and the bran itself — is the nuttiness the paste is bought for.

In the kitchen

Never boil it: slake it in a ladle of hot stock and stir it in off the flame, since a minute at a rolling boil flattens the aroma and kills the live culture. A tablespoon per 200 ml of stock is a soup; twice that becomes a marinade that will cure fish overnight.
