Oriental Beauty oolong ✦ ◆

Oolong Oriental Beauty (Bai Hao)

Latin nameCamellia sinensis var. sinensis
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginHsinchu and Miaoli, Taiwan
SeasonJune – July
FlavourHoneyed · Fruity · Floral
Typical price200–800 €/kg

What it is

The honey-muscat aroma is a wound response: the tea jassid Jacobiasca formosana feeds on the growing shoot and the plant answers with terpenes, which heavy oxidation then fixes. Growers stop spraying to invite the pest, and the local name pengfeng cha, braggart tea, is said to come from a farmer nobody believed when he reported what his bitten leaves fetched.

In the kitchen

Heavily oxidised, it takes 90 °C and a full three minutes without hardening, and it holds up infused into a custard base at 80 °C. Never roast or reheat it — the honey volatiles go first.

Goes with
