Dry vermouth

Vermouth sec

Latin nameArtemisia absinthium
FamilyCellar
OriginMarseillan, Languedoc
SeasonAll year
Flavourbitter · herbal · warm
Typical price10–18 €/L

What it is

The name is German — Wermut, wormwood — and the wormwood is not decoration: it is the bitterness that survives a reduction, at the point where white wine has gone flat and sour. At Marseillan the base wines spend a year outdoors in oak, through sun, lagoon mist and frost, before a single botanical is infused into them.

In the kitchen

Swap it for white wine in a fish sauce volume for volume, but stop the reduction earlier — the bitterness concentrates faster than the acidity does. An open bottle is a wine, not a spirit: refrigerate it and finish it inside a month.
