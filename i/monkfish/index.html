Monkfish

Lotte

Latin nameLophius piscatorius
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginNortheast Atlantic
SeasonOctober – March
FlavourSweet · Meaty · Marine · Rich
Typical price22–35 €/kg

What it is

So ugly that fishmongers traditionally removed the head before display, and it was once thrown back as worthless. Its dense tail meat behaves like meat rather than fish, which is why it is called the poor man's lobster — a name it has long outgrown in price.

In the kitchen

Strip off every trace of the grey membrane. It shrinks violently in the pan and curls the fillet into a knot.

Goes with

Lard Garlic Saffron Tomato Thyme White wine vinegar Leek Olive oil
