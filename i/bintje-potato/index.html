Bintje potato

Bintje

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)August – November
Typical price (France)1,50–3 €/kg

What it is

A Frisian schoolmaster, Kornelis Lieuwes de Vries, bred it in 1904 and named it after a former pupil, Bintje Jansma, then seventeen — he named most of his 125 varieties after his children and his students. Its dry, high-starch flesh is exactly what a two-bath fry wants, and exactly why it disintegrates in a pot of boiling water.

In the kitchen

Fry twice: 150 °C until limp and still pale, rest ten minutes, then 180 °C for colour. For purée, steam or bake it in its skin — boiled Bintje takes on water and turns to glue under a whisk.
