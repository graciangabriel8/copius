Cannellini bean

Haricot cannellini

Latin namePhaseolus vulgaris
FamilyPulses
OriginTuscany, Italy
SeasonAll year
Flavourcreamy · mild · nutty · earthy
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Tuscans are nicknamed mangiafagioli, bean-eaters, and this is the bean they mean. The classic preparation is al fiasco — cooked overnight in a wine flask buried in the dying embers of a fire, with oil, garlic and sage.

In the kitchen

Save a ladle of the cooking liquid to loosen any purée. It emulsifies with olive oil far better than water does.
