Malic acid

Acide malique

FamilyTexture agents
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)20–40 €/kg

What it is

Carl Wilhelm Scheele pulled it out of apple juice in 1785, and the name looks no further than that: malum, apple. It is the acid a fruit spends as it ripens, which is why a green apple bites and a ripe one has stopped.

In the kitchen

At equal weight it reads 10 to 20 percent sourer than citric, so start at 1 g per kilo of puree and taste. On a sour sweet it stays put where citric goes sticky, because it draws far less water out of the air.
