Rack of lamb

Carré d’agneau

Latin nameOvis aries (rib rack)
FamilyCuts
OriginThe first eight ribs, frenched
SeasonMarch – June
Flavourmeaty · delicate · rich
Typical price28–45 €/kg

What it is

Eight ribs scraped clean to the bone — “frenched” — until the rack looks architectural; two racks interlocked become the guard of honour of British wedding tables. Under its thin cap of fat sits the tenderest lamb on the animal, built for a green persillade crust.

In the kitchen

Sear the fat cap first, paint with mustard, press on the herb crumbs, then a short hot roast: pink at the bone, rested until the juices settle.

Goes with

Rosemary Garlic Mustard Honey Thyme
