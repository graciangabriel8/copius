What it is

Chorizo was a pale sausage for its first several centuries: a sixteenth-century Castilian recipe lists pork, garlic, clove, white wine and salt, and nothing red. Pimentón came back from the Americas and the monks of Yuste in Extremadura worked out the oak-smoke drying that fixed its colour and flavour, remaking the sausage entirely.

In the kitchen

Start it in a cold dry pan: the pimentón is fat-soluble, and the orange fat that runs out is the point — pour it over potatoes or into rice rather than draining it away. Dropped into hot oil it scorches bitter in seconds.
