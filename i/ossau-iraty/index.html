Ossau-Iraty

FamilyDairy
OriginBéarn & Basque Country
SeasonAll year
Flavournutty · sweet · rich · mild
Typical price24–36 €/kg

What it is

Made from the milk of Manech and Basco-Béarnaise ewes, whose shepherds still move them up to summer pasture on foot. The Basques eat it with black cherry jam from Itxassou — a pairing so fixed that the cheese is rarely served without it.

In the kitchen

If you cannot find black cherry jam, quince paste does the same job: the sheep's milk needs something sweet and slightly tart against it.

Goes with

Cherry Quince Walnut Honey Fig Almond Grape Espelette pepper
