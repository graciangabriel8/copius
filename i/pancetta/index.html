Pancetta

pancetta

Latin nameSus scrofa (belly, cured)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginItaly
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Salty · Meaty · Peppery
Typical price (France)18–32 €/kg

What it is

Salt, pepper, often juniper or fennel, three weeks to three months, and no smoke at any stage — that absence is the whole definition. Rolled tight it is arrotolata, left flat it is tesa, and Italian kitchens keep them apart: the roll is for slicing raw, the slab for cutting into cubes.

In the kitchen

Cut it into thick lardons rather than thin strips — thin ones give up their fat and turn to leather in the same minute. Twenty minutes in the freezer and the knife goes through cleanly.
