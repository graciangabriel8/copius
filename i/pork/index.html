What it is

“Everything is good in the pig, from head to tail,” says the French proverb — and charcuterie is the proof: centuries of salting, smoking and patience invented to waste nothing. Every farm once measured winter by its pig.

In the kitchen

Modern pork is lean and unforgiving: brine chops an hour before cooking, and pull them off the heat while still blushing at the bone.
