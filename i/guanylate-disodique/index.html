Disodium guanylate

Guanylate disodique

FamilyTexture agents
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

Kuninaka Akira closed the set in 1957, isolating it from dried shiitake and completing what Ikeda and Kodama had started. Drying is what makes it: a fresh shiitake holds almost none, and the dried cap holds a great deal, which is why the two are not the same ingredient and why a recipe asking for dried mushrooms rarely accepts fresh ones.

In the kitchen

Same rule as inosinate — with a glutamate source, never instead of one. Soaking water from dried shiitake already carries it, so a stock built on that needs none added.
