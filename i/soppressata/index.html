soppressata

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)25–40 €/kg

What it is

It is pressed flat under boards or weights while it cures, which is exactly what the name says and why the slice comes out oval instead of round. Soppressata di Calabria has been a DOP since 1998; the pressing drives out air pockets, so it dries evenly and keeps far longer than a round salame of the same weight.

In the kitchen

Slice on the diagonal to get the whole oval face, about 2 mm. If the fat has greyed at the cut end, take a centimetre off and carry on — that is oxidation from the exposed face, not spoilage.
