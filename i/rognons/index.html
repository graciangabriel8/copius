Kidneys

Rognons

Latin nameRen
FamilyCuts
OriginFrance
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · earthy · meaty · musky
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

Veal kidneys are the ones worth seeking — milder and finer than lamb or beef, and a Lyonnais standard served in a mustard cream. The surrounding fat is the suet that made English steamed puddings possible.

In the kitchen

Sear them fast and serve them pink. Overcooked kidney turns grainy and ammoniac, and there is no recovery.

Goes with
