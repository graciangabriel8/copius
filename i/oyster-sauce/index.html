Oyster sauce

Sauce d’huître

Latin nameCrassostrea spp. (reduction)
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginGuangdong, China
Season (France)All year
FlavourUmami · Salty · Sweet · Rich
Typical price (France)8–14 €/kg

What it is

The sauce exists because a Guangdong cook left a pot of oyster soup on the fire too long in 1888 and came back to a dark reduction; Lee Kum Sheung sold it, and the house he founded is still the biggest name on the shelf. Worth remembering when reading a label: many bottles now list water, sugar and starch ahead of any oyster.

In the kitchen

Add it at the end, off a hard flame — its sugars scorch and turn bitter in a wok. A teaspoon per portion, and cut the soy sauce back, since it already brings salt, sugar and thickener.
