The dish that changed something · 1893

Peach Melba

Auguste Escoffier · 1846–1935 · Savoy and Carlton hotels, London

Poached peach, raspberry purée, vanilla ice cream, and nothing hidden. Written at the Savoy for the singer Nellie Melba, it set the pattern for the named dessert built from three things that are allowed to taste of themselves.

Why it works

The raspberry's acidity cuts the sugar of the poached peach, the vanilla sits under both as a bridge, and the cold of the ice cream stops the whole thing cloying. Three elements, one job each.
