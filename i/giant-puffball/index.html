Giant puffball ✦

Vesse-de-loup géante

Latin nameCalvatia gigantea
FamilyMushrooms
OriginMeadow edges and hedgerows
Season (France)August – October
FlavourMild · Earthy · Delicate
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

A large one holds something on the order of seven trillion spores, which is exactly why its edible window is so short: the flesh is food while it is pure white and becomes a bag of powder as soon as they ripen. No gills, no stem, no cap — a uniform white block, which is why it is cut into steaks and handled as butchery rather than as mushroom.

In the kitchen

Cut it in half before you buy it or carry it home: any yellow, olive or brown inside means the spores have started and the whole thing is finished. Slice it two centimetres thick, egg-and-breadcrumb it and fry — it is a sponge, so a wet pan gives you a wet slice.
