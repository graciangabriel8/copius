Zante currant

Raisin de Corinthe

Latin nameVitis vinifera 'Black Corinth'
FamilyNuts
OriginIonian islands, Greece
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · tangy · fruity · sweet
Typical price8–14 €/kg

What it is

The English word currant is a worn-down raisins of Corinth, the medieval trade name for these tiny dried grapes shipped out of the Greek port. The blackcurrant borrowed the name in the 1570s, because the new northern berry looked like them — the grape came first, not the bush.

In the kitchen

Soak them ten minutes in warm water, rum or verjuice and drain well before folding them into a dough; dry ones pull water out of the crumb and scorch on the crust. Toss them in a spoonful of the flour so they stay suspended instead of sinking.
