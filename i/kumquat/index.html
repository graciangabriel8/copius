Kumquat

Kumquat

Latin nameCitrus japonica
FamilyFruits
OriginSouthern China
SeasonDecember – March
FlavourSour · Sweet · Citrusy · Bitter
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

The citrus that inverts itself: the peel is sweet and the flesh is sharp, so it is eaten whole and the order of flavours runs backwards. In southern China a potted kumquat tree at New Year is a wish for prosperity.

In the kitchen

Roll it between your fingers before eating to burst the oil glands in the skin. It doubles the perfume.

Goes with

Honey Vanilla Dark chocolate Ginger Duck Cinnamon Almond Clove
