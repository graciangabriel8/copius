Harissa

Latin nameهريسة
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginTunisia
Season (France)All year
FlavourHot · Smoky · Earthy · Pungent
Typical price (France)8–16 €/kg

What it is

Tunisia's national condiment, and the chillies in it arrived with Ottoman traders after the Columbian exchange. The best is from Nabeul, where the peppers are sun-dried on rooftops before being ground with caraway, coriander and garlic.

In the kitchen

Caraway is what makes it harissa rather than chilli paste. If yours has none, it is missing the defining note.

Its 14 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
