Guanciale

Guanciale

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)20–35 €/kg

What it is

Pork jowl — guancia means cheek — cured with salt and pepper and hung until its fat turns to perfume. Rome guards it jealously: without guanciale there is no true carbonara, no amatriciana, and suggesting bacon in its place is the fastest way to start an argument in Trastevere.

In the kitchen

Start it in a cold pan and let the fat melt slowly to glass-crisp — that rendered gold is the sauce; never add oil beside it.
