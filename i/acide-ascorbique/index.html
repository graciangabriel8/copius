Ascorbic acid

Acide ascorbique

Latin nameAcidum ascorbicum
FamilyTexture
OriginCitrus and paprika
SeasonAll year
Flavourmild · sour · fresh
Typical price20–40 €/kg

What it is

Albert Szent-Gyorgyi, who isolated it from paprika and took the 1937 Nobel for it, wanted to name the unidentified sugar ignose, then godnose; his editor refused both. What sells today is not vitamin C the nutrient but vitamin C the tool - a powder bought to stop things browning.

In the kitchen

One gram in a litre of water, brushed on or dipped, holds cut apple, avocado and artichoke pale for hours. In bread it is a dough conditioner at 20 to 40 ppm, a scant pinch per kilo of flour; past that the dough tightens instead of strengthening.
