Beefsteak fungus ✦

Langue de bœuf

Latin nameFistulina hepatica
FamilyMushrooms
OriginOld oaks and sweet chestnut
Season (France)August – October
FlavourSour · Meaty · Earthy
Typical price (France)12–25 €/kg

What it is

It grows on living oak and sweet chestnut, and the wood it colonises turns a deep russet that cabinetmakers sell at a premium as brown oak. Cut the bracket and it bleeds a red juice; the flesh under the skin is sour with tannin rather than savoury.

In the kitchen

Take only young, soft brackets and slice them paper-thin across the grain for a raw salad - the acidity replaces the vinegar. Cooked whole it turns tough and inky; if you cook it at all, blanch it once and discard the black water.
