Puy lentil

Lentille verte du Puy

Latin nameLens culinaris
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginHaute-Loire, Auvergne
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Peppery · Nutty · Mild
Typical price (France)8–14 €/kg

What it is

The first vegetable in France to be granted an AOC, in 1996. It grows on volcanic soil at altitude where the summers are dry, and the lack of water is what keeps the skin thin and stops the lentil bursting — scarcity as a quality control.

In the kitchen

Never salt the cooking water at the start. Salt firms the skins and they take twice as long to soften.
