Brie

OriginSeine-et-Marne, Île-de-France
SeasonAll year
Flavourcreamy · buttery · mild · earthy
Typical price10–16 €/kg

What it is

At the Congress of Vienna in 1815, while Europe was being carved up, the diplomats held a side contest for the best cheese of their nations. Talleyrand entered Brie and won. It has been called the king of cheeses ever since — elected, unusually, by foreigners.

In the kitchen

Take it out an hour before serving. Cold Brie is chalk; at room temperature it slumps and smells of mushroom and cream.

Goes with

Apple Walnut Honey Grape Butter Button mushroom Black truffle Fig
