Pimentón de la Vera

Pimentón de la Vera

Latin nameCapsicum annuum
FamilySpices
OriginLa Vera, Extremadura
SeasonAll year
FlavourSmoky · Sweet · Woody
Typical price30–60 €/kg

What it is

The peppers dry ten to fifteen days over smouldering holm oak in a two-storey drying house, the fire never touching them, until four fifths of their weight is gone. The story goes that the first seeds reached the Jeronymite monks of Yuste in the sixteenth century; the protected designation came only in 1996.

In the kitchen

Smoke and sugar both burn: take the pan off the heat, count a few seconds, then stir the powder into warm fat so it blooms without scorching. For a chorizo mix, count 20 to 30 g per kilo of meat.
