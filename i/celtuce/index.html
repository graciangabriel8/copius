Celtuce

Laitue asperge

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)March – May, October – November
Typical price (France)6–12 €/kg

What it is

China selected the same species as garden lettuce for the opposite trait: a stem that thickens and stays sweet instead of bolting into bitterness, the leaves reduced to an afterthought. An American seed house put it on the Western market in the 1940s under a coined name, celery plus lettuce, and it went nowhere.

In the kitchen

Peel deep — the outer layer is stringy and bitter, and you can see where it ends, a pale ring inside the green. The jade core wants a minute at most in the wok, or a salting and a rinse to be eaten raw.
