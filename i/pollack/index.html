Lieu jaune

Season (France)October – March
FlavourSweet · Mild · Marine
Typical price (France)15–25 €/kg

What it is

Lieu jaune and lieu noir are two different fish and the price gap runs to about fivefold: the yellow one is line-caught from small boats off the Pointe de Bretagne and lands with its flanks unbruised. The curved lateral line and the jutting lower jaw are how you tell it at the stall.

In the kitchen

The flakes are large and let go all at once, so cook thick portions and stop early: 50 °C at the centre, then two minutes off the heat. Skin-on searing works here where it fails on cod.
