Pine shoots

Pousses de pin

Latin namePinus sylvestris
FamilyHerbs
OriginScots pine woods, from Spain to Siberia
Season (France)April – May
FlavourResinous · Warm · Citrusy
Typical price (France)0,50–0,60 €/pièce

What it is

Herbalists pick the bud while it is still shut, in April, and dry it for cough teas. The cook waits a few weeks more, for the soft green candle that pushes out of it. Pine has nursed winters since antiquity — sanatoriums were ringed with it — and in Poland the spring shoots still go into a jar of sugar for the winter syrup.

In the kitchen

Layer the shoots with sugar in a jar, leave two to four days, then strain. The syrup is more resinous than spruce, so go by the spoonful — over yoghurt, ricotta or a fruit salad. Take only a few per tree, where picking is allowed, and check the needles come in pairs: yew’s grow singly and flat, and yew is lethal.
