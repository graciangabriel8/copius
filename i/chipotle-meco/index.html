Chipotle meco

Chipotle meco

Latin nameCapsicum annuum 'Jalapeño'
FamilySpices
OriginChihuahua and Veracruz, Mexico
SeasonAll year
Flavoursmoky · hot · woody · toasty
Typical price50–100 €/kg

What it is

Both chipotles begin as a red jalapeño, but the meco is left longer on the plant and then smoked roughly twice as long as the morita — long enough to come out ash-grey and stiff as cardboard. The morita, softer and purple-brown, is what nearly every tin and packet labelled chipotle actually contains.

In the kitchen

It will not rehydrate the way a morita does: give it thirty minutes in near-boiling water and expect to blend and sieve rather than chop. Use half what a recipe asks of morita, since the smoke reads about twice as strong.
