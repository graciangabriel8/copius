March hygrophorus ✦

Hygrophore de mars

Latin nameHygrophorus marzuolus
FamilyMushrooms
OriginMontane fir and beech, 1000-2000 m
SeasonFebruary – May
FlavourSweet · Earthy · Floral
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

Marzuolus is simply of March: it fruits under melting snow in mountain fir and beech, when nothing else edible is up. Grey on grey litter and usually half-buried, it is hunted by feeling for domes underfoot rather than by eye.

In the kitchen

The flesh is thick and holds almost no water, so it takes a real sear and barely shrinks: three minutes a side in foaming butter, like a scallop. Brush the base hard — it comes up full of soil.
