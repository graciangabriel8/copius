Anchovy paste

Pâte d’anchois

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)30–60 €/kg

What it is

The direct descendant of garum, the fermented fish sauce Rome put on everything and shipped in amphorae across the empire. Its glutamates dissolve completely into a sauce, which is why it deepens a dish without ever tasting of fish.

In the kitchen

Melt it in warm oil before anything else joins the pan. It disappears, and nobody will identify it.
