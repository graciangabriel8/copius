Cuttlefish

Seiche

Latin nameSepia officinalis
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginEastern Atlantic & Mediterranean
Season (France)March – June, September – October
FlavourSweet · Marine · Umami · Rich
Typical price (France)10–18 €/kg

What it is

Its ink was the original sepia — the brown of old photographs and Renaissance drawings is named after this animal, and was made from it for centuries. The internal shell is the cuttlebone sold in birdcages.

In the kitchen

Thicker and meatier than squid, and it takes longer. Give it forty minutes of gentle braising rather than a fast sear.
