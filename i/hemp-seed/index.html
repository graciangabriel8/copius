Hemp seed

Graine de chanvre

Latin nameCannabis sativa
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginCentral Asia
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Green · Creamy · Mild
Typical price (France)12–20 €/kg

What it is

The same species as the drug plant but a different cultivar, bred for fibre and seed and containing almost no THC. France is the largest hemp producer in Europe and has grown it continuously since the age of sail, when it made rope.

In the kitchen

Never cook it — the oil oxidises fast and turns fishy. Scatter it raw over a finished dish.
