Dog cockle

Amande de mer

Latin nameGlycymeris glycymeris
FamilyShellfish
OriginGravel beds of the Atlantic shelf
SeasonSeptember – April
Flavourmarine · nutty · briny
Typical price6–12 €/kg

What it is

The shell lays down one growth ring a year and never pauses, which is why researchers off western Scotland have aged dog cockles at close to two hundred years and built dated sea-temperature records out of them. The sclerochronologist reads the shell the cook throws away.

In the kitchen

It never gapes, so go in at the hinge with a stiff blade rather than a flexible oyster knife. Eat it raw within a day of landing: heat sets the adductor hard in under a minute, and nothing brings it back.
