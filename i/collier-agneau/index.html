Lamb neck

Collier d’agneau

Latin nameAgneau — collier
FamilyCuts
OriginFrance
SeasonMarch – June
Flavourrich · meaty · earthy · umami
Typical price9–15 €/kg

What it is

The cut navarin is built on, and the one most likely to be sold cheap because it looks like offcuts. It is threaded with connective tissue that turns to gelatine, giving a sauce body no leaner cut can produce.

In the kitchen

Buy it on the bone, in thick rounds. Boned and diced it loses exactly the thing that makes it worth cooking.
