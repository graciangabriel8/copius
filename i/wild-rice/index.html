Wild rice

Riz sauvage

Latin nameZizania palustris
FamilyGrains & flours
OriginGreat Lakes, North America
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Toasty · Earthy · Smoky
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Not rice at all but an aquatic grass, and it is sacred to the Anishinaabe, who call it manoomin and still harvest it from canoes by knocking the grain into the boat with wooden sticks — a method protected by treaty.

In the kitchen

It takes forty-five minutes and is ready when the grains split open and curl. Undercooked it is simply inedible.
