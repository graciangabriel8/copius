Sichuan pepper

Poivre du Sichuan

Latin nameZanthoxylum simulans
FamilySpices
OriginSichuan, China
SeasonAll year
Flavournumbing · citrus · woody · hot
Typical price50–150 €/kg

What it is

Not a pepper at all but the husk of a citrus-family berry. Its molecule, sanshool, tricks the touch receptors of the lips into feeling a vibration — researchers measured the phantom buzz at around fifty hertz. Paired with chili it creates málà, Sichuan’s “numbing-hot”, a sensation more than a taste.

In the kitchen

Toast the husks dry, discard the gritty black seeds, grind coarsely. Start with a pinch — the tingle builds like applause.
