Boudin blanc

boudin blanc

Latin nameSus scrofa (forcemeat, milk-bound)
FamilyMeat
OriginRethel, Ardennes
SeasonNovember – January
Flavourmilky · delicate · mild · rich
Typical price12–22 €/kg

What it is

The Rethel version is said to date from 1626, when an officer of the king hiding in the Ardennes from Richelieu opened a charcuterie to make a living. It has held an IGP since 2001 and is one of the very few boudins blancs made with no starch and no breadcrumb at all — meat, fresh milk, whole eggs.

In the kitchen

It is already cooked; you are only warming it. Poach it or pan it in butter below 80 °C, turning gently — above that the milk-and-egg bind breaks and the sausage splits and weeps.

Goes with

Apple Butter Cream Black truffle Onion Calvados
