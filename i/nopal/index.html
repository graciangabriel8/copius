Nopal ✦

Nopal

Latin nameOpuntia ficus-indica
FamilyVegetables
OriginCentral Mexican highlands
SeasonMarch – June
FlavourSour · Green · Fresh
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

The paddle is not a leaf but a flattened stem — the spines are the leaves, reduced to needles — which is why it stores water like a reservoir and behaves like a vegetable rather than a green. It is the cactus of the Mexican coat of arms, the one the eagle stands on.

In the kitchen

Griddle it whole and dry over high heat until the bright green turns olive and the surface squeaks — that is the mucilage gone. Boiled instead, salt the water hard, drain the moment the colour changes and rinse cold, or you will be eating okra.

Goes with
