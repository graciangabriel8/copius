Koikuchi shoyu

Sauce soja koikuchi

Latin nameGlycine max, Triticum aestivum
FamilyCondiments
OriginChiba and Hyogo, Japan
SeasonAll year
Flavoursalty · umami · toasty · rich
Typical price5–12 €/L

What it is

Roughly equal parts soybean and roasted wheat, koikuchi is about four fifths of everything Japan brews, and the wheat is what separates it from Chinese soy sauce — it supplies the alcohol and the sweet, almost floral aromatics. Counterintuitively it is the less salty of the two standard grades: about 16 per cent against usukuchi’s 19.

In the kitchen

Its aromatics are volatile — add it at the end, or in two goes, because a sauce simmered from the start with koikuchi tastes only of salt by service. Once opened it oxidises in about a month at room temperature; keep it cold.
