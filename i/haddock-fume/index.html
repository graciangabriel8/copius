Smoked haddock

Haddock (églefin fumé)

Latin nameMelanogrammus aeglefinus (smoked)
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginAberdeenshire
Season (France)All year
FlavourSmoky · Salty · Marine · Mild
Typical price (France)18–28 €/kg

What it is

Fishwives in Findon, south of Aberdeen, hung lightly salted haddock in their chimneys over peat fires, and by the 1830s the result — finnan haddie — was selling in London. French kitchens borrowed the English name of the fish and made it mean the cure instead: ask for haddock in Paris and you are handed a smoked fillet.

In the kitchen

Poach it in milk barely trembling, eight to ten minutes, and keep the milk — it is the base of cullen skink and of any sauce you build after. Bright yellow fillets are dyed; a real cure is pale straw.
