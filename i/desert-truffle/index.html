Desert truffle ✦

Terfèze

Latin nameTerfezia claveryi
FamilyMushrooms
OriginSaharan and Arabian scrub
SeasonFebruary – April
FlavourEarthy · Mild · Nutty
Typical price25–60 €/kg

What it is

Terfezia is not a Tuber at all: it lives with a low desert shrub, Helianthemum, and swells after the winter storms, harvesters reading the cracks the growing fungus opens in the sand. Bedouin tradition holds that lightning makes the crop, and the thing itself is nearly odourless, firm as a potato, and sold by the sack rather than the gram.

In the kitchen

Peel it: the skin holds sand that no washing removes, and the flesh underneath needs plenty of fat and long, gentle cooking to soften. Treat it as a root vegetable — braised in a lamb tagine, or in samna with onion — and expect texture, not perfume.
