What it is

John Woodhouse, a Liverpool merchant sheltering at Marsala in 1773, fortified the local wine for the voyage home and found that England would buy it; a quarter-century later Nelson's fleet was ordering it by the pipe. The dry bottling, from Grillo grown on the western Sicilian plain, finishes on bitter almond — the note that reads as savoury depth in a pan sauce.

In the kitchen

Buy the bottle marked secco or vergine; the sweet one turns a scaloppine into dessert. Deglaze off the flame and reduce by half before any butter goes in, or the sauce will split.
