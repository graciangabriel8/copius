Pork belly

Poitrine de porc

Latin namePorc — poitrine
FamilyCuts
OriginFrance
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · meaty · umami · smoky
Typical price8–14 €/kg

What it is

Layers of fat and meat in alternating bands — the same cut that becomes bacon, pancetta and lardons depending on which country cures it. Chinese red-braising and French confit arrive at almost the same result by different routes.

In the kitchen

Dry the skin uncovered in the fridge overnight before roasting. It is the only reliable route to crackling.

Goes with

Soy sauce Star anise Garlic Ginger Cider vinegar Honey Thyme Black pepper
