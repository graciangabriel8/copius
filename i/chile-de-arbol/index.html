Chile de árbol

Chile de árbol

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)30–60 €/kg

What it is

The name means tree chilli, after the woody stem the plant builds rather than any relation to a tree. It keeps its bright red through drying where most chillies go brown, which is why it is the one reached for when a salsa has to look red as well as burn — 15,000 to 30,000 Scoville, ten times an ancho.

In the kitchen

Fry them whole in oil for ten seconds and then use the oil as much as the chilli — capsaicin is fat-soluble and most of it crosses over. Stems on and seeds in, or you have given up the only thing this chilli is for.
