Candied angelica

Angélique confite

Latin nameAngelica archangelica
FamilySweet pantry
OriginMarais poitevin, Niort
Season (France)All year
FlavourBitter · Musky · Resinous · Herbal
Typical price (France)50–100 €/kg

What it is

Angelica reached the Marais poitevin with monks who grew it as medicine, and the nuns of Niort turned the hollow stems into a confection in the eighteenth century, candied in grooved batons. It is a biennial cut in its second spring, when the stem is thick but has not yet gone woody towards the flower.

In the kitchen

Dice it small: the flavour is musky and resinous, and more than a teaspoon per kilo of dough takes over everything around it. Rinse and dry the batons before they go into a cake, or the surface sugar sends them straight to the bottom.
