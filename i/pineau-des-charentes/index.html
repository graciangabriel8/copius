Pineau des Charentes

Latin nameVitis vinifera
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginCharente, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Fruity · Honeyed · Warm
Typical price (France)14–24 €/L

What it is

Pineau is a mistelle, not a wine: unfermented Charentais grape must muted with young Cognac, so the sugar in the bottle is grape sugar that never met a yeast. Legend has it a sixteenth-century vigneron poured must into a cask still holding eau-de-vie and left the mistake in the cellar for years before tasting it.

In the kitchen

In a shellfish sauce add it early and give it a full minute of heat — the young Cognac tastes raw if it goes in at the end. Once opened it fades fast: refrigerate, and drink or cook it within a fortnight.
