Red palm oil (dendê) ✦

Huile de palme rouge (dendê)

Latin nameElaeis guineensis
FamilyFats & oils
OriginWest Africa & Bahia
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Rich · Warm
Typical price (France)8–16 €/L

What it is

Unrefined palm oil is the richest natural source of carotene there is, several times a carrot's, which is what makes it orange enough to stain a pot. It crossed the Atlantic with the slave trade and Bahian cooking still runs on it as dendê, while the refined white version — same fruit, no colour, no smell — goes into soap and biscuits.

In the kitchen

One tablespoon colours and flavours a whole pot; more turns the dish waxy and cloying. It is semi-solid at room temperature, so warm it gently — it does not need a high flame to go liquid.
