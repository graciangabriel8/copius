Conpoy ◆

Conpoy (Saint-Jacques séchées)

Latin nameMizuhopecten yessoensis (dried adductor)
FamilyFish
OriginHokkaido and the South China coast
SeasonAll year
Flavourumami · sweet · marine · rich
Typical price200–600 €/kg

What it is

Conpoy is graded like coinage, by the diameter of the disc, and the broad flat ones cost several times the broken grade — which most kitchens buy anyway, since the muscle is shredded before it is used. XO sauce, invented at the Peninsula hotel in Hong Kong in the 1980s, is in essence a way of eating conpoy by the spoonful.

In the kitchen

Steam them over water with a splash of Shaoxing for twenty to thirty minutes instead of soaking cold — they pull apart into threads. Keep the liquid underneath: it is a finished stock, not waste.

Goes with

Shaoxing wine Ginger Spring onion Chicken Rice Dried shiitake (donko) Jinhua ham Egg
