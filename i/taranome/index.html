Pousse d’aralia (taranome)

Latin nameAralia elata
FamilyVegetables
OriginEast Asian woodland
SeasonMarch – May
Flavournutty · resinous · bitter · buttery
Typical price80–150 €/kg

What it is

Taranome is the terminal bud of Aralia elata, a thorny tree, and a forager takes only that top bud if the tree is to shoot again the following spring. It is called the king of the sansai, and greenhouse-forced cut branches put it in Tokyo shops from December, months ahead of the April buds in the hills.

In the kitchen

Trim the brown scales at the base and fry it as tempura at 170°C for about ninety seconds, served with salt rather than dipping sauce — soy buries the resin note that is the whole point. Any bud longer than about 6 cm has set its fibres; braise those instead.
