Sherry vinegar

Vinaigre de Xérès

Latin nameVinagre de Jerez DOP
FamilyCondiments
OriginJerez, Andalusia
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · nutty · rich · woody
Typical price8–20 €/L

What it is

Aged in the solera system, the same cascade of stacked barrels used for the wine: each cask is partly drawn off and topped up from the one above, so a bottle contains a blend of many years and no single vintage exists.

In the kitchen

It is strong enough to use by the teaspoon. A few drops finish a lentil soup or a gazpacho better than a whole lemon.

Goes with

Olive oil Almond Ibérico ham Lentils Garlic Tomato Chickpeas Paprika
