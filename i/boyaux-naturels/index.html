Natural casings

Boyaux naturels

FamilyCuts of meat
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)10–25 €/kg

What it is

Casings are sold not by weight but by calibre in millimetres, and under their trade names: menu de mouton for merguez, menu de porc for the breakfast sausage, chaudin and baudruche for the big drying saucissons. The number commands everything downstream — a wider casing dries more slowly, so a charcutier chooses the skin before choosing the meat.

In the kitchen

They arrive packed in salt: rinse them, soak at least half an hour in tepid water, and run water through the inside so they slide onto the horn. Stuff them slack — a casing filled tight has nowhere to go when you twist the links, and it splits.
