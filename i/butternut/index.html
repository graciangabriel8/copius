Butternut squash

Butternut

Latin nameCucurbita moschata
FamilyVegetables
OriginMassachusetts, 1944
Season (France)September – January
FlavourSweet · Nutty · Earthy · Creamy
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

Unusually for a vegetable, it has a birth certificate: Charles Leggett bred it in Stow, Massachusetts in 1944, aiming for something sweeter than the squashes then grown. He said it was smooth as butter and sweet as a nut, and named it on the spot.

In the kitchen

The neck is solid flesh and the base is all seed. Cut them apart and treat them as two different vegetables.
