Sunflower seed

Graine de tournesol

Latin nameHelianthus annuus
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginNorth America
SeasonAll year
FlavourNutty · Toasty · Mild · Earthy
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Domesticated in what is now the United States some four thousand years ago, then taken to Russia, industrialised, and sold back to America as a Russian crop. The head follows the sun only while growing; a mature flower faces east and stops.

In the kitchen

Toast them and they stop tasting like birdseed. Untoasted they contribute texture and very little else.

Goes with

Honey T65 bread flour Yogurt Raisin Oats Dark chocolate Salt Pumpkin seed
