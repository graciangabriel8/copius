Buck’s-horn plantain

Plantain corne-de-cerf

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)October – April
Typical price (France)20–40 €/kg

What it is

It is a salt-tolerant coastal plant, which is where the faint saline edge comes from, and Italian growers sell the cultivated form as minutina or erba stella. That form — narrow antler-shaped leaves cut as a salad crop — is a table vegetable, not the broad medicinal plantain of lawns and verges.

In the kitchen

Cut it, do not pull it: the crown regrows three or four times across a winter, which is the whole economy of the crop. Dress it late — the leaves are crunchy and hold well, but salt collapses them within a few minutes.
