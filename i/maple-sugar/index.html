Maple sugar

Sucre d’érable

FamilySweet pantry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)35–70 €/kg

What it is

For most of its history maple was stored as sugar rather than syrup — hard cakes kept and travelled, and a liquid did neither before sealed tins. The Eastern Woodlands nations who developed sugaring made blocks; the can of syrup is the recent form.

In the kitchen

Swap it weight for weight with white sugar but drop the oven about 10 °C: it browns noticeably faster. It cakes hard in a damp cupboard, so keep it sealed and sieve it before creaming.
