Lardo

Lardo

Latin nameLardo di Colonnata IGP
FamilyMeat
OriginColonnata, Tuscany
SeasonAll year
Flavourrich · salty · herbal · creamy
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

Cured in basins carved from the same Carrara marble Michelangelo used, packed with salt, rosemary and garlic, and left for months. The marble's porosity and constant cool are the point — the quarrymen who invented it ate it on bread for lunch.

In the kitchen

Slice it almost transparent and lay it on warm bread or hot potatoes. The heat is what makes it melt and release its perfume.

Goes with

Rosemary T65 bread flour Black pepper Garlic Potato Fig Sage Olive oil
