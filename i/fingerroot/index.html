Fingerroot ✦

Krachai (gingembre digité)

Latin nameBoesenbergia rotunda
FamilySpices
OriginSoutheast Asia
Season (France)All year
FlavourPungent · Bitter · Citrusy · Warm
Typical price (France)10–25 €/kg

What it is

The rhizome grows as a bunch of slim fingers hanging off a central knob, which is why Malay calls it kunci, a key, and English calls it Chinese keys. Its bite is camphorous rather than hot — cineole and camphor carry it, not the gingerols that make ginger burn.

In the kitchen

Cut it into fine matchsticks rather than mincing it: in kaeng pa and fish curries it is meant to be chewed. It goes in with the paste at the start, not as a finishing aromatic.
