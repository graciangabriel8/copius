Sea cucumber

Holothurie séchée (concombre de mer)

FamilyShellfish & molluscs
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)150–400 €/kg

What it is

Chinese banquet cooking counts four sea treasures — abalone, shark fin, fish maw and this — and of the four it is the only one that tastes of almost nothing. It is bought for texture and for what it absorbs: no muscle, no bone, no blood, an echinoderm closer kin to a sea urchin than to any fish.

In the kitchen

Soak it three to five days in the fridge in changes of cold water, with a brief boil each day, and never let oil near it — a trace of grease turns the body wall to slime. Braise it in a stock that already tastes finished, because it takes everything from the liquid.
