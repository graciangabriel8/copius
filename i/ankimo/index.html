Monkfish liver (ankimo)

Foie de lotte (ankimo)

Latin nameLophius litulon
FamilyFish
OriginCold northern Japanese waters
SeasonNovember – February
Flavourrich · creamy · umami
Typical price40–90 €/kg

What it is

Monkfish liver is graded much like foie gras — the winter livers landed in Aomori run to a kilo and are sorted by colour before sale. In Japan it is not a fish course but a sakana, a thing eaten to make the sake taste better.

In the kitchen

Soak the liver in salted water, then draw out every vein with tweezers while it is still cold; the veins are what make it bitter. Roll it tight in foil, steam 30 minutes, chill overnight and slice it cold with ponzu.

Goes with

Ponzu Daikon Negi Sudachi Junmai sake Koikuchi shoyu Wasabi
