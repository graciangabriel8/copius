Herring

Hareng

Latin nameClupea harengus
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginNorth Atlantic & Baltic
Season (France)October – February
FlavourRich · Salty · Marine · Smoky
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

The fish that built cities. The Hanseatic League ran on salted herring, Amsterdam is said to be built on its bones, and a fourteenth-century Dutchman's method of gutting it at sea gave the Netherlands a two-century commercial advantage.

In the kitchen

Smoked, salted or soused, it wants something starchy and something sour alongside — potato and onion, or apple and cream.
