Kinako

Kinako (farine de soja grillé)

Latin nameGlycine max
FamilyPulses
OriginJapan
SeasonAll year
Flavournutty · toasty · mild · earthy
Typical price12–20 €/kg

What it is

Kinako is nothing but soybeans dry-roasted and milled, and the name means, flatly, yellow flour. The roast is the whole of it — the same beans boiled taste of very little, which is why kinako is a finishing ingredient and never a cooking one.

In the kitchen

Toast it thirty seconds in a dry pan before use; kinako that has sat on the shelf smells of cardboard and the heat brings it straight back. Cut it with sugar at four to one plus a good pinch of salt, or it clings to the tongue and dries the mouth.

Goes with

Adzuki bean Okinawan black sugar Mizuame Glutinous rice Matcha Honey
