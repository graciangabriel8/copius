Paddlefish caviar

Caviar de polyodon

FamilyRoe & caviar
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)0,7–1,2 €/g

What it is

The paddlefish is not a sturgeon at all: it sits in its own family, filters plankton through a rostrum nearly a third of its own length, and lives in the Mississippi basin. Its roe travels under the CITES species code SPA, and those three letters are how you tell a legitimate tin from one quietly calling itself something dearer.

In the kitchen

It is softer than sturgeon roe and goes flat within a day of opening, so buy the small tin. Its earthy side holds up better against warm potato and soured cream than against a plain blini.
