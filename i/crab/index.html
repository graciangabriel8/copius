Brown crab

Tourteau

Latin nameCancer pagurus
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginNortheast Atlantic
Season (France)May – October
FlavourSweet · Marine · Rich · Umami
Typical price (France)8–16 €/kg

What it is

Two entirely different foods live inside one shell: white claw meat, sweet and fibrous, and brown body meat, soft and intensely savoury. Most people only meet the white and never learn what the brown can do to a sauce.

In the kitchen

Buy it live and heavy for its size. A light crab has recently moulted and its shell is half full of water.
