Celery

Céleri branche

Latin nameApium graveolens
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean marshes
Season (France)July – November
FlavourFresh · Green · Salty
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

Greeks crowned athletes with celery leaves and planted it on graves; Victorians served it in crystal vases as a table luxury. Today it works anonymously in every stock — the third of the French mirepoix trinity.

In the kitchen

Its leaves are a seasoning in themselves — chop them like an herb over soups and salads.
