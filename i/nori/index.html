Nori

Latin namePyropia yezoensis
FamilySeaweed
OriginJapan & Korea
Season (France)All year
FlavourMarine · Umami · Toasty · Salty
Typical price (France)90–180 €/kg

What it is

When Japan’s nori harvests mysteriously collapsed after the war, it was a British scientist, Kathleen Mary Drew-Baker, whose research on seaweed life cycles saved the crop — she never visited Japan, yet fishermen there still honour her each April as the “Mother of the Sea”.

In the kitchen

Wave a sheet over a flame until it turns emerald and crisps. Crumbled into butter or over eggs, it adds the sea without the fish.
