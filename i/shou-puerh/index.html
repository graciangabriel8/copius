Shou pu-erh

Pu-erh cuit (shou)

Latin nameCamellia sinensis var. assamica
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginMenghai and Kunming, Yunnan
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Woody · Rich
Typical price (France)60–150 €/kg

What it is

Wet-piling, wo dui, was worked out in 1973 at the Kunming and Menghai factories to get in weeks the taste of a raw cake aged for decades: damp leaf is heaped a metre high under cloth and turned while the microbes hold the core near 50-60 °C. It is not an older tea than sheng but a different manufacture, and the result — beetroot, damp wood, forest floor — is closer to a savoury ingredient than a drink.

In the kitchen

Rinse twice: the first two pours of any ripe cake carry the smell of the pile and are thrown out. Then use it hot and strong, 10 g per litre, as half the braising liquid for pork belly or duck legs with star anise — it deepens colour as much as flavour.
