Dried barberry ✦

Épine-vinette séchée (zereshk)

Latin nameBerberis vulgaris
FamilySpices
OriginSouth Khorasan, Iran
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · tangy · fruity
Typical price20–40 €/kg

What it is

Almost all of Iran’s zereshk comes from one corner of South Khorasan around Qaen, where the seedless bush has been cultivated for roughly two centuries. The berries are dried in the shade rather than the sun, which is the whole reason they stay scarlet instead of going brown.

In the kitchen

Soak them ten minutes, then bloom them in butter for thirty seconds and no longer — a minute and they blacken and turn bitter. A pinch of sugar in the pan rounds the acidity without making them sweet.
