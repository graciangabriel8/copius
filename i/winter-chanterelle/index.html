Winter chanterelle

Chanterelle en tube

Latin nameCraterellus tubaeformis
FamilyMushrooms
OriginMossy conifer woods, northern Europe
SeasonSeptember – December
Flavourpeppery · woody · delicate
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

It runs months after the girolle has finished, on into the frosts, and grows so thick in the moss that a good patch is picked by the kilo rather than the handful. Hollow-stemmed and papery, it dries better than any other chanterelle and comes back in five minutes, which is how most of the Nordic crop is sold.

In the kitchen

There is nothing in it to sear: it collapses in seconds, so it goes in at the very end, onto a finished cream or a hot omelette. Dried and ground it works better as a seasoning than as a mushroom — a spoonful into a sauce carries the pepper note.
