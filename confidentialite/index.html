Seulement pour limiter le nombre de demandes, contre les abus : l’intérêt légitime à protéger le service, article 6-1-f du RGPD.

24 heures, puis elles sont effacées dans l’heure.

If you ask for a sign-in link

What is collected
Each time a link is requested: a fingerprint of your IP address and, if the address typed is well formed, a fingerprint of that email address with your IP address, whether or not it has access. When a link is issued to an address that has access, a fingerprint of the email address alone. Each time a link is opened: a fingerprint of your IP address. A fingerprint is a one-way calculation made with a secret key, never the addresses themselves. No message is sent to an address that has no access.
What for, and on what basis
Only to limit how many requests can be made, against abuse: the legitimate interest in protecting the service, article 6(1)(f) GDPR.
For how long
24 hours, then deleted within the hour.
