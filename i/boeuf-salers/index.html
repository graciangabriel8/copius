Salers beef

Bœuf Salers

Latin nameBos taurus (Salers)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginCantal, Auvergne
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Earthy · Rich
Typical price (France)18–35 €/kg

What it is

A Salers cow will not let her milk down without her calf beside her, which is why the herds that make Salers cheese still bring the calf to the parlour twice a day. The meat comes mostly from those cows at the end of their working life: dark, close-grained, and far more strongly flavoured than a young bull's.

In the kitchen

A côte three fingers thick, salted an hour ahead, grilled hard and then rested fifteen minutes off the heat — that close grain needs the rest more than it needs the fire. It is wasted as fillet; the flavour is in the muscles that worked.
