Rooibos

Rooibos

Latin nameAspalathus linearis
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginCederberg mountains, South Africa
Season (France)All year
FlavourWoody · Sweet · Warm
Typical price (France)25–50 €/kg

What it is

It is a legume, not a tea plant, and it grows commercially nowhere but the Cederberg — attempts to move it have failed on the soil and the fire cycle it depends on. Benjamin Ginsberg, a trader at Clanwilliam, is credited with curing the chopped shoots in barrels from 1904, which is what turned a local bush infusion into a product.

In the kitchen

It has so little tannin that it cannot go bitter: leave it an hour, boil it, reduce it — nothing happens but concentration. That is what makes it the infusion for milk, custard bases and brines, where black tea would turn astringent.
