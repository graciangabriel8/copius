Stuffed duck neck

cou de canard farci

Latin nameAnas platyrhynchos (neck, confit)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginGascony
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Meaty · Salty · Warm
Typical price (France)9–16 €/pièce

What it is

The neck skin is pulled off whole like a sleeve, sewn shut at one end, filled with a forcemeat of duck, pork and foie gras, then confited and kept under fat. It exists because a Gascon farm raising ducks for foie gras threw nothing away: the skin was the casing it already had.

In the kitchen

Lift it from the fat, wipe it, and sear it in a dry pan until the skin crisps — add no fat, there is plenty inside. Serve the slices warm on a salad or cold from the terrine, but never let it boil in a sauce.
