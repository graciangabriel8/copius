Hibiscus flower

Fleur d’hibiscus (karkadé)

Latin nameHibiscus sabdariffa
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginWest Africa & Mexico
SeasonAll year
FlavourSour · Tangy · Fruity · Floral
Typical price20–40 €/kg

What it is

Bissap in Dakar, karkadé in Cairo, agua de jamaica in Mexico City — the same dried calyx, brewed hot or steeped cold overnight, and the same deep red, cranberry-sour glass at the end of it. The colour is an anthocyanin that behaves like litmus: a squeeze of lime turns it scarlet, a pinch of bicarbonate pushes it towards violet.

In the kitchen

Steep it, never boil it — five minutes off the boil gives clean cranberry acidity, while a simmer turns it stewed and drying. Sweeten while it is still hot, since the acidity reads much sharper cold.

Goes with

White sugar Ginger Lime Mint Vanilla Raspberry Orange Cinnamon
