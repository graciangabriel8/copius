Rapeseed oil

Huile de colza

Latin nameBrassica napus
FamilyFats & oils
OriginEurope
Season (France)All year
FlavourGreen · Nutty · Mild · Fresh
Typical price (France)4–8 €/L

What it is

The original plant made an oil too high in erucic acid to eat safely, and it was sold as a machine lubricant. Canadian breeders bred that compound out in the 1970s and renamed the result canola — a new food invented by subtraction.

In the kitchen

Buy the cold-pressed version for dressings — it is grassy and bright yellow. The refined one is for heat and nothing else.
