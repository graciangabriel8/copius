Peanut oil

Huile d’arachide

Latin nameArachis hypogaea
FamilyFats & oils
OriginSouth America, via West Africa
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Nutty · Toasty · Delicate
Typical price (France)4–8 €/L

What it is

Its smoke point is high enough that it fries chips twice without breaking down, which is why chip shops and Chinese kitchens both settled on it. Refined, it carries almost none of the protein that triggers nut allergy.

In the kitchen

It can be strained and reused several times for deep frying, which no delicate oil tolerates. Filter it while still warm.
