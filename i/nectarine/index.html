Nectarine

Nectarine

Latin namePrunus persica var. nucipersica
FamilyFruit
OriginChina
SeasonJuly – September
Flavoursweet · fruity · floral · tangy
Typical price3–6 €/kg

What it is

Not a hybrid of anything — it is a peach with a single recessive gene that switches off the fuzz. Peach trees occasionally throw nectarines and nectarine trees throw peaches; growers have found both on the same branch.

In the kitchen

The smooth skin means it bruises where a peach would not show it. Buy them loose and lay them in a single layer.

Goes with

Basil Almond Vanilla Prosciutto Honey Mint Cream Raspberry
