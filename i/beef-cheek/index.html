Beef cheek

Joue de bœuf

Latin nameBos taurus (masseter)
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginThe chewing muscle of the jaw
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Rich · Umami
Typical price (France)12–18 €/kg

What it is

A muscle that worked every waking hour of the animal’s life, woven through with collagen as payment. Braised three hours it performs the great conversion: sinew into gloss, toughness into spoon-soft daube — the cut that taught cooks that time is an ingredient.

In the kitchen

Don’t trim the silverskin obsessively — that gelatine is the future body of your sauce. It is better the next day, and better still the day after.
