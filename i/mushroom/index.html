Champignon de Paris

Latin nameAgaricus bisporus
FamilyMushrooms
OriginCultivated in France
Season (France)All year
FlavourEarthy · Umami · Mild
Typical price (France)4–8 €/kg

What it is

Under Napoleon’s Paris, growers discovered that abandoned quarry tunnels — cool, damp, dark — were perfect for mushrooms, and the catacomb farms gave it its French name. The world’s most cultivated mushroom began underground in the capital of light.

In the kitchen

Don’t crowd the pan: mushrooms steam in company and brown in solitude.
