Shiro shoyu ✦

Sauce soja blanche (shiro)

Latin nameTriticum aestivum (fermented)
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginHekinan, Aichi
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Sweet · Mild
Typical price (France)14–24 €/L

What it is

Shiro shoyu inverts the recipe — roughly nine parts wheat to one of soybean — and brews for months rather than years, around Hekinan in Aichi. It comes out the colour of straw, which is the entire point: it seasons a clear broth or a chawanmushi without tinting it.

In the kitchen

It is as salty as any shoyu, so dose it like one. What it cannot do is last: add it at the end of cooking, keep the bottle cold and finish it within two months, because it browns and goes flat quickly once opened.
