What it is

Constantly confused with cumin, and in several languages the two names are almost identical — which has caused centuries of recipe mistranslation. Caraway is the sharper, more anise one, and it is the seed in rye bread and in every sauerkraut worth eating.

In the kitchen

It is the missing note in most home harissa and in cabbage dishes that taste flat. A pinch changes both completely.
