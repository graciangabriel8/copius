Blond chocolate

Chocolat blond

Latin nameTheobroma cacao
FamilySweet
OriginTain-l'Hermitage, Drome
SeasonAll year
Flavourtoasty · creamy · sweet · milky
Typical price18–30 €/kg

What it is

In 2004 Frederic Bau left white chocolate in a bain-marie during a demonstration and came back to something the colour of shortbread; Valrhona spent eight years making the accident repeatable and launched Dulcey in 2012. What happened was a Maillard reaction in a product with no cocoa solids at all - the browning is entirely milk.

In the kitchen

Treat it as white chocolate, only cooler - melt no higher than 45C, temper down to 26-27C and work at 28C. Its already-caramelised milk sugars catch fast, so build ganache at 35C and no hotter.

Goes with
