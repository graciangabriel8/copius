Manchego

FamilyDairy & eggs
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)20–35 €/kg

What it is

The zigzag pattern round its side is a fossil. Wheels were once pressed inside plaited esparto grass belts, and the weave printed itself into the rind; the moulds are plastic now but the pattern is required by the designation. Don Quixote's saddlebags carried it.

In the kitchen

Cut it in thin triangles from the wedge, never in cubes. With quince paste and a few almonds it is a finished course.
