Rib of beef

Côte de bœuf

Latin nameBos taurus (rib section)
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginThe rib cage, bone left in
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Rich · Umami · Toasty
Typical price (France)25–45 €/kg

What it is

The celebration cut: marbled enough to baste itself, big enough to demand company, always carved and shared. Dry-aged for a month or two, its edges develop the nutty, almost blue-cheese depth of enzymes at slow work — beef that has learned patience.

In the kitchen

Salt generously, sear on all faces including the fat edge, then finish low with a thermometer, not a timer. Rest it half its cooking time — the bone keeps it warm.
