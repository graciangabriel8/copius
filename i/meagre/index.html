Meagre

Maigre (ombrine)

Latin nameArgyrosomus regius
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginGironde estuary and Atlantic coast
Season (France)May – July
FlavourMild · Meaty · Marine
Typical price (France)12–22 €/kg

What it is

Sciaenids drum: the males vibrate muscles against the swim bladder, and spawning meagre in the Gironde can be heard from a boat thirty metres off. Fishermen used to locate the shoals by ear, leaning over the side.

In the kitchen

Low fat means it dries fast. Cut 3 cm steaks on the bone, salt them an hour ahead and roast at 140 °C until a skewer meets no resistance — treat it like veal, not like bass.
