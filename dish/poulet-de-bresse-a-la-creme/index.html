Signature dish

Bresse chicken with cream

Élisa Blanc, la Mère Blanc · 1883–1949 · Vonnas, Ain

The Mère Blanc's dish at Vonnas, and the one that made the Lyonnaise mères a destination rather than a local habit.

Why it works

The bird is cooked in its own fat and the cream goes in off the heat: above a simmer the cream would break, and the sauce is the cooking liquid rather than an addition to it.

What is in it

Bresse chicken Cream Churned butter
