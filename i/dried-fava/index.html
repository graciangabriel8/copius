Dried broad bean

Fève sèche

Latin nameVicia faba
FamilyPulses
OriginNear East
SeasonAll year
Flavourearthy · nutty · mild · bitter
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Egypt eats it at breakfast as ful medames, simmered overnight in a narrow-necked pot, and the dish is older than the pyramids. In Sicily and Puglia it is boiled to a purée called macco and served with wild greens.

In the kitchen

Buy them already skinned if you can. Peeling a kilo of soaked dried favas by hand is an afternoon's work.

Goes with

Olive oil Cumin Lemon Garlic Parsley Chili pepper Escarole Onion
