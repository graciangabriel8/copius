Broad bean

Fève

Latin nameVicia faba
FamilyVegetables
OriginNear East
Season (France)April – July
FlavourSweet · Earthy · Green · Mild
Typical price (France)4–7 €/kg

What it is

One of the oldest crops in cultivation, and the only vegetable Pythagoras forbade his followers to eat — nobody has ever satisfactorily explained why. In the Middle East it feeds breakfast as ful medames, a dish older than the pyramids.

In the kitchen

Double-pod anything past the first young ones: shell, blanch a minute, then slip each bean out of its grey jacket.
