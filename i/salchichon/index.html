Salchichón

salchichón

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)18–35 €/kg

What it is

Where chorizo takes pimentón, salchichón takes whole black peppercorns and nothing red at all — often the same meat, the same cellar, separated by that one decision. The Vic version, from Catalonia, carries an IGP and cures right through the cold winter of the Plana de Vic.

In the kitchen

Serve it at 18 to 20 °C, cut on a slight bias about 2 mm thick; fridge-cold, the fat is opaque and mute. Peel the casing back only as far as you are cutting.
