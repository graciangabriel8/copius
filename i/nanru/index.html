Red fermented bean curd (nanru)

Tofu fermenté rouge (nanru)

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

Nanru is bean curd cured under salt and huangjiu with red yeast rice, which dyes it through to the centre and pushes the ferment towards wine rather than towards sharpness. Cantonese and Hakka cooks reach for it as much for the colour as the taste: the red on nam yu pork ribs comes from the cheese itself, not from any added dye.

In the kitchen

Mash a cube with a spoonful of its own brine before it meets the meat — dropped in whole it never disperses and leaves pockets of salt. Two cubes season a kilo of pork belly, and no further salt is needed.
