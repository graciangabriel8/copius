Goose

Oie

Latin nameAnser anser domesticus
FamilyMeat
OriginEurope & Egypt
SeasonNovember – January
Flavourrich · meaty · umami · earthy
Typical price14–25 €/kg

What it is

The Christmas bird of Europe before the turkey crossed the Atlantic, and geese were once walked to market in flocks with tar on their feet to protect them on the road. A roasting goose renders close to a litre of fat.

In the kitchen

Prick the skin all over and pour off the fat three or four times during roasting. Keep every drop of it.
