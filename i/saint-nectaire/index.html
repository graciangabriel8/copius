What it is

It ripens on rye straw in volcanic cellars, and takes on the cellar: mushroom, damp stone, hazelnut. It reached Versailles through Marshal de Senectaire, who served it to Louis XIV — one of the few French cheeses named after the courtier who marketed it rather than the village that made it.

In the kitchen

Look for the green casein plate on the rind: oval means farmhouse, square means dairy. The difference is real.
