Ancho chilli

Piment ancho

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–70 €/kg

What it is

Ancho means wide, and the name says nothing more than that: it is a poblano left to ripen red on the plant and dried flat. Hold one up to a lamp — if the light through the skin is red you have an ancho, and if it is brown you have a mulato, a different strain of the same pod that tastes of chocolate rather than raisin.

In the kitchen

Press it flat on a dry pan for fifteen seconds a side and no more: the moment it smells toasted it is one breath from bitter, and burnt chilli cannot be diluted back out of a mole. Soak the toasted pods twenty minutes in hot water, then taste that water before you blend with it — it is often bitter, and stock is the safer choice.
