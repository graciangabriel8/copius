Salted butter

Beurre demi-sel

Latin nameBeurre demi-sel
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginBrittany, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Creamy · Rich · Buttery
Typical price (France)9–15 €/kg

What it is

Brittany was exempt from the gabelle, the salt tax that crushed the rest of France, so Breton dairies salted their butter freely while everyone else could not afford to. A tax boundary drawn centuries ago still divides French breakfast tables.

In the kitchen

Reduce the salt elsewhere in the recipe, especially in baking. Demi-sel carries enough to matter in a cake.
