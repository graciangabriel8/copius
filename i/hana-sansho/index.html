Hana-sanshō (sanshō flower buds) ✦

Fleurs de sanshō

Latin nameZanthoxylum piperitum
FamilySpices
OriginNara and Hida, Japan
SeasonApril
Flavourcitrus · numbing · floral · woody
Typical price500–1200 €/kg

What it is

Sanshō trees are male or female, and only the male ones flower without setting fruit — those unopened clusters are hana-sanshō, on the market for about two weeks in April and then gone. A single tree gives a handful, which is why a spring sukiyaki of Hida beef and flower buds is priced by the gram rather than by the portion.

In the kitchen

Add them off the heat, thirty seconds before serving — the tingle is volatile and a simmer strips it out. To keep them, cook the buds down in soy, sake and mirin as tsukudani and freeze in tablespoon portions; they hold their perfume a year.

Goes with
