Signature dish

Kir Breton

Dominique Crenn · 1965 · Atelier Crenn, San Francisco

An edible version of the Breton aperitif served as the first bite at Atelier Crenn: a thin white chocolate and cocoa butter sphere filled with apple cider and topped with crème de cassis.

Why it works

Cocoa butter melts just below body temperature, so the shell gives way on the tongue instead of being chewed and releases the cider it held as liquid in a single burst.

What is in it

Apple White chocolate Cocoa butter Blackcurrant
