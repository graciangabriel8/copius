Pike

Brochet

Latin nameEsox lucius
FamilyFish
OriginNorthern hemisphere fresh water
SeasonOctober – March
Flavourdelicate · mild · sweet · earthy
Typical price15–25 €/kg

What it is

Lyon built a dish around its worst feature: pike is riddled with fine forked bones, so cooks pounded the flesh, sieved it and bound it into quenelles. The bones are the reason the technique exists.

In the kitchen

Do not attempt a plain fillet unless someone very skilled has boned it. Quenelles or a terrine are the safe routes.
