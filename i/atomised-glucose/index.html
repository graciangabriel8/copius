Atomised glucose

Glucose atomisé

FamilySweet pantry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)6–12 €/kg

What it is

It is glucose syrup sprayed into a hot chamber and dried in flight, so what lands is a powder of the same dextrins with the water gone. Confectioners buy it by dextrose equivalent — around DE 21 for sorbets, higher when they want sweetness — and it is the DE, not the weight, that says how it will behave.

In the kitchen

Use it to raise the dry matter of a sorbet without raising sweetness: replacing 3 to 5 % of the sugar with DE 21 powder gives body and stops the ice going coarse. Sift it into the dry sugars before it meets any liquid, or it lumps instantly.
