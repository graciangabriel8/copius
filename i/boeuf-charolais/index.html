Charolais beef

Bœuf Charolais

Latin nameBos taurus
FamilyMeat
OriginCharolles, Burgundy
SeasonAll year
Flavourmeaty · rich · mild · umami
Typical price15–30 €/kg

What it is

Burgundy registered the breed early — the first Charolais herd book opened at Nevers in 1864 — and it has since gone to dozens of countries, almost always as a sire, bought to put muscle on other people's cows rather than to be eaten where it lands. At home it is a carcass breed with white fat and broad, coarse-grained muscles that reward long slow wet cooking far more than a hot fast fire.

In the kitchen

The coarse grain is a virtue in a braise: cut paleron or gîte into pieces at least 5 cm across so the fibres have something to hold together, and keep the pot at 85 °C for four hours rather than boiling it. Smaller cubes shred to threads long before the collagen has finished.
