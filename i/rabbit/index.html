Rabbit

Lapin

Latin nameOryctolagus cuniculus
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginIberian peninsula
SeasonAll year
FlavourDelicate · Mild · Meaty · Sweet
Typical price12–18 €/kg

What it is

Spain may be named after it — one theory derives Hispania from a Phoenician word for the land of hyraxes, the sailors having mistaken rabbits for something familiar. Monks farmed them in medieval France as a Lenten loophole.

In the kitchen

The saddle and the legs cook completely differently — the saddle in fifteen minutes, the legs in an hour. Separate them.
