Tie Guan Yin oolong

Oolong Tie Guan Yin

FamilyTea & infusions
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)80–200 €/kg

What it is

The name means Iron Goddess of Mercy, and legend has it that a farmer in Anxi who kept a neglected temple of Guanyin swept was shown a single shoot in a rock crevice in a dream. The modern style is rolled into tight balls and barely oxidised, so a leaf sold as a dark tea a century ago now brews closer to a green.

In the kitchen

Infuse 6 g in 150 ml at 95 °C and reuse the ball five or six times — the orchid aroma peaks on the second and third infusions, not the first. To scent a cream, hold the leaves in it at 60 °C for 20 minutes and never let it boil.
