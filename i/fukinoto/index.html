Fukinoto (butterbur bud) ✦

Bourgeon de pétasite (fukinoto)

Latin namePetasites japonicus
FamilyVegetables
OriginJapan, snowmelt ground
SeasonFebruary – April
Flavourbitter · herbal · earthy · fresh
Typical price60–120 €/kg

What it is

Fukinoto is the flower bud of the butterbur, up out of half-thawed ground before the plant has put out a single leaf; once the flowers open the bud is gone, and what stands there weeks later is fuki, the stalk vegetable. Its bitterness comes with pyrrolizidine alkaloids, which is why the traditional handling is a boil and a long soak rather than a rinse.

In the kitchen

Halve the buds, drop them into salted boiling water for two minutes, then leave them fifteen in cold water — that takes the harsh edge and keeps the aromatic bitterness. For tempura, skip the boil and fry them whole at 170 °C; the oil does the same job.

Goes with

Miso White sugar Hon-mirin Sesame Walnut Salt
