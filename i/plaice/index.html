Plaice

Carrelet

Latin namePleuronectes platessa
FamilyFish
OriginNorth Atlantic & North Sea
SeasonApril – September
Flavourdelicate · sweet · mild · marine
Typical price8–15 €/kg

What it is

It is born symmetrical, swimming upright like any fish, and then one eye migrates across the skull over a few weeks until both sit on the same side. The orange spots are unmistakable and fade within hours of death.

In the kitchen

It is thin and cooks in three or four minutes. Flour, butter, lemon — anything more elaborate buries it.

Goes with

Butter Lemon Capers Parsley T55 flour Shrimp Black pepper Cream
