Omija (schisandra berry) ✦

Baie de schisandra (omija)

Latin nameSchisandra chinensis
FamilyTea & infusions
OriginMountain forests of Korea and Manchuria
SeasonAugust – September
FlavourSour · Bitter · Fruity · Warm
Typical price40–80 €/kg

What it is

Its Chinese name, wu wei zi, means five-flavour berry: the skin sour, the flesh sweet, the seeds bitter and pungent, the whole faintly salty. Korean cooks never boil it — hot water drags tannin out of the seeds and the clear red goes muddy and harsh.

In the kitchen

Infuse the whole dried berries in cold water overnight, about 30 g to the litre, then strain without pressing — crushing the fruit releases exactly the seed bitterness you spent the night avoiding.
