Avocado

Avocat

Latin namePersea americana
FamilyFruit
OriginCentral Mexico
SeasonAll year
Flavourcreamy · nutty · mild · buttery
Typical price4–9 €/kg

What it is

It evolved to be swallowed whole by giant ground sloths and other megafauna that have been extinct for ten thousand years. Without them it should have died out too — it survives because humans took over the job of moving the seed.

In the kitchen

The stone does nothing to stop guacamole browning. Only acid and keeping air off the surface actually work.
