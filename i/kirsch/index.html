Kirsch

Kirsch

Latin namePrunus avium
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginFougerolles, Alsace and the Black Forest
Season (France)All year
FlavourFruity · Bitter · Warm
Typical price (France)25–50 €/L

What it is

The stones are crushed in with the fruit and ferment alongside it: amygdalin from the kernels becomes benzaldehyde in the still, and that bitter-almond note is what no cherry liqueur has. Fougerolles in the Haute-Saone holds an appellation of its own for its kirsch, and it takes something like ten kilos of cherries to fill a bottle.

In the kitchen

In fondue it is mechanics rather than flavour - a spoonful stirred in with the cornflour is what traditionally keeps the melted cheese loose instead of stringy. In a pate a bombe or a syrup, add it once the pan is off the heat, since most of the aroma leaves within the first minute of boiling.
