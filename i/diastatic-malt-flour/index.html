Diastatic malt flour

Farine de malt diastasique

Latin nameHordeum vulgare
FamilyGrains
OriginMaltings of northern Europe
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · toasty · nutty
Typical price10–20 €/kg

What it is

Diastatic and non-diastatic malt are the same sprouted barley; the only difference is the kiln, and somewhere above 60 °C the amylases die and what is left is a flavouring. Active malt is sold by degrees Lintner, a number that measures how fast it converts starch into the sugar the dough has not yet made for itself.

In the kitchen

0.1 to 0.5% of the flour weight is the entire useful range — overdose it and the crumb goes gummy while the crust darkens long before the loaf is baked through. Leave it out of long-fermented dough, which has already made its own sugar.
