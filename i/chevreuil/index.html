Roe deer

Chevreuil

Latin nameCapreolus capreolus
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginEuropean woodland edge
Season (France)September – January
FlavourDelicate · Mild · Meaty · Earthy
Typical price (France)25–45 €/kg

What it is

The roe is the only hoofed animal in Europe with delayed implantation: it mates in July and the embryo lies dormant until December, so the fawn arrives in spring. The carcass is small — twenty kilos or less — and the meat correspondingly fine-grained and barely gamey.

In the kitchen

A saddle or a loin needs four minutes a side and a rest, no more. The long juniper-and-red-wine marinade that red deer expects bruises and sours it — roe is better with butter and pepper alone.
