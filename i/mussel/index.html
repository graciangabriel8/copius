Moule

Latin nameMytilus edulis
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginNorth Atlantic
Season (France)September – February
FlavourBriny · Marine · Sweet · Umami
Typical price (France)3–6 €/kg

What it is

Bouchot mussels are grown on wooden posts driven into tidal flats, a method a shipwrecked Irishman is said to have devised in the Bay of Aiguillon in 1235. The posts keep them off the sand, so they carry no grit.

In the kitchen

Discard any that stay open when tapped before cooking, and any that stay shut after. Both rules matter.
