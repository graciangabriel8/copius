Scarole

Latin nameCichorium endivia var. latifolium
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean
Season (France)September – December
FlavourBitter · Mild · Earthy · Fresh
Typical price (France)1,50–3 €/pièce

What it is

The broad-leaved sister of frisée, sturdy enough that southern Italy treats it as a cooking green rather than a salad — wilted with garlic, anchovy and raisins, or folded into a Neapolitan Christmas pie.

In the kitchen

Use the dark outer leaves for the pot and keep the pale heart for the salad bowl. They are two different vegetables.
