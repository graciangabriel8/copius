Scallop coral

FlavourBriny · Bitter · Rich · Marine
Typical price (France)5–12 €/kg

What it is

The great scallop is a simultaneous hermaphrodite, and the comma of coral is one organ carrying both halves: the orange ovary and the cream-white testis fused along a single curve. American boats shuck it off at sea and throw it away; French kitchens buy it by weight and dry it for powder.

In the kitchen

Salt it, dry it at 70 °C until brittle and grind it — a spoonful of coral powder gives a sauce more scallop flavour than the muscle ever will. Cooked whole it goes grainy, so thirty seconds in a hot pan and no more.
