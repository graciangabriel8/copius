Longan

Longane

FamilyFruits
Season (France)July – September
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

Its Chinese name, longyan, means dragon eye: peel one and a ring of translucent flesh stares back around a black seed. Dried, it darkens and turns smoky-sweet, at which point it leaves the fruit bowl for the stockpot and the pharmacy drawer.

In the kitchen

Dried longan wants twenty minutes of soaking and then a slow simmer: it releases its sweetness into a broth without scorching. Fresh, shake the bunch — a rattle means the flesh has shrunk away from the shell and the fruit is a week too old.
