Tarbais bean

Haricot tarbais

Latin namePhaseolus vulgaris
FamilyLegumes & pulses
OriginHautes-Pyrénées, France
Season (France)All year
FlavourCreamy · Mild · Earthy · Nutty
Typical price (France)15–28 €/kg

What it is

Traditionally grown climbing up maize stalks, which act as living stakes — the two crops were planted together for two centuries. Its skin is unusually fine, so it absorbs the fat of a cassoulet without disintegrating, which is why the dish specifies it.

In the kitchen

Soak twelve hours, then start it in cold water and bring it up slowly. Dropping beans into boiling water splits every skin.
