Rhubarb

Rhubarbe

Latin nameRheum rhabarbarum
FamilyFruit
OriginSiberia & China
SeasonApril – June
Flavoursour · fruity · fresh · grassy
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Botanically a vegetable, legally a fruit — a US court ruled it so in 1947, since it was eaten as one. In Yorkshire’s “rhubarb triangle”, forced stalks grow in pitch-dark sheds and are harvested by candlelight; growers say you can hear them creak as they rise.

In the kitchen

Never the leaves — they are toxic. Roast the stalks rather than stewing them: they keep their shape, their colour, and their proud acidity.
