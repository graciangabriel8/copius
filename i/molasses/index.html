Molasses

Mélasse

Latin nameMelassum
FamilySweet
OriginCaribbean
SeasonAll year
Flavourbitter · rich · smoky · earthy
Typical price5–10 €/kg

What it is

What is left after sugar crystals are spun out, and it was the raw material of rum and of the triangular trade. In 1919 a storage tank burst in Boston and sent a wave of it through the streets, killing twenty-one people.

In the kitchen

Blackstrap is the third boiling and is genuinely bitter. For baking you almost always want the lighter grade.

Goes with

Ginger Dark chocolate Rye flour Clove Cinnamon Butter Coffee Orange
