The dish that changed something · c. 2016

Trompe-l’œil fruit

Cédric Grolet · 1985 · Le Meurice, Paris

A dessert moulded and painted as the fruit it is made of, cut open to show it is not one. The first pastry judged as much by photograph as by taste, for better and for worse.

Why it works

The shell is thin enough to give under a spoon and flavoured with the fruit it imitates, so the eye is wrong and the mouth is right.

What is in it

Hazelnut White sugar Vanilla
