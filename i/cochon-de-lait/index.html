Suckling pig

Cochon de lait

Latin nameSus scrofa domesticus
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginFarmyards across Europe
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Buttery · Mild · Meaty
Typical price (France)14–25 €/kg

What it is

A piglet taken at four to six weeks, still on the sow, has collagen that has not yet set into tough connective tissue and skin thin enough to blister into glass. In Segovia the roasters carve the finished animal with the edge of a plate to prove how tender it is, then smash the plate on the floor.

In the kitchen

Dry the skin uncovered in the fridge overnight, roast skin-down under a weight, then turn it and give it fifteen minutes at 240 °C to finish. Damp skin steams instead of blistering and stays leathery whatever you do afterwards.
