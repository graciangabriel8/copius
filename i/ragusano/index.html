Ragusano

Latin nameRagusano DOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginHyblaean plateau, Sicily
Season (France)All year
FlavourGreen · Pungent · Salty · Umami
Typical price (France)22–35 €/kg

What it is

For centuries it sold as caciocavallo ragusano, named for the way the blocks were hung in pairs a cavaddu, astride a beam. Taking its DOP in 1996 meant dropping the word caciocavallo, so a cheese traded since the fourteenth century changed its name in order to keep its origin.

In the kitchen

Buy it aged past a year and grate it; young, it stays elastic and goes rubbery in the pan. Its grassy bitterness wants sweetness against it — long-cooked tomato, or roast onion.
