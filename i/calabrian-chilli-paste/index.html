Calabrian chilli paste

Crème de piment de Calabre

Latin nameCapsicum annuum
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginCalabria, Italy
SeasonAll year
FlavourHot · Fruity · Peppery
Typical price20–35 €/kg

What it is

Calabria took up the American capsicum harder than anywhere else in Italy and bred it into local cultivars with names like naso di cane, dog nose, for the blunt angular pod. The household paste is only those pods, salt and olive oil — no vinegar, no fermentation, which is why it reads as fruit before it reads as heat.

In the kitchen

It carries in fat, so melt a teaspoon into warm oil or butter at the start of a sauce rather than stirring it in at the end; added off the heat it stays a raw stripe of chilli. One teaspoon seasons a pan of pasta for four.
