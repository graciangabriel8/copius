What it is

Ugni blanc makes a wine too thin and too acid to drink, around nine percent, which is precisely what the charentais still wants - and it is now very nearly all that Cognac plants. The angels' share, two or three percent of each cask lost to the air every year, feeds a black fungus called Baudoinia on the town's walls, so the ageing cellars can be found by their soot.

In the kitchen

Flambe off the heat and away from the extractor: pour, let it warm for a few seconds, then light it, because cold spirit hitting a hot pan will not catch. In a sauce, reduce it almost to dry before the stock goes in, or the finished sauce keeps a raw spirit edge that nothing later will cover.
