Buckwheat flour

Farine de sarrasin

Latin nameFagopyrum esculentum
FamilyGrains
OriginYunnan, China
SeasonAll year
Flavourearthy · bitter · nutty · toasty
Typical price3–7 €/kg

What it is

Brittany calls it blé noir, black wheat, though it is neither wheat nor a grass — it is a relative of rhubarb and sorrel. It grew where wheat would not, on poor acid soil, which is exactly why Brittany built a cuisine on it.

In the kitchen

It has no gluten, so a galette batter needs resting overnight for the starch to swell and hold together in the pan.

Goes with

Buttermilk Egg Salted butter Prosciutto Comté Honey Apple Cider vinegar
