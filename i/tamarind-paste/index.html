Tamarind paste

Pâte de tamarin

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)6–12 €/kg

What it is

The name comes from the Arabic tamr hindi, date of India, although the tree is African - medieval traders carried it east and the misnomer travelled with it. Its sourness is mostly tartaric acid, the acid of grapes rather than of lemons, which is why it reads round and deep where citrus reads sharp.

In the kitchen

Block tamarind is soaked twenty minutes in hot water and pushed through a sieve; the jarred concentrate is two to three times stronger and much darker, so start at a third of the quantity and taste. It thickens as it reduces - add it early in a curry for body, late in a dressing for edge.
