Cotechino

cotechino

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)December – January
Typical price (France)10–18 €/kg

What it is

The story goes that it was invented in 1511, during the siege of Mirandola by Julius II, when a cook to the Pico family packed the last pigs into casings cut from trotter skin so the meat would keep. Whatever its origin, the rind is the point: it dissolves into gelatin over hours of poaching, which no other boiling sausage does.

In the kitchen

Wrap it in muslin, start it in cold water and hold it at a bare tremble for two and a half to three hours; at a rolling boil the casing bursts and the gelatin you cooked for goes into the water.
