Skate liver

Foie de raie

FamilyFish & seafood
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

It melts at barely above room temperature and turns rancid within a day, so it never reaches a display case: it is ordered from the fishmonger the morning the boat lands. Where cod liver is cured and tinned, this one is used fresh and almost pouring, closer to a sauce than to an organ.

In the kitchen

Poach it whole in a court-bouillon sharp with vinegar, five minutes at a bare tremble, and lift it before it slumps. Or whisk it raw into a beurre noisette off the heat — it thickens like an egg yolk, and splits the same way if it boils.
