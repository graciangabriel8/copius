What it is

Eight-thousand-year-old jars in Georgia still carry wine residue — humans domesticated the grape before writing, the wheel or most cities. The table grape is the sober sibling of history’s most storied fruit.

In the kitchen

Freeze a bunch on a summer day: frozen grapes are tiny sorbets. Roasted, they turn jammy and love cheese.
