Black radish

Radis noir

FamilyVegetables
Season (France)October – February
Typical price (France)3–5 €/kg

What it is

Coal-black outside, bone-white within, and considerably fiercer than its pink cousin. Central European kitchens grated it with goose fat and salt as a winter relish, and pharmacists sold it for the liver — a claim it still carries in French health-food shops.

In the kitchen

Salt the grated flesh for twenty minutes and squeeze it out. That draws off the bitterness and leaves the clean heat.
