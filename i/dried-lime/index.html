Dried lime

Citron noir séché

Latin nameCitrus aurantiifolia
FamilySpices
OriginPersian Gulf
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · bitter · earthy · musky
Typical price30–60 €/kg

What it is

Limes boiled in brine and dried in the sun until they are hollow, light and almost black — loomi in the Gulf, limoo omani in Iran. The fermentation that happens as they dry turns the fresh acidity into something dusty and slightly bitter.

In the kitchen

Pierce it twice and drop it in whole, then remove it before serving. Ground, it clouds the dish and turns aggressively bitter.

Goes with

Lamb Rice Turmeric Onion Chickpeas Cilantro / Coriander Cinnamon Saffron
