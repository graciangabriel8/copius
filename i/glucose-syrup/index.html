Glucose syrup

Sirop de glucose

Latin nameGlucosum liquidum
FamilySweet pantry
OriginEurope, 1811
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Mild · Delicate · Milky
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

Invented by a Russian chemist during the Napoleonic blockade, when cane sugar could not reach the continent — he made it from potato starch. Confectioners use it not for sweetness but to stop other sugars crystallising.

In the kitchen

It is what keeps sorbet scoopable and caramel smooth. A spoonful is a technical decision, not a sweetening one.
