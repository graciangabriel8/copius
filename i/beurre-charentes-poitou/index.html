Charentes-Poitou butter

Beurre Charentes-Poitou

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)12–28 €/kg

What it is

The appellation is built on maize: it must make up at least half of the cows' daily base ration, and never less than 1.5 kg of dry matter even at pasture. The specification credits that diet with the saturated fats that keep the butter firm yet malleable, which is why pastry cooks laminate with it. The cream is ripened for at least 16 hours at 7–21 °C, long enough to build lactones, which the specification ties to fruity notes, and the wider range of compounds behind its hazelnut and fresh-cream taste.

In the kitchen

Use it where the butter has to hold: puff pastry, sablés, a galette dough. Let it lose its fridge chill for a few minutes before laminating - it is firm, not brittle. It is also sold as 'Beurre des Charentes' or 'Beurre des Deux-Sèvres': the same appellation.
