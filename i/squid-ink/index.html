Squid ink

Encre de seiche

Latin nameSepia officinalis (ink)
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginMediterranean coasts
SeasonAll year
FlavourMarine · Briny · Umami · Rich
Typical price60–120 €/kg

What it is

The cephalopod’s smoke bomb, turned into the kitchen’s most dramatic pigment. Venice stirs it into risotto nero, Catalonia into arròs negre — dishes that arrive black as a new moon and taste, against all appearance, of gentle sea and sweet rice.

In the kitchen

A teaspoon suffices for a whole pan of rice; add it with the stock, not at the end, so the brininess cooks in and mellows.
