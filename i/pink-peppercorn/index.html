Pink peppercorn

Baies roses

Latin nameSchinus terebinthifolius
FamilySpices
OriginBrazil, grown on Réunion
Season (France)All year
FlavourFruity · Resinous · Floral · Sweet
Typical price (France)60–120 €/kg

What it is

Not a pepper but a cashew relative, which is why the FDA banned imports in 1982 over allergy fears; France protested on behalf of its Réunion growers, produced the toxicology and had the ban lifted. Almost all the aroma sits in the brittle pink shell, and the seed inside is a small hard pip that gives nothing.

In the kitchen

Crush them between your fingers over the finished plate — a mill shatters the shell to dust and a hot pan kills them outright, the aroma being a volatile resin that goes at the first real heat. Anyone allergic to cashew or pistachio should leave them alone.
