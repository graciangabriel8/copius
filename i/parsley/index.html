What it is

Greeks planted parsley on graves and crowned victors with it at the Nemean games — an herb of both mourning and triumph. French cooking made it a daily workhorse: half the savoury dishes of the repertoire end in a flurry of chopped parsley.

In the kitchen

Flat-leaf for flavour, curly for looks. The stems carry more taste than the leaves — tie them into every stock.
