Foie gras ◆

Foie gras

Latin nameAnser & Anas (foie)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginEgypt of the pharaohs; Alsace & the Southwest
SeasonAll year
FlavourRich · Buttery · Sweet · Delicate
Typical price50–90 €/kg

What it is

Reliefs at Saqqara show Egyptians fattening geese 4,500 years ago; Rome fed them figs and called the delicacy iecur ficatum — “fig liver” — and ficatum became foie, fegato, hígado: the very word for liver in Romance languages descends from this dish.

In the kitchen

Terrine barely set and eaten with a spoon’s edge, or escalopes seared in a dry, screaming pan for a minute a side — the fat it releases is a sauce in itself. Sweetness beside it, never sugar on it.

Goes with

Fig Quince Honey Grape Black pepper
