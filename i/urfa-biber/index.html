Urfa biber

Piment d’Urfa

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

For a week or more the peppers are laid out in the sun by day and wrapped tight in cloth at night, so the moisture drawn to the surface is pushed back into the flesh and the fruit ferments as it dries. No smoke ever touches them: the tobacco, raisin and cocoa are made by that sweating alone.

In the kitchen

It is sold oiled and salted, so taste it before you salt the dish. Warm a spoonful in oil barely above hand temperature rather than frying it — past a simmer the raisin note goes and only ash is left.
