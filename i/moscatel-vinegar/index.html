Moscatel vinegar

Vinaigre de Moscatel

Latin nameVitis vinifera
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginAndalusia, Spain
Season (France)All year
FlavourSour · Sweet · Floral
Typical price (France)8–18 €/L

What it is

Muscat of Alexandria is among the oldest grapes still farmed, and in Andalusia its wine is sweet enough that vinegar made from it keeps a residue of sugar the acetobacter never reaches. The sherry vinegar rules give it a category of its own, aged in the same soleras as the rest.

In the kitchen

It carries sugar, so it glazes: reduce it by half in the pan after searing foie gras or duck and it naps on its own. In a vinaigrette use less oil than usual — three to one is plenty.
