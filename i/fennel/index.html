Fennel

Fenouil

Latin nameFoeniculum vulgare
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean basin
SeasonMay – October
Flavouranise · fresh · sweet
Typical price3–5 €/kg

What it is

The plain of Marathon takes its name from the Greek word for fennel, which grew wild there — the famous run was named after a field of it. Prometheus, myth says, smuggled fire to humans inside a fennel stalk.

In the kitchen

Raw and shaved, it crunches with licorice freshness; braised, it melts and turns gentle. Keep the fronds as a free herb.

Goes with

Orange Salmon Olive oil Parmesan Lemon Olive Sardine
