Yellow pectin

Pectine jaune

Latin nameHigh-methoxyl pectin (E440i)
FamilyTexture agents
OriginCitrus peel and apple pomace
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Fruity
Typical price (France)50–100 €/kg

What it is

This is the slow-set high-methoxyl grade, and slow is the point: it will not gel until the acid goes in, which is why a pâte de fruits is cooked to 106-107 °C, taken off the heat, given its acid last and poured before it can catch in the pan. Once that gel forms it is permanent — reheating a pâte de fruits melts nothing and only cooks it darker.

In the kitchen

It needs both walls to stand: at least 65 % sugar and a final pH near 3.4, so make the acid up as equal parts citric acid and hot water and stir in 8 to 10 g of that solution per kilo at the last second. Add the acid early and it sets in the pot.
