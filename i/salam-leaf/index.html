Indonesian bay leaf (salam) ✦

Feuille de salam

Latin nameSyzygium polyanthum
FamilyHerbs
OriginJava and Sumatra
SeasonAll year
Flavoursour · herbal · mild · woody
Typical price15–30 €/100 g

What it is

It is a myrtle, cousin to the clove and the guava, and has nothing botanically to do with the laurel whose name it was given by Dutch colonial cooks. The taste follows the family, not the label: faintly sour and astringent, closer to weak tea than to the camphor of a European bay.

In the kitchen

Two or three whole leaves per kilo of meat, dropped in at the start of a long simmer. Dried, it is almost odourless in the hand, so trust the count rather than your nose — and never substitute European bay, which rewrites the dish.

Goes with

Lemongrass Galangal Turmeric Makrut lime leaf Coconut milk Shallot Candlenut Tamarind paste
