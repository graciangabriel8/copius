Quail

Caille

Latin nameCoturnix coturnix
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginEurasia & Africa
SeasonAll year
FlavourDelicate · Meaty · Sweet · Earthy
Typical price3–5 €/pièce

What it is

The Israelites in Exodus are fed on quail driven in by the wind, which matches the real thing — migrating quail cross the Mediterranean in exhausted flocks and drop on the far shore in numbers. Egypt has netted them that way for millennia.

In the kitchen

Spatchcock it and cook it fast and hot. A whole quail roasted slowly is dry breast and raw thigh at the same moment.
