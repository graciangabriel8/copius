Boquerones

Boquerones (anchois marinés au vinaigre)

FamilyFish & seafood
Season (France)April – August
Typical price (France)25–40 €/kg

What it is

Spain keeps a hard line between the salt-cured anchoa and the vinegar-cured boquerón: one fish taken in opposite directions, darkened by months of fermentation or whitened by acid in an afternoon. Since 2006 Spanish law has required the fish to be frozen before it is marinated, because vinegar changes the texture of a raw anchovy without killing anything living in it.

In the kitchen

Marinate in white wine vinegar cut with a third of its volume in water, and stop the moment the flesh turns opaque — two to four hours for small fillets. Drain, then cover with oil and sliced garlic; left overnight in the vinegar they go chalky.
