Génépi

Génépi

Latin nameArtemisia umbelliformis
FamilyHerbs
OriginAlpine screes above 2000 m
SeasonJuly – August
FlavourBitter · Herbal · Resinous
Typical price25–50 €/100 g

What it is

A Savoie prefectoral decree of June 2021 capped picking at 120 sprigs per person per day, after whole colonies were stripped from the screes above 2,000 metres where the plant grows. Distillers already worked to a rule older than the law: forty sprigs, forty sugar lumps, forty days.

In the kitchen

The classic maceration is forty sprigs and forty sugar lumps in a litre of neutral spirit, forty days and no longer — past that the bitterness turns woody. A single dried sprig is already enough to mark a cream or a sorbet base.
