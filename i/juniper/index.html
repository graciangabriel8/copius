Juniper berry

Baie de genièvre

Latin nameJuniperus communis
FamilySpices
OriginNorthern hemisphere scrubland
Season (France)All year
FlavourResinous · Woody · Bitter · Fresh
Typical price (France)20–40 €/kg

What it is

The only spice we harvest from a conifer — each “berry” is a tiny cone that takes two years to ripen. It gave gin its soul and its name, via the Dutch genever, and it remains the mountain accent in choucroute, game and everything that tastes of a walk through cold pines.

In the kitchen

Crush the berries flat before they go in — whole, they stay mute. Three or four suffice for a whole braise; ten make it a pine forest.
