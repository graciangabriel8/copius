Limousin beef

Bœuf Limousin

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

The Limousin pulled ploughs across the granite soils of its plateau long before anyone selected it for meat — the herd book opened in 1886 and turned a draught ox into a butcher's breed. Its thin hide and light bone are why the carcass yields more sellable meat than almost any other animal in France.

In the kitchen

Little cover fat means little protection — grill or roast it fast and hot, pull it rare, and rest it as long as it cooked. Braised, it gives dry strands rather than gelatine.
