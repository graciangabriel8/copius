Black truffle

Truffe noire

Latin nameTuber melanosporum
FamilyMushrooms
OriginPérigord & Provence, France
Season (France)December – February
FlavourEarthy · Umami · Rich
Typical price (France)700–1500 €/kg

What it is

Brillat-Savarin called it “the diamond of the kitchen”. For centuries pigs hunted it — too well, since they ate their finds — until trainers switched to dogs, who trade the black nugget for a biscuit without regret.

In the kitchen

Never cook it hard: shave it over warm eggs, pasta or potatoes at the last second. Store it with your eggs — the perfume passes through the shell.
