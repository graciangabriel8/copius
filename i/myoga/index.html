Myoga ✦

Myoga

Latin nameZingiber mioga
FamilyVegetables
OriginJapanese woodland shade
SeasonJuly – October
FlavourFresh · Pungent · Floral
Typical price60–120 €/kg

What it is

The story goes that the plant first grew on the grave of Shurihandoku, a disciple of the Buddha who could not hold on to his own name, and Japanese households still half-joke that eating too much of it makes you forgetful. What you eat is the flower bud, pushed up at ground level separately from the leaves, not the rhizome its ginger cousins give.

In the kitchen

Shred it lengthways and soak the shreds five minutes in cold water to take the raw edge off, then drain hard. It goes on at the very end, over cold noodles or chilled tofu, never into the pan.
