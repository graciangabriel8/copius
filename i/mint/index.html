What it is

Minthe was a nymph whom jealous Persephone crushed underfoot; Hades could only give her a scent that rises whenever she is stepped on. From Marrakech tea to Vietnamese tables, no herb refreshes more of the planet.

In the kitchen

It spreads like gossip in a garden — grow it in a pot. Bruise the leaves, don’t chop them, for drinks.
