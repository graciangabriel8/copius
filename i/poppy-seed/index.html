Poppy seed

Graine de pavot

Latin namePapaver somniferum
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginMediterranean
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Toasty · Bitter · Earthy
Typical price (France)10–20 €/kg

What it is

Central Europe grinds them into a sweet paste for strudel and beigli, which is a different food from the whole seeds scattered on a bagel. It takes roughly three thousand seeds to make a gram, and the plant is the opium poppy.

In the kitchen

Grind them or soak them in hot milk before baking. Whole and dry they contribute crunch and almost no flavour.
