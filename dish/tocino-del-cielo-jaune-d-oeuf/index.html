Signature dish

Tocino del cielo (candied egg yolk on a caramelised macaron)

Iñaki Aizpitarte · 1971 · Le Chateaubriand, Paris 11e

An Andalusian dessert of egg yolk set in sugar syrup, served at Le Chateaubriand as a single caramelised bite sitting on a macaron base.

Why it works

The concentrated sugar syrup binds the free water and raises the temperature at which the yolk's proteins coagulate, so the yolk sets into a dense, smooth custard rather than curdling into grains.

What is in it

Egg White sugar
