Porcini oil

Huile de cèpe

FamilyFats & oils
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)50–90 €/L

What it is

Unlike truffle oil, which is almost always built on synthetic 2,4-dithiapentane, porcini oil is usually what the label says: dried Boletus edulis left to steep until the mushroom aroma migrates into the fat. The caps are dried first because drying is what makes the aroma — a fresh porcini gives the oil almost nothing.

In the kitchen

Stir it into risotto or polenta off the heat; cooking drives the aroma off and leaves you with plain oil. If mushroom pieces are still in the bottle, keep it refrigerated and finish it within a month — low-acid solids under oil are a botulism risk at room temperature.
