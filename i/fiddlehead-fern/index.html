Fiddlehead fern ✦

Tête de violon

Latin nameMatteuccia struthiopteris
FamilyVegetables
OriginNorth-eastern woodland riverbanks
SeasonApril – May
Flavourgrassy · earthy · bitter
Typical price35–70 €/kg

What it is

A run of illness across New York State and western Canada in 1994 was traced to lightly sautéed fiddleheads; Health Canada has advised a full fifteen-minute boil ever since, and the toxin responsible has still not been identified. Only the ostrich fern is eaten — its crozier is smooth and grooved on the inner face, and the fuzzy ones growing beside it are not.

In the kitchen

Fifteen minutes in plenty of boiling water, water thrown away, then refresh and sauté — a second short blanch in clean water improves the flavour markedly. Never sauté them raw, however good the colour looks in the basket.
