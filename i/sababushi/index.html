Sababushi (maquereau séché)

Latin nameScomber japonicus (dried, smoked)
FamilyCondiments
OriginJapan
SeasonAll year
Flavourumami · smoky · rich · meaty
Typical price60–120 €/kg

What it is

Made exactly like katsuobushi — simmered, boned, smoked over weeks — but from mackerel, which carries far more fat than skipjack and leaves a darker, sweeter broth. Soba houses keep it for precisely that: a dashi with enough body to stand up to the black soy of a tsuyu.

In the kitchen

Its fat turns bitter if boiled: steep the shavings at about 80 °C for two minutes, then strain — never squeeze the flakes.

Goes with

Kombu Soy sauce Hon-mirin Soba noodles Shiitake Negi Katsuobushi
