Desiccated coconut

Noix de coco râpée

Latin nameCocos nucifera
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginIndo-Pacific
Season (France)All year
FlavourSweet · Nutty · Creamy · Toasty
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

Drying was what let coconut leave the tropics — fresh flesh spoils within days, dried it keeps for a year. The Victorian appetite for coconut cake and macaroons is entirely a consequence of that one process.

In the kitchen

Toast it in a dry pan before it goes into anything. Raw desiccated coconut tastes of cardboard.
