Lardo di Colonnata

lardo di Colonnata

Latin nameSus scrofa (backfat, cured)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginColonnata, Carrara
SeasonAll year
FlavourRich · Buttery · Herbal · Salty
Typical price35–60 €/kg

What it is

It cures in basins hollowed from Carrara marble — the porous Canaloni vein, which holds cold and damp on its own — rubbed inside with garlic, then packed in layers with sea salt, rosemary and pepper for six to ten months. Quarrymen carried it down the mountain as their working lunch long before the IGP of 2004 turned it into a restaurant luxury.

In the kitchen

Slice it almost transparent and lay it on something hot — toast, a boiled potato, a piece of fish — so it melts rather than sits. It is fat, not meat, and it never goes near a pan.
