Grouper

Mérou

Latin nameEpinephelus marginatus
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginMediterranean rock and drop-offs
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Mild · Marine
Typical price (France)25–45 €/kg

What it is

Every dusky grouper begins life as a female and only turns male at around fourteen years and eighty centimetres, so fishing out the largest individuals removes the males from an entire reef. France has held a moratorium on spearfishing it in the Mediterranean since 1993.

In the kitchen

Ask for the head and frame with it: grouper bone is so rich in gelatine that a two-hour stock sets to a firm jelly when cold. That jelly is the base of a braise needing neither cream nor flour.

Its 9 pairings are in the full version (€5.90 a month or €49 a year).
