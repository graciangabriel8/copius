Apricot

Abricot

Latin namePrunus armeniaca
FamilyFruit
OriginCentral Asia & China
SeasonJune – August
Flavoursweet · sour · floral · fruity
Typical price4–8 €/kg

What it is

Its Latin name blames Armenia, but the tree came from China, where it was cultivated four thousand years ago. The name travelled through Latin praecoquum, the early-ripening one, into Arabic and back into Europe with the article attached.

In the kitchen

It does not ripen after picking — only softens. A hard pale apricot will never become sweet, so buy by smell.

Goes with

Almond Honey Vanilla Peach Rosemary Pistachio Cream Thyme
