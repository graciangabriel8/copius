Sisteron lamb

Agneau de Sisteron

FamilyMeat & poultry
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)25–45 €/kg

What it is

The IGP granted in 2007 covers lambs of rustic Provençal ewes — Mérinos d’Arles, Préalpes du Sud, Mourérous — raised under the mother and finished on dry pastoral ground. The name comes from the market town where the region’s lambs were graded and sold, not from any flock the town keeps itself.

In the kitchen

Do not marinate it: the garrigue flavour sits in the fat and acid strips it out. Roast the leg fifteen minutes at 210 °C, then drop to 160 °C until the bone reads 55 °C, and rest it twenty minutes.
