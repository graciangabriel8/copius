Squid

Calamar

Latin nameLoligo vulgaris
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginEastern Atlantic & Mediterranean
Season (France)May – October
FlavourSweet · Marine · Delicate · Umami
Typical price (France)12–20 €/kg

What it is

Its muscle has almost no fat and a great deal of collagen, which is why there are exactly two ways to cook it — under two minutes or over thirty. Anything between those and it is rubber, and no amount of skill rescues it.

In the kitchen

Score the inside of the tube in a diamond pattern before it hits the pan. It curls decoratively and cooks evenly.
