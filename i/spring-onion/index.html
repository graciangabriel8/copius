Spring onion

Cébette

Latin nameAllium fistulosum
FamilyVegetables
OriginChina
Season (France)March – August
FlavourFresh · Pungent · Green · Mild
Typical price (France)4–8 €/kg

What it is

A different species from the bulb onion — it never swells and never means to. Cultivated in China for two thousand years, it is one of the few alliums used raw at the finish of a dish rather than cooked down at the start.

In the kitchen

White and green are two ingredients. Cook the white with the aromatics, scatter the green raw over the finished plate.
