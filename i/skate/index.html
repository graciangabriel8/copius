Skate wing

Aile de raie

Latin nameRaja clavata
FamilyFish
OriginNortheast Atlantic
SeasonOctober – March
Flavoursweet · marine · delicate · rich
Typical price14–24 €/kg

What it is

It has cartilage rather than bone, so the flesh comes away in ribbed strands rather than flakes. It also smells faintly of ammonia if it is even slightly old — one of the few fish where the nose gives an unambiguous verdict.

In the kitchen

Raie au beurre noisette is the dish: browned butter, capers, a splash of vinegar. It has been the standard for two centuries because nothing beats it.

Goes with

Capers Butter White wine vinegar Parsley Potato Lemon Black pepper Shallot
