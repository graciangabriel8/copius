What it is

Kale is the cabbage that never learned to make a head, and it is closer to the wild ancestor than any of its cousins. During the Second World War the British Dig for Victory campaign pushed it into every garden because it survives frost that flattens everything else.

In the kitchen

Frost genuinely sweetens it — the plant makes sugars as antifreeze. Massage raw leaves with oil and salt to break them down for a salad.
