Bitto

FamilyDairy & eggs
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)35–60 €/kg

What it is

It is made only between June and September, in a calècc — a low drystone enclosure roofed with a canvas that moves up the pasture with the herd — where the cow's milk is worked minutes after milking and given a share of milk from Orobica goats. Wheels carry the year of their summer, and a good one is still edible ten years on.

In the kitchen

Past five years the paste turns brittle and crystalline: stop trying to slice it, break it away with the point of a knife and let the shards come back to room temperature before serving.
