Fugu shirako ✦ ◆

Laitance de fugu

Latin nameTakifugu rubripes (milt)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginShimonoseki, in winter
Season (France)December – February
FlavourCreamy · Rich · Marine · Delicate
Typical price (France)150–400 €/kg

What it is

In a fish whose liver and ovaries can kill, the milt is harmless — it is the one internal organ a licensed fugu cook may serve, and it peaks in the weeks before the winter spawn. It has nothing to do with the cod milt sold under the same word: firmer, sweeter, and priced accordingly.

In the kitchen

Salt it and blister it close to the coals, a minute a side — the skin should mottle while the inside stays liquid. Ponzu and grated daikon; anything creamy is redundant.
