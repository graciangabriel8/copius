Medlar

Nèfle

Latin nameMespilus germanica
FamilyFruits
OriginCaucasus & Persia
Season (France)November – December
FlavourSweet · Sour · Earthy · Musky
Typical price (France)8–15 €/kg

What it is

It is only edible once it has half-rotted on the branch or in straw, a process politely called bletting — and Shakespeare and Chaucer both made the obvious jokes. It was a staple winter fruit for centuries before sugar arrived.

In the kitchen

Wait until it is brown and yielding — that is ripe, not spoiled. Then scoop the pulp out with a spoon.
