What it is

It is a hunter rather than a filter feeder: it wedges the lip of its own shell between a bivalve’s valves and pushes its proboscis in, which is why whelk pots are baited with crab instead of being left to fill. Nearly all the French catch comes from a single bay off the Cotentin, and Bulot de la baie de Granville now carries a European protected geographical indication.

In the kitchen

Start them in cold court-bouillon, count twenty minutes from the boil, then take the pan off the heat and let them cool in their own liquid — that last step, not the timing, is what keeps them tender. Pull the black spiral off the tail before serving, and buy medium sizes; the biggest are always rubber.
