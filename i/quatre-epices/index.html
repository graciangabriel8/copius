What it is

White pepper, nutmeg, clove and ginger — the charcutier's blend, and it is what makes a French terrine or a boudin taste French rather than merely of pork. It is one of the few classical blends that is savoury rather than sweet.

In the kitchen

Pepper should dominate, not clove. If yours smells mainly of clove it is out of balance and will overwhelm a pâté.
