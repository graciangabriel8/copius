Quinoa

Latin nameChenopodium quinoa
FamilyGrains & flours
OriginAndean altiplano
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Earthy · Mild
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

The Incas called it chisaya mama — “mother of all grains” — and the emperor sowed the first seeds each year with a golden spade. Technically a seed cousin of spinach, it grows at altitudes where wheat gives up.

In the kitchen

Rinse it well — its natural coating is bitter — and toast it dry before adding water for a nuttier result.
