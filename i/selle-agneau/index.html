Saddle of lamb

Selle d’agneau

Latin nameAgneau — selle
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginFrance
Season (France)March – June
FlavourDelicate · Rich · Meaty · Sweet
Typical price (France)22–35 €/kg

What it is

Both loins still joined across the backbone, and the grandest thing a butcher can hand you — it is a restaurant cut, carved at the table. Escoffier's selle d'agneau was a set piece of the classical service.

In the kitchen

Ask the butcher to remove the chine bone but leave the saddle whole. Carving it otherwise is genuinely difficult.
