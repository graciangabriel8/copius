What it is

Egyptian labourers ate it for strength, Greek athletes for stamina, and folklore armed it against vampires. Few ingredients have been simultaneously medicine, talisman and seasoning for five thousand years.

In the kitchen

Crushed, it roars; sliced, it speaks; whole and roasted, it whispers sweetly. Choose its voice before you cook.
