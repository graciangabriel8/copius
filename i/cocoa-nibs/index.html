Cocoa nibs

Grué de cacao

Latin nameTheobroma cacao
FamilySweet
OriginCentral & South America
SeasonAll year
Flavourbitter · toasty · earthy · nutty
Typical price25–45 €/kg

What it is

Roasted cocoa beans cracked into fragments, before any sugar or grinding — this is what chocolate is made from, and it is unrecognisably bitter. The Aztecs drank it with chilli and used the beans as currency.

In the kitchen

Use them as you would a nut, for crunch and bitterness against something sweet. They never soften.

Goes with
