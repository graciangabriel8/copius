Cecina de León

cecina de León

Latin nameBos taurus (hind leg, smoked and cured)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginLeón, Castile
SeasonAll year
FlavourSmoky · Salty · Meaty · Woody
Typical price50–80 €/kg

What it is

Only four muscles of the hind leg qualify — tapa, contra, babilla, cadera — and between salting and drying the pieces spend twelve to sixteen days over slow-burning oak or holm oak. The minimum cure is seven months, twelve for the Reserva, and it is that smoke that separates it from every Italian air-dried beef.

In the kitchen

Drier and leaner than bresaola, so cut it a shade thicker and pour good olive oil over the slices five minutes before serving — the fat it lacks has to come from somewhere. Leave the lemon alone.
