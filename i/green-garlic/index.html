Green garlic

Ail nouveau

Latin nameAllium sativum
FamilyVegetables
OriginSpring garlic beds
Season (France)April – June
FlavourSweet · Mild · Pungent
Typical price (France)6–12 €/kg

What it is

Curing is what makes garlic sharp: weeks of drying concentrate the bulb's sulphur compounds as it loses water and the cloves seal themselves into papery jackets. Pulled in spring before any of that, it is one soft undivided bulb on a juicy stalk, and the whole plant is edible.

In the kitchen

Use the white and the pale green of the stalk, sliced into rounds like a leek — there is nothing to separate, the cloves have not formed yet. Give it a fortnight in the fridge, not a season on the shelf.
