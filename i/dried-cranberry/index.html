Dried cranberry

Canneberge séchée

Latin nameVaccinium macrocarpon
FamilyFruits
OriginNorth America
SeasonAll year
FlavourSweet · Sour · Fruity · Tangy
Typical price9–15 €/kg

What it is

Almost always sweetened before drying, because an unsweetened dried cranberry is close to inedible — the fruit is far too acidic. Read the label: many are sweetened with apple juice concentrate rather than sugar, and they taste different.

In the kitchen

Their acidity cuts fat, which is why they work in a stuffing or a grain salad far better than raisins do.

Goes with
