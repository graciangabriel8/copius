Capon

Chapon

Latin nameGallus gallus domesticus
FamilyMeat
OriginRome
SeasonNovember – December
Flavourrich · meaty · delicate · buttery
Typical price12–22 €/kg

What it is

A castrated cockerel, fattened slowly, and the practice began in Rome as a workaround: a law of 162 BC limited how many hens a household could eat, and said nothing about roosters. The fat marbles into the meat instead of sitting under the skin.

In the kitchen

It is the one bird that genuinely rewards a long, slow roast — the internal fat bastes it from within.
