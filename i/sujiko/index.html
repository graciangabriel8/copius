Sujiko ✦

Sujiko (œufs de saumon en poche)

Latin nameOncorhynchus spp. (roe sac)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginHokkaido & Tohoku
Season (France)September – November
FlavourSalty · Rich · Umami · Briny
Typical price (France)60–110 €/kg

What it is

Suji is the streak of membrane still holding the eggs: roe taken before it ripens and salted inside its sac, so it stays smaller, darker and denser than loose grain. The loose version took a foreign name — ikura is Russian, икра, brought back by Japanese fishermen in the early twentieth century — while the older sac form kept its Japanese one.

In the kitchen

Slice it across the sac a centimetre thick and lay it on hot rice — the membrane holds the block together and anything thinner falls apart. It is saltier than ikura by design, so serve half as much.
