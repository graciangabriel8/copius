Nigella seed

Nigelle

FamilySpices
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Called black cumin, black caraway and black onion seed, and it is none of the three — it belongs to the buttercup family. It is the black seed of Islamic and Ayurvedic medicine, and it is what speckles the top of a naan.

In the kitchen

Scatter it on bread before baking, or bloom it in hot oil. Ground, it turns dusty and loses the oniony top note.
