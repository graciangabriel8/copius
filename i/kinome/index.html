Kinome (sanshō leaf) ✦

Kinome (jeune feuille de sanshō)

Latin nameZanthoxylum piperitum
FamilyHerbs
OriginJapanese gardens and hillsides
SeasonApril – May
FlavourCitrusy · Fresh · Tingling
Typical price1–3 €/g

What it is

Kinome is the spring leaf of the same tree that gives sanshō pepper, sold by the leaf rather than by weight, and it appears for a few weeks in April and May alongside the first bamboo shoots. Cooks slap it once between their palms before it goes on the plate, bruising the oil cells so the scent lifts.

In the kitchen

Never chop it — bruise it whole in the palm and lay it on, or the citrus note oxidises within a minute. It tingles faintly rather than numbing like the dried peppercorn, so it suits delicate things: clams, tofu, bamboo.
