Grondin

Latin nameChelidonichthys lucerna
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginEastern Atlantic & Mediterranean
Season (France)October – March
FlavourSweet · Marine · Mild · Meaty
Typical price (France)6–12 €/kg

What it is

It grunts audibly when landed, using its swim bladder — which is where both names come from, grondin from gronder, to growl. Long dismissed as bycatch, it is one of the essential fish of a real bouillabaisse.

In the kitchen

Mind the spines on the head and gill covers — they are genuinely sharp. Ask the fishmonger to trim them.
