Hazelnut

Noisette

Latin nameCorylus avellana
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginEurope & Asia Minor
Season (France)September – October
FlavourNutty · Sweet · Toasty · Buttery
Typical price (France)12–22 €/kg

What it is

In Celtic legend, nine hazel trees dropped their nuts of wisdom into a sacred pool, and the salmon who ate them knew everything. Later, Napoleon’s blockade cut Turin off from cocoa — chocolatiers stretched what little they had with local hazelnuts, and gianduja was born of scarcity.

In the kitchen

Roast, then rub in a tea towel: the papery skins fall away and the bitterness goes with them.
