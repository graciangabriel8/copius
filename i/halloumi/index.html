Halloumi

Halloumi

Latin nameΧαλλούμι
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginCyprus
SeasonAll year
FlavourSalty · Milky · Mild · Umami
Typical price14–22 €/kg

What it is

It survives a hot pan because the curd is cooked in whey before it is salted, which sets the proteins so firmly they will not flow again. Cypriot makers fold a mint leaf into the fold before brining — originally to keep it, now simply because that is how it is done.

In the kitchen

Dry-fry it, no oil, in a very hot pan and do not move it until it releases. Eat it hot; cold halloumi turns to rubber.
