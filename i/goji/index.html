Goji berry

Baie de goji

FamilyFruits
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Grown in China for centuries as a medicinal ingredient in soups and teas rather than a snack — it is in the same family as the tomato and the aubergine. Its Western career as a superfood dates only to the early 2000s.

In the kitchen

Simmer them in a broth or a congee the way Chinese cooks do. Eaten dry by the handful they are leathery and sour.
