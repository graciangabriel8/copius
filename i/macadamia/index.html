Macadamia

Noix de macadamia

Latin nameMacadamia integrifolia
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginQueensland, Australia
Season (France)All year
FlavourButtery · Sweet · Creamy · Nutty
Typical price (France)35–60 €/kg

What it is

The hardest shell of any commercial nut — it takes about three hundred atmospheres to crack, which is why it is the most expensive nut in the world. It is also toxic to dogs, for reasons nobody has fully explained.

In the kitchen

It is over seventy per cent fat, so it burns fast. Toast it at a low temperature and watch it constantly.
