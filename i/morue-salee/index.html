Salt cod

Morue salée

Latin nameGadus morhua (salted)
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginNorth Atlantic banks
Season (France)All year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Marine · Rich
Typical price (France)15–30 €/kg

What it is

Basque crews were working the Newfoundland banks by the early sixteenth century and salted their catch aboard, because the damp air there made the Norwegian trick of simply drying it in the wind impossible. Salt cod then fed Catholic Europe through every lean Friday of the year — a fish caught in one hemisphere and eaten in another, centuries before refrigeration.

In the kitchen

Desalt it skin up in cold water in the fridge — 24 hours for the soft pale cure, 48 for the hard yellow one — changing the water four or five times. Taste a shred before you commit: over-salted brandade cannot be rescued.
