Apricot kernel

Amandon d'abricot

Latin namePrunus armeniaca
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginMalatya, Anatolia
Season (France)All year
FlavourBitter · Nutty · Delicate
Typical price (France)18–35 €/kg

What it is

The almond flavour in amaretti and in most noyau liqueurs is not almond at all: it comes from this seed, cracked out of the apricot stone. Sweet or bitter is a cultivar trait rather than a stage of ripeness, so the same-looking kernel is eaten by the handful in Malatya and weighed out by the gram in a distillery.

In the kitchen

Crack the stones with the flat of a knife rather than a nutcracker — a nutcracker shatters shell into the kernel. Blanch and rub the brown skin off before grinding; that is where most of the bitterness sits.
