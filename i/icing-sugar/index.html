Icing sugar

Sucre glace

Latin nameSaccharum pulverulentum
FamilySweet
OriginEurope
SeasonAll year
Flavoursweet · delicate · mild · milky
Typical price2–5 €/kg

What it is

Ground to a powder and cut with two or three per cent starch to stop it caking — which is why it clouds a liquid and why it can never be used to make caramel. The starch is the reason, not the fineness.

In the kitchen

Always sift it, without exception. It clumps in the box and a lump will not dissolve in a buttercream.

Goes with

Butter Vanilla Lemon Cream Strawberry Dark chocolate Raspberry Almond
