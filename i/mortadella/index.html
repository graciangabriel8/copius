Mortadella

mortadelle

Latin nameSus scrofa (emulsified, cooked)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginBologna, Emilia
Season (France)All year
FlavourMild · Rich · Creamy · Nutty
Typical price (France)15–28 €/kg

What it is

The likelier of the two etymologies goes back to farcimen myrtatum, a Roman sausage seasoned with myrtle berries and mentioned by Varro and Pliny; the other points to the mortarium, the mortar the meat was pounded in. Either way the cubes of neck fat are still cut by hand, and the whole piece steam-cooks for hours in dry-air ovens before it is ever sliced.

In the kitchen

Have it cut at the counter and eat it the same day: sliced thin, the fat cubes oxidise within hours and the perfume goes flat. Warm the slices briefly and it becomes another thing entirely.
