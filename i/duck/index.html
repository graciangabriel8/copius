What it is

China domesticated the duck millennia ago and perfected its roasting into imperial ceremony. France answered with its own schools — rare Rouen duck, pressed at the table in silver contraptions, and the Southwest’s confit, where the bird is preserved in its own gold.

In the kitchen

Score the fat, start it in a cold pan, and pour off the rendered fat as you go — then roast your next potatoes in it and understand the Southwest of France.
