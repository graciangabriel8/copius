Cotentin cider

Cidre Cotentin

Latin nameMalus domestica (cider apples)
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginNorthern Manche, Cotentin peninsula
Season (France)All year
FlavourBitter · Buttery · Herbal
Typical price (France)9–17 €/L

What it is

The appellation is built on bitterness: bitter and bittersweet apples such as Taureau, Petit amer and Marin Onfroy must cover at least 70% of the orchard, and main varieties make up at least 60% of every cuvée. The first fermentation runs on the fruit's own yeasts, and the sparkle comes only from at least eight weeks of fermentation in the bottle; carbonation and pasteurisation are banned. At 18 g of sugar per litre or less it must be labelled extra-brut.

In the kitchen

The extra-brut, with 18 g of sugar per litre at most, is the one for the pan: it reduces without turning syrupy, so deglaze pork chops or scallops with it and finish with cream. Stand the bottle upright in the fridge for a day so the yeast deposit settles, and pour gently.
