Yogurt

Yaourt

Latin nameLac fermentatum
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginCentral Asia & Balkans
Season (France)All year
FlavourTangy · Creamy · Milky · Fresh
Typical price (France)2–4 €/kg

What it is

Nomads discovered it by accident: milk carried in animal-skin bags fermented on horseback into something tart that kept. Centuries later, a scientist studying Bulgarian villages full of centenarians credited their yogurt — and Europe has eaten it for breakfast ever since.

In the kitchen

To cook with it without curdling, temper it: whisk a little hot liquid into the yogurt before the yogurt goes into the pot.
