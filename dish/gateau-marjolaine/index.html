The dish that changed something · c. 1945

Gâteau marjolaine

Fernand Point · 1897–1955 · La Pyramide, Vienne

Nut meringue layered with three creams, cut in a long rectangle. Point assembled rather than baked, and the entremets that fill French pastry windows today are all built the same way.

Why it works

The dacquoise is dry and sweet, the creams are fat and barely sweet, and the layers are thin enough that no mouthful is only one or the other.

What is in it

Hazelnut Almond Cream Dark chocolate
