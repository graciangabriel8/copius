Junmai sake

Saké junmai

Latin nameOryza sativa
FamilyWines & spirits
OriginJapan (Nada, Fushimi)
Season (France)All year
FlavourUmami · Mild · Rich
Typical price (France)20–40 €/L

What it is

Sake sold in Japan as cooking sake is salted to a couple of percent so that it counts as a seasoning rather than a drink, and escapes liquor tax; junmai means pure rice, and carries nothing but rice, water, koji and yeast. The category once demanded a polishing ratio as well, a requirement dropped in the 2000s - today the single rule is that no distilled alcohol goes in.

In the kitchen

Let it boil for a minute before it meets anything delicate: the alcohol leaves and the amino acids stay, and it is those that soften fish odour and round out a broth. Because it carries no salt, count it as liquid rather than seasoning, and season only once it has reduced.
