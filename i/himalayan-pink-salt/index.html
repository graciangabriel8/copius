Himalayan pink salt

Sel rose de l’Himalaya

Latin nameNatrii chloridum
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginKhewra mine, Punjab
SeasonAll year
FlavourSalty · Mild · Earthy
Typical price4–10 €/kg

What it is

The salt comes from the Khewra mine in Punjab, worked room-and-pillar since a British engineer laid out its main tunnel in 1872, and the pink is iron oxide — haematite and goethite — caught in the halite. The story that Alexander the Great's horses found the deposit by licking the rocks is repeated everywhere, but it reads as a colonial-era embellishment rather than a record.

In the kitchen

The sawn block, not the grinder, is what this salt is for: bring it up to heat slowly over forty-five minutes to two hours or it cracks, then sear prawns or thin beef straight on the surface. Never wash it — scrape it and wipe it barely damp, because water dissolves it away.
