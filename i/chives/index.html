Ciboulette

Latin nameAllium schoenoprasum
FamilyHerbs
OriginEurope & Asia
SeasonApril – September
Flavourmild · fresh · pungent
Typical price2–3 €/botte

What it is

The gentlest member of the onion clan, gathered wild in Europe since the Middle Ages, when bunches were hung against evil spells. It offers the allium idea — without the allium consequences.

In the kitchen

Scissors beat the knife: snip it directly over eggs, potatoes or cream, and its purple pompom flowers are edible too.

Goes with

Egg Potato Cream Scallop Asparagus Cod Yogurt
