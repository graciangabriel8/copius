Honey

Miel

Latin nameApis mellifera (nectar)
FamilySweet pantry
OriginEverywhere bees fly
SeasonAll year
FlavourSweet · Floral · Honeyed · Warm
Typical price7–15 €/kg

What it is

Jars of honey found in pharaohs’ tombs were still edible after three thousand years — it is the only food that never spoils. A single pound represents the nectar of about two million flowers; every spoonful is a map of a landscape in bloom.

In the kitchen

Crystallised honey isn’t spoiled — warm the jar gently in water and it clears. Match its strength to the dish: acacia whispers, chestnut argues.
