Chrysanthemum greens ✦

Shungiku

Latin nameGlebionis coronaria
FamilyVegetables
OriginMediterranean; grown in East Asia
SeasonNovember – March
Flavourherbal · bitter · grassy
Typical price6–12 €/kg

What it is

The plant is a Mediterranean wildflower that travelled east along the trade routes and became a vegetable there while Europe kept it in the flower border. Chinese cooks have eaten tong hao for well over a thousand years; French seed catalogues still sell the same species as a summer annual.

In the kitchen

Ten seconds in the broth and out — held longer it turns bitter and metallic, and no seasoning brings it back. Add it per portion at the table in a hot pot, never to the pot at the start.

Goes with

Sesame Soy sauce Kombu Silken tofu (kinugoshi) Miso Toasted sesame oil
