Hedgehog mushroom

Pied-de-mouton

Latin nameHydnum repandum
FamilyMushrooms
OriginEuropean woodland
SeasonSeptember – December
FlavourNutty · Sweet · Earthy · Mild
Typical price12–25 €/kg

What it is

Underneath it has spines rather than gills — tiny soft teeth that shed spores, which is why French calls it a sheep's foot and English a hedgehog. It has no dangerous lookalike at all, which makes it the beginner's mushroom.

In the kitchen

Scrape the spines off if it is a large one — they turn bitter with age. Young specimens need nothing.

Goes with

Butter Parsley Garlic Cream Shallot Thyme Egg Chicken
