Lamb’s lettuce

Mâche

Latin nameValerianella locusta
FamilyVegetables
OriginEurope
SeasonOctober – March
Flavournutty · delicate · mild · grassy
Typical price12–20 €/kg

What it is

For centuries it was a weed pulled from cornfields after the harvest, which is why German still calls it Ackersalat — field salad. Nantes turned it into a crop in the nineteenth century, and the Loire valley still grows most of what France eats.

In the kitchen

Wash it in a large bowl of standing water, never under a tap. The rosettes trap grit at the base and a jet just drives it deeper.
