Kokotxas (hake throat)

Kokotxas (gorges de merlu)

FamilyFish & seafood
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–80 €/kg

What it is

Kokotxa is the Basque name for the V of muscle under a hake's chin, and it outsells the fillet several times over — €60 to €90 a kilo against a fraction of that for the loin. It is bought for what it releases rather than what it tastes of: warmed gently in olive oil, its collagen emulsifies the oil into pil-pil, a sauce with no butter, no cream and no flour in it.

In the kitchen

Keep the oil below a simmer — around 65 °C — and swirl the pan in circles off the heat; oil that boils splits the emulsion and no whisking brings it back. Skin side down first, and let them give up their gelatine before you start swirling.
