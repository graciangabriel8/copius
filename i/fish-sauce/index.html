Fish sauce

Nuoc-mâm

Latin nameEngraulidae (fermented)
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginVietnam & Thailand
SeasonAll year
FlavourSalty · Umami · Pungent · Rich
Typical price5–12 €/L

What it is

Anchovies and salt, stacked in wooden barrels under the tropical sun for a year — the direct heir of Rome’s garum, still pressed the old way in Cetara, Italy, as colatura. The first amber run of the barrel, nhĩ, is graded and prized like an extra-virgin oil.

In the kitchen

Smell the bottle, then trust the pot: cooked, it sheds its roar and leaves pure depth. A teaspoon secretly improves a bolognese or a vinaigrette.
