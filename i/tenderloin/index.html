Beef tenderloin

Filet de bœuf

Latin nameBos taurus (psoas major)
FamilyCuts
OriginAlong the spine — the muscle that never works
SeasonAll year
Flavourdelicate · meaty · mild
Typical price38–70 €/kg

What it is

The laziest muscle on the animal, and therefore the tenderest — and the mildest, which is why it attracts escorts: its centre cut was named Chateaubriand by the writer’s own chef, and Rossini lent his name to the version crowned with foie gras and truffle. Tenderness invites orchestras.

In the kitchen

It has no fat to protect it: sear hard, finish gently, rest long. Because its voice is quiet, give it a talkative sauce — pepper, truffle, or brown butter.
