Rat-tail radish pod ✦

Radis serpent

Latin nameRaphanus caudatus
FamilyVegetables
OriginJava and South Asia
Season (France)July – September
FlavourPeppery · Pungent · Fresh
Typical price (France)15–25 €/kg

What it is

It is a radish grown for its seed pods, and it reached European seed catalogues in the 1860s billed as the Java radish, a curiosity that never caught on though the pods had long been an ordinary vegetable in India. The root beneath is woody and inedible: the plant has put everything into the pod.

In the kitchen

Take them young, at pencil thickness or less; beyond that a papery string runs down each seam and the seeds go hard. Thirty seconds in hot oil with garlic, or raw in a quick vinegar pickle — anything longer and the pepper heat cooks straight out.
