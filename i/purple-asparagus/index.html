Purple asparagus

Asperge violette

Latin nameAsparagus officinalis
FamilyVegetables
OriginAlbenga, Liguria
Season (France)April – June
FlavourSweet · Green · Delicate
Typical price (France)12–20 €/kg

What it is

The Violetto d'Albenga owes its colour to a doubled chromosome count, forty instead of twenty, rather than to any growing method, and it carries roughly a fifth more sugar than a green spear. Cut as the tip breaks daylight, it never builds the fibre a green spear develops standing up.

In the kitchen

Boiling turns it green in under a minute, since the anthocyanins are water-soluble and heat-fragile and simply wash out. Eat it raw and shaved, or roast it dry at high heat to keep some colour, and never peel it.
