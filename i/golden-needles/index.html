Dried lily buds (golden needles) ✦

Fleurs de lys séchées (aiguilles d’or)

Latin nameHemerocallis citrina
FamilyHerbs
OriginDatong, Shanxi, China
SeasonAll year
Flavourmusky · earthy · honeyed
Typical price3–6 €/100 g

What it is

Daylily buds must be picked in the few hours before they open, which is why the Shanxi harvest starts before dawn; they are then steamed and sun-dried into amber strands. The stomach upset caused by raw buds was blamed on colchicine for decades, until the plant’s genome was sequenced and turned out to lack the pathway for it entirely.

In the kitchen

Soak twenty minutes in warm water, then snap off the pale hard base of each bud — that end never softens and is what gets mistaken for the ingredient being tough. Knot them in pairs before they go into hot and sour soup so they stay findable in the bowl.

Goes with
