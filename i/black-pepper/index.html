Black pepper

Poivre noir

Latin namePiper nigrum
FamilySpices
OriginMalabar coast, India
Season (France)All year
FlavourHot · Woody · Warm
Typical price (France)18–40 €/kg

What it is

Medieval Europe paid rents, dowries and ransoms in peppercorns — “peppercorn rent” survives in English law. Vasco da Gama sailed around Africa chasing this berry; whole cities, from Venice to Lisbon, grew rich on its trail.

In the kitchen

Grind it at the last moment — pre-ground pepper is mostly dust and regret. Try it on strawberries: it works.
