Grapefruit

Pamplemousse

Latin nameCitrus × paradisi
FamilyFruit
OriginBarbados
SeasonNovember – April
Flavourbitter · sour · citrus · fruity
Typical price2,50–4,50 €/kg

What it is

An accidental hybrid of the pomelo and the sweet orange, found growing in Barbados in the eighteenth century. Its name is agricultural rather than poetic: the fruit hangs in tight clusters on the branch, like grapes.

In the kitchen

A pinch of salt on the cut half suppresses the bitterness far better than sugar does — it blocks the receptors directly.
