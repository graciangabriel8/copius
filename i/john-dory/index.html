John Dory

Saint-Pierre

Latin nameZeus faber
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginEastern Atlantic & Mediterranean
SeasonOctober – April
FlavourDelicate · Sweet · Marine · Mild
Typical price30–50 €/kg

What it is

The dark spot on its flank is said to be Saint Peter's thumbprint, left when he lifted the fish to take a coin from its mouth — hence saint-pierre. It is so thin from the side that a large one yields surprisingly little flesh.

In the kitchen

Ask for it filleted and keep the bones — the head and frame make one of the best fish stocks there is.
