Damson

Prune de Damas

Latin namePrunus domestica subsp. insititia
FamilyFruits
OriginDamascus, Syria
SeasonAugust – October
FlavourSour · Bitter · Fruity · Tangy
Typical price5–10 €/kg

What it is

Named for Damascus, where Roman traders found it, and far too astringent to eat off the tree. Its whole purpose is transformation: the acidity and heavy pectin make a jam and a gin that no sweeter plum can match.

In the kitchen

Do not try to stone them raw. Cook them whole and lift the stones out with a slotted spoon as they float free.
