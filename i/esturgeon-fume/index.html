Smoked sturgeon ◆

Esturgeon fumé

Latin nameAcipenser baerii (smoked)
FamilyFish & seafood
OriginFrench & Italian caviar farms
Season (France)All year
FlavourSmoky · Rich · Nutty · Meaty
Typical price (France)60–120 €/kg

What it is

A caviar farm kills a fish for a kilo or two of roe and is left with twenty kilos of flesh, which is how smoked sturgeon became a product rather than a waste stream. New York’s appetizing counters were selling it long before that — cut as thin as smoked salmon and priced above it.

In the kitchen

Slice it thick, three or four millimetres: the flesh has almost no flake and a thin slice simply falls apart. Horseradish cream and rye rather than lemon, which strips out the nutty fat.
