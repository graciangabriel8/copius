Walnut oil

Huile de noix

Latin nameJuglans regia
FamilyFats
OriginPérigord & Dauphiné
SeasonAll year
Flavournutty · rich · toasty · earthy
Typical price20–35 €/L

What it is

Pressed in stone mills in the Périgord, where the kernels are roasted before crushing — that roasting is the whole flavour. Before it was a condiment it lit lamps and thinned the paints of Renaissance workshops.

In the kitchen

Never heat it. It turns bitter almost immediately, and it goes rancid fast — keep the bottle in the fridge.

Goes with

Walnut Belgian endive Blue cheese Lamb’s lettuce Apple Sherry vinegar Pear Comté
