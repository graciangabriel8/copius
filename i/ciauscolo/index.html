Ciauscolo ✦

ciauscolo

Latin nameSus scrofa (soft salami)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginMacerata, Marche
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Salty · Smoky · Meaty
Typical price (France)20–35 €/kg

What it is

The name is thought to descend from the Latin cibusculum, little food: it was eaten in small pieces between meals to get through winter work in the fields. It has held an IGP since 2009 and the meat is ground two or three times, which is why it spreads instead of slicing.

In the kitchen

Spread it on warm bread rather than crisp toast — the heat should soften the fat without melting it to oil. Straight from the fridge it tears the crumb; give it twenty minutes on the counter.
