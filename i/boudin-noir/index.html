Black pudding

Boudin noir

Latin nameBotellus niger
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginAncient Greece
Season (France)All year
FlavourRich · Earthy · Meaty · Umami
Typical price (France)8–14 €/kg

What it is

Homer describes it in the Odyssey — a stomach filled with fat and blood, roasted on a fire — which makes it one of the oldest recorded prepared foods in Europe. Every region of France has a version, and they argue about apples.

In the kitchen

Cook it gently and never pierce the skin. High heat splits the casing and the whole thing empties into the pan.

Its 12 pairings are in the full version (€4.90 a month or €39 a year).
