Montbéliard sausage

saucisse de Montbéliard

Latin nameSus scrofa (smoked sausage)
FamilyMeat & poultry
OriginFranche-Comté
Season (France)All year
FlavourSmoky · Meaty · Warm · Salty
Typical price (France)14–22 €/kg

What it is

An IGP since 2013, defined down to its shape: at least 25 mm across, slightly curved, and seasoned with cumin — which in Franche-Comté means caraway, the cumin des prés of the meadows. It is smoked in a tuyé, the wide chimney-room of a Comtois farmhouse, but far more lightly than its broad neighbour from Morteau.

In the kitchen

Poach it 25 minutes in water that never boils, starting cold and unpricked; boiling bursts the casing and empties the smoke into the pot.
