Scorzonera

Scorsonère

Latin nameScorzonera hispanica
FamilyVegetables
OriginSouthern Europe
Season (France)October – February
FlavourDelicate · Sweet · Nutty · Earthy
Typical price (France)5–9 €/kg

What it is

Black-skinned, white-fleshed, and named from an old belief that it cured snakebite — scorzone, the viper. It shares a plate and a reputation with salsify, and most French markets sell one under the other's name without anyone minding.

In the kitchen

Peel it under water and drop it straight into acidulated water. The sap oxidises in seconds and stains hands brown for days.
