Duo jiao

Duo jiao

Latin nameCapsicum annuum
FamilyCondiments
OriginHunan
SeasonAll year
Flavourhot · sour · salty · pungent
Typical price10–18 €/kg

What it is

Duo jiao means chopped chilli: fresh red chillies minced with salt and packed into a water-sealed jar, where they sour over weeks with no bean paste, sugar or oil involved. Hunan spoons it straight onto a split fish head and steams the two together, so the liquid the chilli gives up becomes the sauce.

In the kitchen

Steam it, do not fry it: the bright sourness is volatile and a hot wok flattens it into ordinary heat. It carries enough salt to season a whole fish on its own.

Goes with

Chili pepper Garlic Ginger Grouper Spring onion Rapeseed oil Salt Silken tofu (kinugoshi)
