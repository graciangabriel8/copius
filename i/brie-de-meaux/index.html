Brie de Meaux

Latin nameBrie de Meaux AOP
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginSeine-et-Marne, Île-de-France
SeasonAll year
FlavourCreamy · Earthy · Buttery · Mild
Typical price18–28 €/kg

What it is

The curd goes into the mould in layers, lifted by hand with a pelle à brie — a flat perforated shovel — because a pump would shear it. Some twenty-five litres of raw milk make a single wheel a little over thirty-six centimetres across, most of which then leaves again as whey.

In the kitchen

Ripe means the paste bulges but still stands. A chalky white line down the centre means another week at 10 °C, wrapped in paper — never film, which suffocates the rind.
