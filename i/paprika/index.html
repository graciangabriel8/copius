What it is

An American pepper that walked through Ottoman camps into Hungarian kitchens and became a national identity. The scientist who first isolated vitamin C did it from Szeged paprika — and won a Nobel Prize for the national spice.

In the kitchen

Paprika burns and turns bitter in seconds: add it off the heat, into something moist, never into hot dry oil.
