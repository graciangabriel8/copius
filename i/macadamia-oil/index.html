Macadamia oil

Huile de macadamia

Latin nameMacadamia integrifolia
FamilyFats & oils
OriginQueensland, Australia
SeasonAll year
FlavourButtery · Nutty · Creamy
Typical price40–60 €/L

What it is

The macadamia is the only Australian native to have become a global commercial crop, and most of the world's orchards descend from seed carried to Hawaii in the 1880s. Close to 80% of its fat is monounsaturated, which is why it holds a pan where walnut oil would turn bitter.

In the kitchen

It holds to about 200 °C and will take a gentle sear, but treat it as a seasoning: a spoonful over the fish at the end does more than a panful under it.
