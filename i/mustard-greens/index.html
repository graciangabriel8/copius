Mustard greens

Moutarde de Chine

Latin nameBrassica juncea
FamilyVegetables
OriginHimalayan foothills
Season (France)March – May, October – November
FlavourPungent · Peppery · Bitter · Hot
Typical price (France)4–8 €/kg

What it is

The same plant that yields brown mustard seed produces a leaf with the same fire in it — bite one raw and the heat arrives in the nose, not the mouth. The American South braises it for hours with pork; Sichuan salts and ferments it into zha cai.

In the kitchen

Heat destroys the pungency, so decide which you want. Raw in a salad it bites; braised twenty minutes it turns sweet and mild.
