Cantaloupe melon

Melon

Latin nameCucumis melo var. cantalupensis
FamilyFruit
OriginPersia & Armenia
SeasonJune – September
Flavoursweet · musky · floral · fruity
Typical price2–4 €/pièce

What it is

Named for Cantalupo, a papal estate outside Rome where the seed was first grown in Europe. In France Cavaillon claims it so completely that Alexandre Dumas willed the town his complete works in exchange for an annuity of twelve melons a year.

In the kitchen

Judge it by weight and by the scent at the stem end. A heavy melon that smells of nothing was picked far too early.

Goes with

Prosciutto Mint Black pepper Basil Lime Goat cheese Olive oil Honey
