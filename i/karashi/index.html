Karashi (Japanese mustard)

Karashi (moutarde japonaise)

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)40–70 €/kg

What it is

Karashi is brown mustard seed ground fine and nothing else — no vinegar, no wine, no salt — so it makes its own heat only once water lets the enzyme meet its precursor. Kumamoto packs it into the holes of a lotus root and fries the slice, a dish the story goes was devised to feed an ailing lord.

In the kitchen

Mix the powder with water at body temperature, never hot, and let it stand ten minutes — boiling water kills the enzyme and leaves bitterness with no heat. It fades within half an hour, so mix it as you serve.
