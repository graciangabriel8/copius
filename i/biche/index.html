Hind

Biche

Latin nameCervus elaphus
FamilyMeat
OriginForests of Europe
SeasonOctober – January
Flavourmeaty · delicate · earthy · mild
Typical price25–45 €/kg

What it is

Kitchens buy the hind under her own name because of the rut: from mid-September a stag runs himself down, all but stops feeding and empties his muscle of glycogen, and the meat comes out dark, musky and tough. The hind goes through none of that, so her flesh stays pale and fine-grained — which is why she is roasted where stag meat is braised.

In the kitchen

The saddle takes twelve minutes at high heat and not a moment more; a hind carries even less fat than roe deer and there is no marbling to forgive an extra minute. Serve at 52 °C internal, and keep any marinade to a few hours — there is no gaminess here to cover up.
