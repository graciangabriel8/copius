Cider vinegar

Vinaigre de cidre

Latin nameAcetum pomorum
FamilyCondiments & ferments
OriginNormandy & Brittany
Season (France)All year
FlavourSour · Fruity · Tangy · Sweet
Typical price (France)3–7 €/L

What it is

The cloudy strand that forms in an unfiltered bottle is the mother — a living mat of bacteria and cellulose that turns alcohol into acid. It is not spoilage, and it can be lifted out and used to start the next batch.

In the kitchen

It is gentler than wine vinegar, so use a little more. It suits pork, cabbage and anything with apple in it.
