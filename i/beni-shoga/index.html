Beni shoga

Beni shoga

Latin nameZingiber officinale
FamilyCondiments
OriginOsaka and western Japan
SeasonAll year
Flavourpungent · sour · salty
Typical price10–20 €/kg

What it is

It is cured in umezu, the brine that rises in the barrel when ume are salted for umeboshi; the red comes from the shiso steeping in that brine, not from the ginger. Gari, the pale sweet sliced kind, belongs to sushi — beni shōga is the savoury one, and it goes on gyūdon, yakisoba and okonomiyaki instead.

In the kitchen

A pinch is the point — it is aggressively salty and sharp, and a spoonful takes over a bowl. Keep the brine when the jar empties and use it as the acid in a dressing: it is citric rather than acetic, and it tastes of the fruit.
