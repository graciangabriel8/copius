Garrotxa

Latin nameFormatge Garrotxa
FamilyDairy & eggs
OriginCatalonia, Spain
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Earthy · Mild · Milky
Typical price (France)28–40 €/kg

What it is

The cheese had all but vanished when, in the 1980s, city-raised Catalans who had moved to the mountains learned to make it again on a course at the Torre Marimon agricultural school. Its grey suede rind, the pell florida, is wild mould allowed to take the surface, and it is where the damp-cellar note in the paste comes from.

In the kitchen

Slice it thin and serve it at room temperature with quince paste — it is firm and chalky rather than creamy, and a thick wedge eats dry.

Goes with

Hazelnut Quince paste Fig Honey Almond Olive oil
