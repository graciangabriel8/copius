Tobiko

Tobiko (œufs de poisson volant)

Latin nameCheilopogon spp. (roe)
FamilyRoe & caviar
OriginJapan & Southeast Asia
Season (France)All year
FlavourBriny · Salty · Mild · Marine
Typical price (France)45–90 €/kg

What it is

Flying fish spawn onto anything floating, so the roe is taken from rafts of straw and palm frond set out for them rather than from the fish — the egg mats are lifted off and washed clean. The wasabi, yuzu and squid-ink colours are dyes added afterwards; the roe itself is a dull orange.

In the kitchen

Add it at the very end and off the heat: the shell is what you are buying, and it softens within a minute in anything hot. Folded through cold mayonnaise or crème fraîche it keeps its crunch for hours.

Goes with

Rice Nori Cucumber Avocado Wasabi Sesame Soy sauce
