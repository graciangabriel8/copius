Sesame

Sésame

Latin nameSesamum indicum
FamilyNuts & seeds
OriginIndia & Africa
Season (France)All year
FlavourNutty · Toasty · Rich
Typical price (France)5–10 €/kg

What it is

“Open sesame!” — the magic words of Ali Baba echo the plant itself: ripe sesame pods burst open at a touch, scattering their seeds. It is likely the oldest oil crop on Earth, pressed in Mesopotamia four thousand years ago.

In the kitchen

Toasted and ground with salt, it becomes gomasio; toasted and pressed, tahini. Raw seeds on bread are decoration — toasted, they are flavour.
