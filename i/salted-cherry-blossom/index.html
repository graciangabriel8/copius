Salted cherry blossom

Fleurs de cerisier salées

FamilyCondiments & ferments
Season (France)All year
Typical price (France)150–300 €/kg

What it is

About four fifths of Japan’s supply comes from one district of Hadano in Kanagawa, where the double-flowered kanzan is picked half open, salted, then steeped in plum vinegar. The almond and hay smell is made by the curing rather than the flower: salt breaks the glycoside and frees the coumarin, which the living blossom barely gives off.

In the kitchen

Rinse and soak five to ten minutes, then taste the soaking water — it is a light floral brine worth keeping for rice or tea rather than pouring away. Under-soaked, a few blossoms will salt a whole dish on their own.
