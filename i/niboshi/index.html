Niboshi

Niboshi (sardines séchées)

Latin nameEngraulis japonicus (dried)
FamilyCondiments
OriginSeto Inland Sea
SeasonAll year
Flavourumami · briny · bitter · marine
Typical price30–60 €/kg

What it is

Niboshi are boiled and sun-dried within a single day, and west of Osaka the same fish is called iriko instead — one product, two names, split along an old dialect line. Their oils oxidise quickly, so unlike katsuobushi they are kept cold and thrown out at the end of a season rather than aged.

In the kitchen

Pinch off the head and pull out the black gut before brewing, then steep them cold overnight instead of boiling. Anything above a simmer draws out exactly the bitterness the gutting was meant to prevent.

Goes with

Kombu Soy sauce Miso Daikon Soba noodles Hon-mirin
