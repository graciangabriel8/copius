Scallop

Coquille Saint-Jacques

Latin namePecten maximus
FamilyShellfish & molluscs
OriginNorth Atlantic & Channel
Season (France)October – March
FlavourSweet · Delicate · Marine · Buttery
Typical price (France)10–18 €/kg

What it is

Pilgrims to Santiago de Compostela sewed its shell onto their cloaks — proof of the journey, drinking cup and begging bowl in one; French still names it after Saint James. Botticelli floated Venus ashore on one: the only ingredient with its own Renaissance masterpiece.

In the kitchen

Dry them obsessively, then ninety seconds a side in a screaming pan: mahogany crust, translucent heart. Moving them early is the only way to fail.
