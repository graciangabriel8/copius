Feather blade

Paleron

Latin nameBœuf — épaule
FamilyCuts of meat
OriginFrance
Season (France)All year
FlavourMeaty · Rich · Umami · Earthy
Typical price (France)12–18 €/kg

What it is

A shoulder muscle split down the middle by a broad sheet of connective tissue, which is why it looks wrong raw and behaves beautifully cooked — that sheet dissolves to gelatine over three hours and bastes the meat from inside.

In the kitchen

It is the cut for bourguignon and carbonnade. Sold as a steak it needs either two minutes or three hours, nothing between.

Goes with

Red wine vinegar Carrot Onion Bay leaf Thyme Lard Button mushroom Garlic
